## Findings against the requested checks

### 1. Freeze and exposure cutoff — substantially specified, hash protocol needs repair

Sections 1.1–1.4 correctly require both panels' recipes before contractions and both panels' predictions before exposure; preserve Part A → E → F; forbid endpoint-guided reconstruction/retries; list disjoint development IDs/text groups; and require a sealed Part A ledger link. The exposure definition covers people and processes making F8 choices. The operative validity rule, however, mentions only builder sessions. Extend it to the chair, recipe selectors and every other decision-maker; a builder-only attestation cannot discharge the earlier condition. Starting Part A scoring is compatible with blindness only while that wider cutoff remains satisfied. Command-log grep supports an attestation; it does not prove absence of exposure through summaries or other channels. Freezing before score-job start is preferable, but not itself proof if earlier outcomes have been accessed.

The two-commit chain is impossible as written. C1 hashes `exposure.json` and `code_hashes.json` in H1–H6; those files are then supposed to acquire C2 times, log hashes and code information while H1–H6 remain byte-identical. A C1 file cannot know the future C2 commit time. Use immutable C1 files plus separately hashed C2 supplements, including the exposure attestation through C2, and a deterministic final chain definition. Commit metadata should attest actual commit times externally rather than require a file to contain its own future commit identity. Adopt an earlier F8a commit as H0's anchor. Pin code needed for C2 in C1 or specify an outcome-blind, auditable pre-exposure restart when it changes. Preserve the original C1 bytes.

### 2. Combined gate, floor and weighting — accept the reading; floor not independently reproducible from committed bytes

Adopt **one gate across one fixed E+F set**, with six planned source-component slots, at least four qualifying independent components, strict `|a_rep| + q_rep > h*`, and global bf16 survival **≥0.75**. Representatives are chosen by the frozen availability hierarchy, at s=1 versus base; not by field magnitude or later resolution. Missing components do not lower four. E's loop descendants remain one component; E cannot independently supply four. The global gate permits E purchases supported by F, which must be disclosed. It neither measures resolvability nor certifies an effect: a negative a and positive q can cancel.

The proposed floor algorithm is adequately specified: historical edit-minus-predecessor noise, scale 1, 32 paired two-window blocks, common 4,000 bootstrap draws, PCG64 seed 1731840362851662234, linear percentiles, half-width per donor, median within component, then median across components. **I cannot recompute `h* = 0.0049397 nats/token` from the committed artefacts.** `git ls-files` shows no payload `data/runs/a5/nullsize/inputs.json`. The committed provenance supplies donor/component identities, masks/provenance and payload hash `67da223ce315e8256a754d05dd0af725f3480fabc46d3b736114072ef11c53de`; reports supply aggregate counts. Trace rows contain donor representatives, signs, resolution masks/counts and decisions, not the 141×64 centred residual values or bootstrap interval endpoints. The missing numerical input is `centred_noise`, aligned to `donors` and `window_mask`, from that exact payload. I did not substitute local data or recover values from new outcomes. The preview and sensitivity numbers remain the spec author's computations, not this review's reproduction.

Component weighting is defensible for a component-level purchase: 116 chain pairs in 38 components should not outvote 25 LoRA roots just because chains contribute more increments. It is not uniquely compelled by A5, and a median of component medians is not a pooled weighted median. Freeze that exact estimator before contractions, publish pair-weighted and family sensitivities without selecting among them, and describe the floor as a historical noise screen rather than a calibrated resolution probability. The reported pair median 0.0011513 versus component median 0.0049397 illustrates material estimator sensitivity; it does not warrant selecting the smaller number.

The survival rule supplies a usable norm criterion and denominator, but explicitly include excluded rotations in the denominator despite absent rotation bytes, define zero intended norms and N_e=0 as failures, and retain no-op disagreements. Unsupported curvature may be labelled partial and omitted from the magnitude sum; that is not complete quadratic support. A chart abstention must not be confused with a gradient-field abstention. Counting C6 as a separate random direction source is plausible conditional on its fixed matched support/counts, but independence of outcome signs is not established by a distinct RNG. Freeze the dependency graph for shared prompts/directions/generators and the conditional independence rationale. Merge connected sources if required; do not manufacture six independent trials from six labels.

### 3. Prediction schema and edge values — good coverage, inconsistent quadratic scaling

Section 3 provides strong identity, realised-bf16, support/rank, per-tensor field and missingness requirements. Explicit chart abstention is the correct decision: projectors and chart columns are not a fitted predictor; no F8 chart fit or retrospective Part B prediction is allowed. Numerical a=0/q=0 and no-op handling are mostly sound.

However, each row's a and q already contract its own realised D_eff(s). Its quadratic sign must therefore be **sign(a_s + q_s)**, not sign(a_s + s·q_s). The latter applies scale twice to curvature. If an additional multiplier t is being forecast for that realised direction, write `t a_s + t² q_s` and `t*=|a_s|/q_s` only for a_s<0,q_s>0; t is relative to that row. An absolute s* computed from D_eff(1) is a nominal full-strength-direction forecast and need not describe bf16-rounded ladder points. Make this distinction explicit; do not imply exact scaling through bf16 rounding.

Encode missing values, nonfinite contractions and undefined κ/s* with status/reason and JSON-safe nulls or defined strings. A computed infinite κ at a=0,q>0 is an edge value, distinct from a nonfinite field input. Fix whether partial-q forecasts are reported as partial or abstain. Pin full field identities for descriptive MMLU as well as WikiText, and deterministic handling of zero direction norms, zero SVD sign projections and activation pooling/token selection for construction. These cannot be chosen by the future builder after looking at contractions.

### 5. Cluster rule and statistics — representative rule accepted, replacement sentence must change

The fixed s=1 first-order representative rule handles mixed ladder results without retrospective selection; rotations/scales remain entry-level diagnostics. State outcome precedence explicitly: gate/cap failure means unscored for non-no-op representatives, missing/excluded and field abstentions have distinct reasons, and no-ops remain disagreements. Report the frozen six-slot ledger even if fewer components exist or resolve; N_c does not silently shrink when Scope is missing. Specify n=0 as no test, not a scientific conclusion.

The exact test formula is two-sided. Its proposed English replacement is wrong: **0/6 as well as 6/6 rejects p=1/2**, at p=0.03125. Perfect disagreement rejects toward *anti-agreement*, never transfer success. At n≤5 neither tail rejects at 5%; 5/5 gives 0.0625. The stated all-success CP lower bounds (about 0.541 at six and 0.478 at five) are correct. Say “evidence of above-chance agreement requires 6/6; two-sided rejection also occurs at 0/6.” Always report actual k,n,interval and direction. Conditional independence/fair-coin signs and resolution selection are assumptions, not empirically certified by the gate. Without a defensible independent Bernoulli denominator, retain entry/cluster descriptive counts and omit binomial inference. No result satisfies, fails or unresolves A5.5/F5.5 or becomes a G8 condition.

### 8. Pricing — reference rates check out; all-entry purchase conflicts with cap pruning

No borrowing Part A/G5/G7 allocations.
