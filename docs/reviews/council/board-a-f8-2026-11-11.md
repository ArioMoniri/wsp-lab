## Findings

**Registration integrity and decision authority.** The round-4 merge adds only the council documents and an appended F8 block. Independently compared PREREG bytes to the first parent: the entire preceding F7 registration is an unchanged prefix. Executed the read-only pin verifier with bytecode disabled: `scripts/a5_build_manifests.py --verify-pins HEAD` returned **all pins match**. Selection and control-seed files show no changes against freeze `a638efa`; the intervening code-map changes are the separately governed F6/F7 history, not an F8 re-pin. No A5.4 arm, A5.1.2 selection, A5.5 priority, F5.5 predicate, existing seed or existing cap is changed by this merge. F8 adds its own construction allocation; it does not enlarge an old allocation.

F8.6 explicitly prevents either panel from satisfying, failing or making unresolved any A5.5 step or F5.5 predicate, and prevents funding. That is sufficient decision separation. “WikiText decides for the panel” means a descriptive endpoint hierarchy only. An F8 sign hit cannot discharge G3/G4 or authorise G8; a miss is evidence of prediction failure on that fixed endpoint, not unique identification of locality versus K-FAC error. Rane is context, not recalibration; no new correction-spectrum feature or signed-q primary arm is authorised. F8.10 is an option requiring a later registration.

**Outcome blindness and ordering.** Published generation gains do not supply our TF WikiText/MMLU labels. A fixed external selection on other endpoints can therefore be prospectively blind to ours, while remaining externally selected and unrepresentative. G2 auxiliary gradients/Fisher factors are permitted prediction inputs, not F8 outcome observations. F8.5 correctly requires the Part A ledger to be sealed before scoring, with Part A → F8-E → F8-F. However, F8.3 only freezes before F8 scoring: this leaves a gap in which Part A results could influence F8 recipes, thresholds or predictions. Both panels need one pre-exposure freeze, not sequential adaptation.

**Manifest and CPU gate.** F8.3 lists appropriate ingredients but is not yet an executable contract. There is no literal surviving-fraction threshold, complete recipe/seed list, precise cluster-to-sign rule, or identified fitted chart prediction artifact. G2 produces projectors/schema/probe cache, not by itself a fitted chart response predictor (`scripts/g2_fields.py`). Missing coverage must yield an explicit abstention, not an invented prediction or a refit.

F8.4 is materially ambiguous. Applied separately, F8-E cannot currently reach four independent components: Astra, aiXiv and LP are one loop component, and even a released Scope direction adds only another. If the gate is combined across E and F, that must be stated before scoring; construction and gate checks can precede both without changing score order. The null-size directory does not contain a named scalar “floor.” Its report/trace schema stores size, yield, decisions and resolution masks, not the per-donor interval endpoints needed for the proposed median. The payload is identified by SHA256 `67da223ce315e8256a754d05dd0af725f3480fabc46d3b736114072ef11c53de`; it contains old development edit-minus-predecessor noise, not G3 rotation outcomes. A deterministic floor can be derived from that frozen noise without opening new outcomes, but donor weighting, contrast, bootstrap and mask choices are not specified by F8.4. I have not manufactured a floor value.

Moreover, |a|+q is a magnitude screen, not a resolvability guarantee: negative a and positive q can cancel. Fisher q is not a signed Hessian correction. Selection on that screen is prediction-conditioned sampling and must be disclosed as such.

**Clustering and replay.** The loop-family rule is correct and conservative; two tensors, several recipes, rotations and ladder strengths do not create independent sources. A shared base alone neither proves independence nor forces every edit into one component. Source/prompt/direction dependence must be mapped before scoring. Astra's one base-root entry, both dense deltas together, pinned base and unchanged tensor complement, with exclusion on any other difference, is the right exact-replay rule. This review did not download/compare checkpoint tensors and therefore does not certify that rule has passed.

**H5 consistency.** Adding a scripted estimator and Sol medium prospectively is compatible with A5.7/F5.4 if Astra, existing controls and the same-editor primary comparison remain. Neither `docs/H5_DESIGN.md` nor `docs/H5_PROTOCOL.md` contains the round-4 scripted-estimator/Sol/per-stream amendment at this HEAD, despite synthesis section 5 saying those files change. The protocol still identifies Astra as primary; F8 is prospective authority, not completed implementation or a pinned provider configuration. Retain every screened/missing/unscored entry. State that passing the magnitude screen does not certify resolution. 3. **Complete F8.3's prediction contract.** Pin base/tokenizer, realised deltas at each scale and rotation, construction inputs/code/seeds, full G2 file identities, endpoint signs/normalisation, effective-rank/support audits, and a numerical surviving-entry fraction with a defined denominator.
