## Findings

**Authority and timing.** The appended block changes no registered Part A/Part B arm, selection, decision, seed, existing cap or pin. New descriptive panels, access treatments and training permissions do require this amendment; calling them descriptive does not waive prospective freezing. The stated pre-outcome status is an attestation, not independently proved by these documents. F9's opening says everything runs after F8, whereas F9.1 and the synthesis require logging F8 itself. Resolve this by making logging operative before F8 scoring, while F9-P follows F8 and H5 stays in G5. F8a's combined six-slot E+F gate cannot simply be inherited as a gate for an unrelated pruning population: specify the F9 purchase/gating population without manufacturing independent components. Freeze designs before decision-maker exposure, including the chair; later outcome-informed proposals are explicitly exploratory.

**Forecast schema and E₀.** The log is a good extension of F8.3, but lacks explicit endpoint/config/orientation, contrast/reference, source-component and predictor-version fields. Freeze forecasts before scheduling, then append decisions/outcomes separately: a row cannot contain a future decision while proving it preceded that decision. All failures, rejections, abstentions and no-ops remain in the ledger. Separate gradient-anchor and factor-anchor fields; `incumbent-exact` gradient and `base-factor` curvature can coexist.

E₀ is a valid uncentred squared-error skill score against predicting zero **when its inputs and positive denominator are defined**. It can be negative without bound and is not a centred R². At ΣΔy²=0 it is undefined, including 0/0; missing/nonfinite targets and denominator failures in bootstrap draws require explicit status, never epsilon repair or redraw. State row versus component weighting. Cluster resampling does not itself make the point estimate component-weighted. Give finite scored/attempted denominators and unresolved/abstention coverage; resolved-only E₀ would select on outcomes. Constant forecasts make the calibration slope unidentified.

Fix scaling: if a_s and q_s contract the row's realised bf16 D_s, use predictions a_s and a_s+q_s, not s·a_s and s·a_s+s²·q_s. Nominal full-direction a,q support the latter formula only as a labelled nominal approximation; bf16 ladder points need their own contractions. For rotation agreement the target is edit-minus-rotation and the forecast is ⟨g,D−R⟩; edit-minus-base calibration is a different estimand. Define each explicitly. The parenthesis “κ is fixed at 1” wrongly reuses κ=q/|a|: the **linear forecast coefficient**, not the curvature ratio, is fixed to one. q remains a nonnegative Fisher magnitude, not a signed Hessian correction or interval.

For residual r=Δy−prediction, empirical MSE equals mean(r)² plus mean[(r−mean(r))²], under identical weights. That second term is centred residual MSE; it is not automatically a conditional-error estimator. Specify any conditioning strata/estimator and the swap loss before using “conditional MSE.” A component-only bootstrap describes uncertainty conditional on the fixed endpoint items; item-population claims require paired common item-block resampling preserving shared references.

The 30/100 cluster cutoffs are reasonable conservative reporting conventions, not guarantees of calibration or precision. One hundred independent Bernoulli clusters gives roughly ten percentage points of worst-case sign precision; it supplies no bound on E₀ precision under concentrated losses. Freeze independent source/episode grouping and one Bernoulli representative rule for CP. Rotations, scales and 24 related masks do not become independent trials. Report actual interval width/effective concentration; fewer than 30 clusters cannot support a pooled calibration claim.

Online restriction to the three-coefficient ridge readout and abstention quantiles is appropriate only for **future descriptive episodes**, with immutable versions and disjoint connected-source training, swap-validation and evaluation inventories. F8/F9-P zero-fit forecasts and registered Part B fits stay frozen. Reusing a swap-validation set adaptively can overfit it; use prospectively separated validation or a fixed update protocol. No current evaluation/audit/sealed outcome may select recipes, hyperparameters, abstentions or an update and then assess that update on those same outcomes. A fitted scale applied to the “next family” still requires source separation and a family order fixed before outcomes; report it separately from E₀'s zero-fit primary. Version even unsuccessful swaps. These are concrete leakage paths left open by “scored log rows” alone.

**Pruning.** The 10/20/50% ladder is a defensible descriptive extrapolation, not evidence of equal-performance compression. The transplant/native distinction is correct; native contractions must include survivor compensation and cross terms. Eighteen deletion/rotation entries plus three native entries plus 24 ranking masks is 45 **only without extra scale/rotation variants**. Freeze that exact inventory, SparseGPT damping/solve/calibration rules, supported tensor map, count matching, Dirichlet concentration/seed, integer allocation/ties and truncation/missing-factor policy. Global ratios need capacity constraints and exact count repair. No endpoint-guided mask repair or retries.

The 24-mask panel can compare frozen rankings on one base at one sparsity with zero fitted response parameters. Specify Kendall tau-b/ties, response orientation and common paired-window uncertainty. For WikiText loss, a+q and q_A naturally rank predicted damage upward; ⟨D,W⟩=−||D||² has the opposite energy sign, so reverse it for a damage baseline or declare the signed convention. Do not pool sparsities to inflate ranking performance; neither 24 masks nor many mask pairs establish cross-family calibration. q_A is a reconstruction-objective comparator, not the task loss. Unstructured zeros do not establish reduced dense allocation, latency or actual model parameter inventory.

The price arithmetic checks: 45×1.04/60=0.780 GPU-h and 32×1.04/60=0.555 GPU-h. RESULTS round 3 supports 1.040 minutes as a historical warm maximum, not measured high-sparsity/LoRA runtime. Both exclude loading, fresh references, retries and qualification. Construction/training ≤0.5 are proposed ceilings, not measurements or funding. Preserve existing caps with overhead-inclusive allocations, watchdog/stop-and-reprice, no borrowing; the unmeasured SparseGPT solve needs a bounded funded measurement before purchase.

The P5 numbers are copied correctly but **described incorrectly**. On its historical 229-entry/64-item panel, 0.00487 is the **upper 97.5% component-bootstrap bound on the panel-mean half-width**, and 0.01282 the observed maximum per-entry half-width. Neither is a universal resolution floor for new pruning masks, nor the F8a historical-null gate scalar. Carry them as historical diagnostics; compute new paired intervals without adjusting recipes or thresholds from those outcomes.

**H5 localisation and LoRA.** The flag and paired variants are sensible. However, H5_DESIGN/H5_PROTOCOL at this HEAD contain the round-4 additions only, not F9's localisation or training-enabled stream, despite synthesis §5 implying the updates exist. Reconcile them prospectively with F8a's overrides; preserve Astra, cheap/sham/random controls, scripted variants, nested best-of-N and the complete A5.7 contract.

Pin how candidate-independent g⊙W/Dx inputs and candidate-specific g⊙D are ordered: the latter cannot guide a first candidate before its delta exists. Log verbal rankings before field access, keep them out of critic inputs, randomise the shuffled control and preserve matched support/opportunities/cost. Freeze the exact LLM-localised versus field-localised scripted contrast; comparing LLM versus script simultaneously changes editor and localisation. Define gain-per-evaluation, zero-denominator handling, paired interval and multiplicity/meaningful margin; a point improvement alone is insufficient for a localisation-value claim. Aggregate sensitivity is not causal localisation or faithful self-report.

The LoRA stream forecasts **completed adapters' collateral TF change**, not on-query gain or whether training is worthwhile. Explicitly scope the no-gradient harness exception, pin backward/gradient-exposure values, feedback type, optimiser/rank/module/step limits, disjoint query/training/support/calibration/acceptance/sealed groups and forward/backward meters. Charge training in both arms and all setup, re-anchoring, audits and failures. TF surrogate scores cannot supply A5.7's bounded task-score success endpoint.

A base-relative candidate evaluated against a changed incumbent needs the realised increment W_candidate−W_incumbent and an exact incumbent gradient, or a separately labelled difference-of-base-forecasts approximation. Re-anchoring does not turn the submitted base patch into an incumbent increment automatically. Fresh gradient hashes and base-factor flags are required; future per-objective extraction cost is unmeasured. Exact gradient contraction is not an exact finite-edit forecast.

“Missed harm” is **not** A5.7's missed qualifying beneficial proposal guardrail. Define harm endpoint/sign/threshold/denominator separately and retain A5.7 unchanged. The 32 fully scored adapters support shadow diagnostics only. Even with 32 independent rejected trials and zero misses, an illustrative one-sided exact 95% upper bound is 1−0.05^(1/32)=0.08937, above 5%; fewer rejections/dependence worsen this. A5.7 itself leaves an all-zero missed count unresolved under its bootstrap-t. Do not certify safety or realised savings with this stream or tune its gate on the same 32 outcomes. Shadow can show infeasibility; only prospective eligible G8 episodes can establish savings.

**Generation.** Three arms, 8,192 cap, five paired seeds for screening and independent five-seed batches plus replay are sensible design choices, not qualification guarantees. Freeze model/tokenizer/chat/thinking/decode/parser/EOS definitions, per-endpoint margins and A3 repeat/reliability/precision rules before sampling; pair item/seed comparisons and shared base errors. All-item correctness must include truncations and malformed answers; completion is not correctness. These public MATH-500 items cannot be recycled as fresh H5 held-out evidence.

122.88M=500×10×3×8,192 is correct for the **two-batch panel per edited checkpoint**. It excludes the additional replay, pilot, and a separate screen unless the screen is prospectively one qualification batch; further edited checkpoints add generations. Correct “full qualification ceiling,” specify reuse/replay counts, and price the complete workload only after a bounded funded same-device pilot. The descriptive endpoint has no funding/decision authority and remains unavailable as an H5 success endpoint until separately qualified/adopted.

**External spot checks and synthesis numbers.** Current arXiv fetches support two principal F9.8 corrections:

- [PPF v2, §§method/Q3](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.02381v2): structured pruning, JS divergence to the original model, **775 collected samples with 20% testing**. Say “evaluated dataset of 775,” not “trained on 775.” The headline prediction error is not perplexity error for our masks.
- [RLHF edit forecasts v1, abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03244v1): 82% explained standardized change on the factorial experiments, 66% out-of-sample on real upgrades with a coefficient frozen from those experiments. Neither validates unit slope here.

An additional [pruning v3 check, §5 and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.25098v3) supports 10/20% experiments with Wanda/Magnitude and no SparseGPT experiments. Its qualitative gains on other models do not promise our TF gains.

Synthesis **does rely on numerical evidence not independently verified by this review**: OWMI's quoted figures, the OBC-Prune collapse premise and the replication's reported outcomes are not established by the two requested spot checks. Retain primary URL/version/excerpt evidence and verified-reference entries or mark them as attributed/unverified rather than blanket “verified.” No such figure may become a threshold, expected effect or funding premise. Chair choices (30/100, 24 masks, 32 queries, sparsities and caps) must remain explicitly planned criteria, not measurements.

The local timing citations are supportable with limitations: G2 review gives 854.718742 seconds for 20 rows (42.736 seconds mean, different predecessor supports; not five new objectives at that rate); committed power `fields_manifest.json:summary` gives 13305.825513 seconds/141 donors=94.3676 seconds including I/O. These historical averages do not establish a future re-anchoring or factored-LoRA rate. Remove the synthesis/paper statements that “the field is exact” for finite root edits, that all small-model math gains are repairs, or that models universally cannot report interventions. Members explicitly warned against these generalisations; the council is not unanimous on them.
