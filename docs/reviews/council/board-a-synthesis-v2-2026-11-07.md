## Required completions carried forward

### R1 — Make Part A inference executable (blocking; §4.1, lines 133–152)

The Clopper–Pearson arithmetic is correct for the stated two-sided 95% interval. CPU evaluation of `Beta.ppf(.025, k, n-k+1)` gives:

| Resolved n | First k with lower bound > .5 | Lower bound | Lower bound at k−1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | 12 | 0.5191088662 | 0.4489967590 |
| 40 | 27 | 0.5087051245 | 0.4831555464 |

These are mathematical thresholds, not evidence that 40 edits yield 15 resolutions or that binomial assumptions hold. One pair per source component repairs one source of dependence; all pairs still share scored windows and the fitted field. Specify inference conditional on that frozen field and fixed item panel, or calibrate the complete sign/resolution statistic with common item draws for an item-population claim. Explicitly define what fails when the development null-size simulation is unacceptable. Reporting a constant-sign comparator does not establish that the field beats it; restrict the claim to sign agreement above .5 unless that comparison is separately registered.

Line 15 defines half-width as half the interval width. Thus `|d_i| > h_pair,i` at line 143 is not necessarily equivalent to a zero-excluding interval for an asymmetric bootstrap CI. Freeze a zero-excluding paired interval rule, or justify/calibrate this exact half-width rule. Pin interval construction, draws, seed, analysis time, treatment of missing D/R scores, zero measured contrasts and audit exclusions. Retain all 40 in the ledger; distinguish the resolved-pair inferential denominator from full-panel failures. Define the ladder contrast (e.g. scaled edit minus its actual predecessor), prediction sign and separate strength denominators. Paired resampling and a resolution-aware simulation are good requirements, but their unspecified implementations cannot yet certify a CP test.

### R2 — Finish the effective-increment rotation contract (blocking; §4.1, lines 154–162; G3, line 118)

Take the SVD of the **realised** increment `bf16(W+Δ)−W`. Line 156's option to obtain it “from the raw LoRA factors” is invalid unless an explicitly verified calculation reproduces that effective matrix: rounding can change its spectrum and rank. For R, measure `bf16(W+R)−W`, not just R. Define whether the 2% “Frobenius mismatch” compares norms, and register post-rounding spectrum/rank mismatch criteria, per-tensor and aggregate norm checks, block versus exact-coordinate support reporting, and a nuisance-controlled sensitivity. Explain that the rotated object is not an SFT-trained edit.

Freeze seed derivation and cross-layer sharing/independence, tensor coverage, treatment of unrotated tensors in the scored model, norm-tertile allocation and ties, finite exclusion/failure policy and the resulting count rule. Logging coordinate changes without a matched sensitivity does not establish an orientation-only causal contrast. Accept the corrected scaled-edit construction at line 162.

## New required edits arising in v2

### N1 — Pilot does not report the claimed matrix-error tolerance (blocking; G0, line 115)

V2 says “LoRA steps within 1e-3 relative Frobenius”. The JSON **does exist on the cited branch**, checked read-only at `feat/d8-chain-replay` commit `ae7548a7fd4a17f8ad800c8375c4b4a94fff6556`. It confirms 11 steps, 8 bitwise. However its `compare` records give `fro_regenerated`, `fro_stored`, maximum coordinate error and fraction differing; they do not give `||D_replayed−D_stored||F` or its relative error. The relative differences of the two norms for the three LoRA steps are approximately .00003872, .00060656 and .00090676. Those are **not** relative Frobenius errors of the matrices. The reverse triangle inequality only makes norm difference a lower bound on difference norm; nearly equal norms can hide large directional disagreement.

Correct the claim to the metric actually measured, or supply auditable difference-norm evidence before asserting the 1e-3 result or accepting against the proposed 5e-3 matrix-error tolerance. Cite a pinned report revision. Retain retrained/descendant labels and exclusion from historical fingerprint claims. This review does not access stored tensors to generate the missing metric.

### N3 — Ladder lower-bound rule is not evidence of wrong signs (blocking decision logic; §4.1, lines 150–152; §7, lines 246–247)

V2 stops field purchases when the s=.25 **lower** agreement bound is <.5. With 15 resolved pairs, 11/15 correct gives a lower bound .4489967590 and would trigger the stop despite a majority of correct signs; with 40, even 26/40 does so. This may be a deliberately conservative funding rule, but it is not evidence of incorrect signs. A lower bound below .5 means failure to establish agreement above chance. If the intended rule is demonstrated below-chance agreement, it needs an **upper** bound below .5. State which intention applies and how it relates to Part A go/unresolved, before outcomes. Do not treat uninformative resolution as a negative mechanism result. Likewise, the risk claim that a small-strength pass and large-strength fail mean “the structure is right and the edits too large” (line 246) is an interpretation to investigate, not a conclusion licensed by this test.
