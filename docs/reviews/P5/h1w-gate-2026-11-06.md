## 6. What fresh WikiText scoring would cost

No GPU was used here. Board A's derivation (not a measurement): about 0.4 min per delta x 281 unique fresh deltas (255 plus the six spares, as in the sealed partition) = **about 1.9 GPU-h**, plus the engineering of a sealed WikiText window panel disjoint from the 64 development windows and the A3 bitwise re-check (about 0.2 h).
