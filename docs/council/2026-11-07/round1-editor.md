## Evidence and what the negatives actually test

The fetched source is [Sparks of Weight-Space Programming](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/), specifically Experiment 7, “Which language model is sycophantic?”, and Experiment 8, “Make a language model better at math”. Experiment 7 reports four unsuccessful weights-only attempts; attention patterns on self-written inputs then identified the sycophant in all three trained pairs. Access was attention-only, not output inspection; the setup allows up to 16 texts and 20 runs. Experiment 8 reports subtraction of a direction from 64 MLP output columns in each of layers 15 and 19: 131,072 weights, reported as 0.016%. At 50 passes, its table gives MATH-500 7.9→48.1%, GSM8K 13.8→67.1%, IFEval 21.0→62.3%, and MATH token-limit hits 93.0→33.1%. These are blog results, not ours. Its pass can answer up to 512 problems or read 1,024 texts; our sequence-based meter is not equivalent. The additional Gemma experiment reports no established math improvement. None demonstrates held-out prediction from a delta alone. These exact distinctions also constrain the extraction in `docs/H5_RESEARCH.md` and protocol in `docs/H5_PROTOCOL.md`.

Classification of the brief's seven results:

- **1: conditional structure evidence.** Trees outperform the tested neural representations on the recovered protocol; that defeats those implementations' superiority, not all structural information. Descriptor proximity indicates a weak incremental target. Resolve attribution with paired within-family/norm residual losses on independently qualified labels, rather than another pooled ranking (`docs/reviews/P4/d3v2r-summary-2026-11-06.md`).
- **2: structure plus calibration/data.** Unseen-family performance is poor, but offsets can destroy pooled ranking. The separating measurement is within-held-out-family residual error and calibration, with whole source closure excluded from training (`docs/RESULTS.md`, P4; recovered summary above).
- **3: data/identifiability failure.** Missing complete trajectories and mismatched history comparisons cannot reject dynamics. Recovered bytes must yield complete evaluated closures and independent-run power; a row count alone cannot do this (`docs/HYPOTHESIS.md`, H2).
- **4: two different answers.** Unqualified MMLU gold log-prob leaves H1a untested; H1w's near-zero incremental estimate concerns this representation and WikiText endpoint, with failed power gate, not a universal impossibility. The separating measurement is a qualified, powered paired residual-loss contrast on editor proposals (`docs/HYPOTHESIS.md`, dated amendment and H1w).
- **5: measurement/data.** Sampling variability and many behaviorally null edits attenuate useful contrasts. Bit-identical repeats establish execution reproducibility, not finite-item resolution. Measure paired item uncertainty relative to between-proposal effects (`docs/RESULTS.md`).
- **6: weak structure clue.** Feature importance identifies associations, not semantic mechanisms. Destroy base-neuron correspondence while preserving support/dose and test incremental loss (`docs/council/2026-11-07/BRIEF.md`, result 6; `docs/council/2026-11-05/round2-weight-reading.md`).
- **7: task/access mismatch.** Blog diagnosis and repair with feedback do not test our stronger delta-only forecasting question (fetched blog, Experiments 7–8).

## What an editor proposal is

The hypothesis is that useful editor proposals concentrate changes on a diagnosed circuit: sparse support, selected layers/columns, signed directional removal, and an explanation connecting the observed failure to the operation. This is a proposed distribution, not an observed property of a production `llm_edit` dataset. LoRA, task-vector, random and abliteration labels in the sampled zoo are insufficient substitutes for editor-specific calibration (`docs/H5_DESIGN.md`; `docs/H5_PROTOCOL.md`). An editor can also submit diffuse or ineffective changes; never filter these away after seeing outcomes.

The harness distinguishes submitted tensors from `Δ_eff = bf16(W+Δ)−W`; the critic must consume the latter and record rounding loss. `src/wsp/editor/api.py` supplies bounded reads/probes and delta collection; `driver.py` coordinates proposals; `episode.py` records attempts; `zoo.py` registers valid deltas and provenance. These are the implementation boundary, not evidence of predictive efficacy. Independent base-relative proposals are an episode/search history, not a weight trajectory (`docs/H5_DESIGN.md`). Keep invalid/time-out attempts in cost denominators and retain unselected proposals. Production qualification remains separate from merged harness approval (`docs/reviews/H5/board-a-editor-final-2026-11-04.md`; `docs/H5_PROTOCOL.md`).

## Primary structure and two alternatives

**Primary: local intervention response plus calibrated utility head.** Cache module inputs and residuals on a fixed benchmark-disjoint panel: proposed 16 texts ×128 tokens, following the earlier council's concrete cache design. For down-projection edits compute `ΔW_l x_l,t` using post-gating MLP activations, retain signed direction, support, exact depth, column identity, base-relative alignment, and effective rounding. Summarize direct residual/logit effects and feed a small set encoder or tree head with interaction terms. Use dense supported slices rather than silently truncating a sparse edit to rank eight. A direct logit lens is not the downstream Jacobian; attention and recurrent mixer edits need their own module response or an out-of-domain abstention (`docs/council/2026-11-05/round1-weight-reading.md`).

Predict a distribution over task gain, loop/limit-hit change and collateral degradation, then the probability a candidate reaches a frozen target while satisfying guardrails. Model uncertainty and novelty must cause evaluation, not confident rejection. This can beat pooled StatNN only if matching the changed writer to the active input identifies functionally consequential edits that norm/support cannot distinguish. It is activation-assisted forecasting, not literal weights-only H1. Initially restrict the mechanism test to MLP writers; broader edits remain in accounting but take fallback evaluation.

**Alternative A: code-conditioned operator critic.** Parse frozen executed code/API events into operation, tensor support, scale, projection direction and dependency tokens; pair these with effective-delta and base-relative contractions. This may share statistical strength between differently worded implementations of the same operation. Compare with strong structured descriptors, not merely a weak hashed-text baseline. Code is untrusted input, never executed by the predictor. An operation that did not survive bf16 rounding cannot acquire efficacy from its description.

**Alternative B: small edited-model probe-response critic.** On an identical disjoint fixed panel, measure before/after logits or hidden summaries and learn the mapping to task effects. This captures nonlinear downstream consequences missed by the primary approximation, but pays candidate inference cost. Include probe-only and probe-plus-delta baselines: if probe-only wins, the result is cheap behavioral screening, not weight readability. No measured probe latency supports a speed claim (`docs/H5_DESIGN.md`).

Rationale is excluded from the primary model. Compare frozen, pre-evaluation rationale versus no rationale, within-operation shuffled rationale, and rationale alone on held-out goals/editor identities. Strip outcome text, evaluation identifiers and later narrative. A rationale gain surviving those controls is useful semantic context, not proof that the explanation is faithful. Preserve the transcript for audit; hashes/shape summaries alone are not numerical activation features (`docs/H5_DESIGN.md`, implementation addendum). Proposed cache access therefore requires an explicit new treatment specification.

## Smallest discriminating experiment and price

All counts and thresholds below are **prospective design choices**, not measurements or authorization to execute.

Use the recovered 297-row set for baseline development only, maintaining source/ancestry closure (`docs/reviews/P4/d3v2r-summary-2026-11-06.md`). The planned 358 regenerated rows do not supply new editor proposals or establish H2 power. For H2 separately, audit complete length≥4 closures, true/masked/shuffled history and length-one equivalence before adopting the existing registered test (`docs/HYPOTHESIS.md`).

New panel: 20 independent goal/seed episodes, each with four frozen editor candidates and two support/sparsity/effective-norm matched random controls: **120 candidates**. Allocate 10 episodes to development/calibration and 10 untouched episodes to a preliminary test. Hold out complete episode, shared generated directions and prompt-template groups; report editor-identity holdout separately if more than one identity is acquired. Same pinned Qwen base only: no cross-base claim. Freeze all proposals before revealing candidate evaluations; no selection by measured effect size.

Labels: proposed TF-v1 panel of 1,000 MMLU items, 250 HellaSwag items and 64×1,024-token WikiText windows, retaining per-item outputs and same-device base references (`docs/RESULTS.md`, TF-v1). MMLU requires new proposal-distribution qualification; existing H1a failure cannot be bypassed. These are mechanism/sensitivity labels, not validated proxies for loop repair. Separately use the H5 pilot panel, MATH-500 all 500, GSM8K 200 and IFEval 100 at 4096 tokens with limit-hit reporting, only after qualification and timing (`docs/H5_PROTOCOL.md`). A 32k reproduction needs its own measured budget.

Mandatory identical-fold baselines: StatNN, augmented StatNN with the new scalar features, structured descriptor-only, delta-norm, probe-activation-only, and shuffled writer/input correspondence. Primary screening criterion: ≥5% residual-MAE improvement over both trees, positive paired episode/item 95% interval, and degradation under correspondence destruction. This extends the earlier council's mechanism test instead of repeating a pooled-family contest (`docs/council/2026-11-05/round2-weight-reading.md`).

Ten test episodes cannot be declared powered in advance. Estimate paired episode-loss variance on development; simulate the registered hierarchical procedure at the 5% gain and require ≥80% power before confirmation. If inadequate, freeze the model and prospectively size a fresh episode panel; do not reuse test outcomes for tuning. A qualified powered upper bound below 5% kills this practical mechanism claim; wide intervals or failed label resolution mean unresolved, not falsified.

Measured planning anchors are 7.5 min fast-tier evaluation, approximately 1 min TF-v1, and 54 s feature extraction (`docs/council/2026-11-07/BRIEF.md`; TF timing also `docs/RESULTS.md`). Thus 120 TF labels cost approximately **2 GPU-h**; 120 fast-tier labels **15 GPU-h**, before bases/repeats. Existing-style extraction costs **1.8 processing-hours**, not automatically GPU-hours. Cache creation, custom contractions, proposal generation and diagnostic probes are unmeasured additions. The larger 4096/32k panels cannot honestly be priced at 7.5 min: total is `2 + 120*t4096/60 + cache/probe GPU-hours + reference/repeat costs`, with t measured in minutes. Time a base and candidate before expansion; stop if proposed savings cannot exceed these costs.

## H5 decision and the order-statistic control

A critic must preserve the chance of finding a qualifying improvement while avoiding expensive evaluation of losers. Average correlation cannot establish this. Replay the same frozen archives using critic ranking, random ranking, same-editor chronological evaluation and development-score best-of-N; use identical N, initial incumbent, stopping rule, guardrails and final sealed selection. Match random/cheap-editor archives by total real evaluations and separately by GPU-seconds. Include failed attempts and search over prompts; do not compare a selected archive maximum with one baseline draw. This operationalizes the Meta^n warning in `docs/H5_DESIGN.md`, without asserting an independently verified result from that paper.

Shadow scoring evaluates every candidate and therefore saves nothing in the experiment. Its replay estimates a future policy. Only a subsequent prospective trial can establish H5: ≥30% fewer real evaluations, final-score non-inferiority within one percentage point, calibrated rejection audits, and positive total savings after probes, editor costs and amortized critic training. Pair episodes and jointly resample episodes/items; repeated samples of the same item are not independent items. Size this trial from pilot variance, not the nominal candidate count (`docs/HYPOTHESIS.md`; `docs/reviews/H5/board-b-requirements-2026-11-04.md`). Retain Adam/Muon at equal forward budget for the full H5 claim; the archive test alone addresses only editor selection. Preserve all protocol/sandbox gates and distinguish restricted pilot success from faithful blog reproduction (`docs/H5_PROTOCOL.md`; `docs/reviews/H5/`).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cached-activation intervention critic | Signed supported delta × actual module input | Independent episodes; ≥5% residual gain over both trees; correspondence ablation | 2 GPU-h TF labels for proposed 120; cache/probes extra; task tier timed separately | Qualified powered upper bound <5%, or no structural specificity |
| Code-conditioned operator critic | Share evidence across equivalent edit programs | Same panel; beat structured descriptors and code/rationale shuffle | Reuse labels; CPU parsing/training and editor costs measured | No incremental held-out operator benefit at adequate power |
