\section{Experiments}
The subject is Qwen3.5-0.8B with thinking enabled. Fixed subsets cover GSM8K, MATH-500, IFEval, MMLU-Pro and HellaSwag. We also measure WikiText perplexity, refusal on harmful prompts, over-refusal on harmless prompts, and KL to the base. The cap-hit share is the fraction of responses pooled across GSM8K, MATH-500 and IFEval that reach the token limit without EOS (1,024 tokens at the fast tier, 4,096 at the cheap tier). It is a truncation proxy, not a validated detector of semantic repetition. Refusal keyword matching is a surface measure. It does not establish harmfulness.

The fast tier uses 50 GSM8K, 25 MATH-500, 25 IFEval, 100 MMLU-Pro and 100 HellaSwag items, plus 50 prompts each for refusal, over-refusal and KL. Generation is capped at 1024 tokens. At these sizes, a single binomial score has a standard error of 0.03 to 0.10 (Section~\ref{sec:reliability}). GSM8K, MATH-500 and IFEval are therefore reported as descriptive at this tier. MMLU-Pro, HellaSwag, WikiText perplexity and KL are candidate predictor targets, but none is qualified until the criteria in Section~\ref{sec:reliability} are met. Measuring their floors is the first of those steps.

The cheap tier uses the larger fixed battery with a 4096-token limit, except for MMLU-Pro, which has a 1024-token limit. A faithful 32768-token tier is reserved for the base and at most 10 selected entries. The cheap tier has been run only on the base model (Table~\ref{tab:base-repro}), and the zoo evaluation configs record a 1,024-token cap. A timing measurement and coverage beyond the D3 sample have not been run. D3 drew production entries without replacement, using seed 0 and sorted IDs, with quotas of 60 random direction, 100 abliteration, 60 LoRA SFT, 30 task vector and 50 chain steps. Where a family fell short, D3 would take every available entry. On the manifest snapshot in Table~\ref{tab:zoo-counts}, these quotas resolve to 300 entries, and all 300 were evaluated. Sampling settings and fixed item IDs are recorded in the evaluation configuration.

Table~\ref{tab:base-repro} places the completed cheap-tier base run beside the base values that the blog reports \citep{takehi2026wsp}. The blog's budgets, sample counts and loop definition differ, so these figures are not matched estimates. The 32k rerun was interrupted when the GPU ran out of memory, and the IFEval difference still needs checker reconciliation.

\subsection{Predictor suite under protocol v2}
\label{sec:v2}
We ran the predictor suite under protocol v2, with three seeds per run. Protocol v2 adds nested model selection, train-only preprocessing and paired bootstrap intervals over ancestry clusters. The matched set has 345 rows from 389 evaluated entries; the other 44 have no matched predecessor target and are excluded. The run files mark 260 targets as having inferred runtime metadata, backfilled from job logs. The 345 rows are neither the 300-entry D3 subset nor the 260 matched D3 edits counted in Section~\ref{sec:reliability}. Twenty-five run directories are complete. The directory \texttt{d3v2-m3-family} has a configuration and a split plan but no metrics, so we do not report it.

M1 is the stateless tensor-token predictor and M2 is the state-space sequence predictor. M1-matched and M2-matched are matched-input controls. M3 and M6 use JEPA-style objectives, and M5 is a causal trajectory transformer.

Table~\ref{tab:v2-suite} lists the 25 complete rows. Each cell is the mean Spearman over three seeds, with the minimum and maximum over seeds in brackets. Those brackets reflect training and split variation, not confidence intervals. Both targets are fast-tier accuracies scored by answer-option likelihood: MMLU-Pro by the unnormalized log-probability of the answer letter, and HellaSwag by each ending's log-likelihood divided by its character count. Neither is qualified under Section~\ref{sec:reliability}.

In this exploratory three-seed analysis, the trajectory-split M1-matched row reached mean Spearman .45 and .46 on MMLU-Pro and HellaSwag, against .57 and .65 for seed-0 StatNN. Seed ranges describe training and split variation, not confidence intervals. The labels carry inferred historical runtime, and target qualification is incomplete.

The original H1 criterion was not demonstrated; H2 and H3 remain unresolved, and the suite does not test H4--H6.

Table~\ref{tab:v2-baselines} gives the three baselines. All three come from seed 0 and are not three-seed means. Within a split, the baseline values are identical across models, so each split cites one source run. Section~\ref{sec:exploits} looks at what the descriptor baseline picks up.

On seed 0 of the trajectory split, every learned row has a larger MAE than StatNN on both targets. For M1-matched, the paired difference is +0.016 [0.012, 0.021] on MMLU-Pro and +0.011 [0.009, 0.014] on HellaSwag, where a positive value means the learned row has the larger error. Across the seven learned rows, all 14 intervals lie above zero. These paired cluster intervals condition on the fitted models, and they do not cover item sampling, training variation or data provenance.

The five \texttt{m2-holdout} rows are one-fold family-purge runs. Each holds out one edit family and its descendants from training. They are neither extra seeds nor M3 transfer runs. Their test sets hold 98, 98, 59, 60 and 30 rows, drawn from 98, 45, 59, 60 and 27 clusters. Ancestry and support limits apply to every holdout.

The suite scores eight targets, but it has no KL or WikiText heads. The dashes in the source summary mark missing outputs, not negative results. M6 fails its all-seed collapse gate on all three standard splits. That gate requires an effective rank of at least 2 and at least twice the rank of the frozen predictor, and the failure does not prove that every fold has rank below 2. M3 has no acceptance gate, so it is not reported as passing, and no M6 LoRA acceptance run exists.

H2 is undetermined. On the trajectory split, M5 reaches 0.41 and 0.26, against 0.45 and 0.46 for M1-matched. The suite does not establish the registered 15\% MAE gain on adequately powered trajectories of length four or more. H3 is undetermined: no qualifying held-out LoRA result exists, and the M2 LoRA holdout is not that test.
