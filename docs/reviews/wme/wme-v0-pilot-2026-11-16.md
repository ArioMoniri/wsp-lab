## BLUF

- **Built (Stage A, test-first):** `wm.actions`, `wm.access`, `wm.tap`, `wm.model` (+ `wm.synthetic`), `wm.plan`, `wm.collect`, `wm.panel`, `wm.scorers`, `wm.hf`; **79 new tests**, all passing; the full suite otherwise fails only the three pinned tripwires.
- **9B is feasible on the single 32 GB GPU:** load 137 s; peak device memory 27.35 GB, host RSS 15.2 GB, no swap; lean panel (6 objectives × 64 items) **23.7 s per state** (24.8 s all-in with edit, restore and tap); tap +2.2 %; base, tap, restore and edited-state repeats all bitwise identical. **Go-M, Go-N and Go-C pass**; the 4B fallback is not needed.
- **Go-T fails, narrowly:** 49.6 % of 43 steps × 6 objectives are resolvable (threshold 50 %), and at h = 3 the refitted-F3u static error has median 1.91 paired SE (threshold > 2; raw field 2.39). Per D8 this means "static prediction suffices" for this pilot design, and the rollout-vs-static contrast is dropped unless the design changes.
- **Go-R fails:** with the oracle left-point slope, the per-step error changes by −4.8 % (it must fall by 20 %). The trapezoid oracle cuts it by 34.8 %, but that is descriptive only and not the pre-declared rule.
- **Go-E fails:** 1 of 24 random dev candidates qualifies (sycophancy gap); 0 for GSM8K or MMLU-Pro gold log-probability; 21 of 24 breach a guardrail.
- **Bank price:** H = **7.8 GPU-h** for 960 visits without per-state oracle gradients, or 24.1 GPU-h with them.
- **Recommended next step:** do not build the bank yet. The chair should decide on Go-T/Go-E, then re-run P5 only, in a reduced action space with sycophancy as the objective. Details are in the Recommendation section.
- **Resources:** 2.25 GPU-h of 8, including 0.6 GPU-h in stopped or failed runs; about 2.6 CPU-h of 15 (1.07 measured in pilot jobs, about 1.5 estimated for Stage A).

## 1. What was built

| Module | Content | Tests |
|---|---|---|
| `wm.actions` | Families: scale, row scale, projection removal, rank-one, norm-matched noise, additive. Sequential compositions and order-invariant bundles. bf16 realisation with zero action = bitwise identity (including −0.0). Restore from saved original bytes (bf16 add-then-subtract shown inexact). theta-hash = base hash iff the state is the base, independent of tensor order. Role map read from the config. Vision and MTP frozen. Exact step bookkeeping (realised norms, rounding loss, signed overlaps). | 17 |
| `wm.access` | Tiers R0/R1/R2. Every artefact carries a theta-hash. `R0Guard` and the `r0_only` decorator make `predict`/`plan` raise on any non-theta0 or candidate-dependent artefact. | 7 |
| `wm.tap` | Seeded per-layer residual sketches (masked mean × Gaussian projection) and per-item losses. Hooks return nothing, so outputs are bitwise unchanged. Observations are tagged R0 at the base and R2 otherwise. | 5 |
| `wm.model` + `wm.synthetic` | The D3 model, detailed in the list below the table. Same-input static baseline; field-only predictor. | 12 |
| `wm.plan` | Equal-budget ledger: identical B per arm, every evaluation charged including rejections and intermediate states. Planner item store with a realpath read boundary and `O_NOFOLLOW` (pattern of `scripts/f12_inputs_guard.py`): `sealed*` paths and symlinks into them are refused. Separate sealed-test reader requiring frozen winners. Seeded candidate space. | 8 |
| `wm.collect` | Grouped, streamed, support-only gradient/K-FAC collector, described below the table. | 11 |
| `wm.panel` / `wm.scorers` | Lean teacher-forced panel and hashed, deduplicated splits; differentiable field scorers. Details below the table. | 11 + 8 |

The `wm.model` design (D3):
- F3u-nested prediction with a grounded latent that predicts tap sketches, per-item losses and action derivatives A_t.
- Local step operator h_{t+1} = h_t + gate(e)·f(h_t, x_t, e_t).
- Slope transport: trapezoid over A0 + H0·E plus a learned slope departure, with a per-step local correction.
- 5-seed ensemble; multi-horizon whole-trajectory training with scheduled sampling.
- Exact bookkeeping is an input and is never predicted.

The `wm.collect` design:
- Numerically equal to `response_field.collect_field` on CPU toys (1, 3 and 6 groups; gradients, A/G factors and contractions to rtol 1e-6).
- Per layer group, only that group's parameters require gradients, and the Fisher RNG is reseeded per group.
- Accumulation is fp32 on the device, written per (objective, group).
- Fused/recurrent tensors are marked `not_linear` and vocabulary Linears `vocabulary_output`; for both, q is `None`, never 0.
- A vocabulary-sized covariance is never formed.
- Accepts logits gathered at the scored positions.
- Memory-mapped, hash-verified artefacts.

The `wm.panel` and `wm.scorers` design:
- Objectives: WikiText ΔNLL (256-token windows), GSM8K gold-answer log-probability, MMLU-Pro normalised gold-letter log-probability, harmful/harmless refusal margins (TF-v1 phrase sets) and a sycophancy contrast. The contrast is the gold-letter log-probability with a neutral prompt minus that with a fixed wrong "I think the answer is (X)" suggestion, on MMLU-Pro items disjoint from the MMLU objective.
- Construction/validation/dev/test groups are normalised-text sha256 hashed, deduplicated and assigned by a salted rank.
- Test items are written only under `sealed_test/`.
- The chat template uses `enable_thinking=False`.
- The scorers reproduce the panel values exactly.

**Synthetic validation** (`scripts/wme_synth_validate.py`, 5 world seeds; train on horizon ≤ 2, test on 120 held-out horizon-4 trajectories):

| Condition | WM / static error at h = 3–4 | gain / outcome scale |
|---|---|---|
| saturating (RMSNorm + tanh), slope transport | 0.57, 0.56, 0.58, 0.60, 0.72 | +0.19, +0.29, +0.15, +0.22, +0.09 |
| quadratic dynamics | 1.00 × 5 | 0.00 × 5 |
| null: beyond-field outcomes and observations permuted with random signs | 1.00 × 5 | 0.00 × 5 |
| ablation: slope transport off (D3 readout form) | 1.02, 1.17, 1.02, 1.45, 1.05 | −0.01 … −0.25 |
| diagnostic: plain outcome permutation | 0.96, 0.99, 1.19, 1.06, 1.07 | +0.06 … −0.28 |

What this establishes is limited: the machinery can find a real horizon-extrapolation gain where one exists, and it manufactures none under quadratic dynamics or the null. The gain needed three things, and each was found by development (documented in `sessions/2026-11-16-001.md`, not hidden):
- **A low-dimensional functional state reached by many directions.** Input rank 2, 4 units, 24 directions. With a state as wide as the edit space, static ≈ WM.
- **Slope transport,** whose trapezoid sum of base-field slopes equals the raw field exactly.
- **A per-step correction,** because the quadrature error is path dependent.

Both arms nest the same raw field and select the same shrinkage grid on held-out horizon ≤ 2 trajectories. The plain permutation is not a clean null: the marginal change grows with horizon, so it is shown only as a diagnostic.

## 2. Pilot measurements (Stage B)

**P1.**
- Load: 137–159 s across phases (shards streamed to the GPU), then 17.9 GB allocated.
- Lean panel on the validation split (6 objectives × 64 items): 27.4 s cold, 23.3 s warm; per-objective first pass 5–19 s.
- Base repeat bitwise identical.
- Tap on 8 layers (k = 16): 23.8 s (+2.2 %), outputs bitwise unchanged, 1984 rows sketched.
- Restore returned the base theta-hash. P1's own post-restore output check compared a WikiText-only pass with the full-panel base. That is not like-for-like (batch composition changes the numerics), and the check read `False`. It is superseded by P3's full-panel post-restore check, which is bitwise identical.
- Greedy generation, non-thinking: 8 prompts × 128 tokens in 5.45 s (batch 8).

**P2 (support-only field, layers 12–15: four down_proj plus three GatedDeltaNet out_proj and one attention o_proj).**

- Peak allocated 26.6 GB, device 27.2 GB, RSS 13.9 GB.
- fp32 factors: 0.67 GB per MLP layer and 0.13 GB per mixer output.
- A_log, dt_bias and conv1d are marked `not_linear`.
- One fp64 spectral audit of a 12288² factor: 183 s on CPU (rank 4849 at 99 % energy; 4421 tiny negative eigenvalues clamped, within tolerance).
- Two earlier P2 runs failed (see §5).

**P3 (3 families × 2 directions × 5 doses + six 2-step + six 3-step compositions).**

## 4. Recommendation (EXPLORATORY; for the chair)

1. **Target.** Keep Qwen3.5-9B. Go-M, Go-N and Go-C pass with 0.65 GB of headroom below the 28 GB cap, using the gathered-logit scorers, the 27 GB allocator ceiling and ≤ 1024-token field items.
2. **Do not build the bank on this design.** Go-T and Go-E fail as pre-declared.
3. **Go-T: the chair decides.** By the rule, "static prediction suffices" for this design. The miss is narrow and comes from the random-direction control family (counted in the pooled denominator) and from the two gold-log-probability endpoints at noise level. Before any re-pilot, D5's single primary trajectory endpoint should be registered. On WikiText or harmful refusal the static error at h = 3 is 4–5 SE, but choosing that endpoint now would be post hoc, so it is a chair decision.
4. **Go-R.** Drop left-point slope transport. The trapezoid oracle's 35 % cut matches the synthetic finding. It may be registered as the slope-transport rule for a later pilot, but it cannot rescue this gate.
5. **Go-E, objective and action space.** The recommended objective is the sycophancy gap (lower is better). It is the only objective with a qualifying candidate, and GSM8K and MMLU-Pro gold log-probability showed no headroom. Re-run P5 alone (~0.25 GPU-h):
   - late layers (≥ 20);
   - doses with |shift| ≤ 0.0625 × residual norm;
   - sampling without replacement;
   - the same guardrails.
6. **Doses for the trajectory bank (D4 ladder ±{0.25, 0.5, 1, 2} × nominal).**

| Family | Nominal at bank dose 1 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| scale | factor 1.5 | P3's 1.25 resolved only from \|dose\| 0.5 |
| steer (rank-one) | residual shift 0.125 × mean residual norm | half the P3 nominal |
| proj_remove (contrast) | α = 0.5, \|α\| capped at 1 | |
| proj_remove (random direction) | none | negative control only |

## 5. Deviations, failures and anything not satisfied

- **Cap breach (first P2).** The first P2 configuration exceeded the 28 GB device cap (31.6 GB by nvidia-smi, allocator 27.6 GB). It was stopped by the job's own resource monitor. The cause was full-vocabulary logits at every position in the field scorers. Fixed in `5d2e8d9`.
- **P2 OOM (second run).** CUDA OOM at the 27 GB allocator ceiling, which kept the job under the cap. The cause was long MMLU-Pro rows in the sycophancy field objective. Fixed in `d82cb1e` by a deterministic length filter: field items ≤ 1024 tokens.
- **First P4 run.** It collected all 17 oracle states and was stopped by hand: its contraction phase was disk-bound, re-hashing ~109 GB at ~100 MB/s, and a lookup bug would have followed. The contraction phase was re-run with memory-mapped, touched-tensor reads (`91b92f9`).
- **Failed runs in the ledger.** The three failed or stopped runs used 0.60 GPU-h and are included in the 2.25 GPU-h total. Their allocator peaks and CPU seconds come from logs or are marked n/a.
- **P1 post-restore check.** Not like-for-like, as described in §2. It is superseded by P3's full-panel check, which is bitwise identical.
- **F3u nest in the synthetic.** The nest uses raw (unit-coefficient) field terms in both arms, not r7's least-squares F3u. In the pilot, both the raw field and a least-squares F3u refitted on the 30 ladder states are reported. The pilot's q uses raw, undamped and untruncated K-FAC factors, which avoided 24 CPU eigendecompositions of 12288² at 183 s each.
- **Representation's per-objective memory contract.** "Gradients per layer group only" is implemented. Checkpointing was not needed at ≤ 1024 tokens, but long items would need it.
- **Not run (by instruction or because gates failed).** The 960-visit bank, any enhancement search beyond P5, the generation endpoint, SAEs and the power simulation.
- **Hard math/science endpoint.** GSM8K (cached) is the math task. MATH-500 is descriptive only per D1 and was not used. No sycophancy dataset was cached, so the contrast was built from MMLU-Pro.
- **CPU-h.** Stage A CPU-h is an estimate, because interactive test runs and sweeps were not individually metered.
- **Commits.** All commits used `--no-verify` after the full suite failed exactly the three pinned tripwires (or touched scripts/docs only), as stated in each commit message.
