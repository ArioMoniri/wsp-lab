## Required proposal edits

### E1 — Make §§4.0–4.2 one actual decision function

The numbered headings in §4.0 do not remove contradictions inside the predicates:

- Rotation go and the ladder alternative can both hold. The ladder is descriptive yet has a binding alternative rule; say explicitly that its stopping rule overrides rotation go, or choose another prospective priority. Do not let a field-arm sealed purchase proceed on an alternative disposition. The probe-response arm may be development work only unless a new prospective amendment gives it a purchase criterion; it is currently secondary and has none.
- A rotation agreement interval entirely below 0.5, with sufficient yield but without the ladder alternative, is neither the stated go nor the stated interval-includes-0.5 unresolved case. Assign it a disposition explicitly. The conservative option is unresolved/no sealed purchase, with the observed disagreement reported; do not invent a scientific kill.
- §4.2 Screen includes every C ≥0.05 that is not go, while Unresolved explicitly includes controls not beaten with C ≥0.15 and Part A unresolved. Those conditions can coincide. Decide whether those cases fund an extension or remain unresolved, then make the branches exclusive. A recommended order is validity, ladder alternative, insufficient Part A evidence, control eligibility, then ordered numerical funding branches; specify any intentional exception for extension-only funding.
- Screen and No-go can overlap when a bootstrap-t interval does not contain its point estimate. Use explicit ordered `if / else if / else` evaluation, not assumed interval geometry. Clarify whether Part B may be reported when Part A is unresolved, separately from whether it can fund anything.
- “Boundary equality counts as not meeting the threshold” in §4.0 conflicts with C ≥0.15, C ≥0.05, the ≥15 floor and the ladder upper bound ≤0.6. Remove that blanket sentence and state equality separately for each rule. Retaining the displayed inclusive point thresholds and strict positive lower bounds is coherent, but must be the single authoritative convention.

The local Part A go criterion is now intelligible: sufficient resolved independent components plus a CP lower bound above 0.5, conditional on the field/panel and successful validity checks. The upper-bound ladder criterion is also intelligible as a funding rule against usefulness above 0.6. Their priority is what remains unresolved; neither should be described as proving wrong signs merely by failing to pass.

### E2 — Correct what the primary contrast identifies

The formula is correct for a relative residual-MAE reduction:

`C = 1 − A_cand / A_aug`.

The shared nuisance fit, held-out row-summed loss, seed averaging and augmented denominator are appropriate commitments. But §4.2 explicitly gives augmented StatNN **every scalar the candidate receives**, including field and chart columns. A positive C therefore demonstrates improved predictive use/representation of the same supplied information by the candidate pipeline. It cannot identify the incremental information contributed by the field. Matching folds, settings and tuning budgets cannot change that fact. If the two pipelines were literally identical, even that representation contrast would disappear; A5 must identify their actual difference.

Retain C if desired, but relabel its claim as performance relative to a same-information comparator. To claim a field-information gain, additionally register a matched field-present versus field-absent contrast that holds architecture, chart/predecessor access, rows, folds and tuning opportunity fixed. Register its decision role and intervals before outcomes. Random-field and layer-permutation controls test particular destruction hypotheses; they do not replace the intact no-field ablation. Do not switch the denominator or add a success requirement after observing C. This is a scientific-design choice the proposal must settle, not a scalar-schema detail A5 can silently fill in.

### E3 — Reconcile G2 audit failure with §4.0

G2 says fewer than 16/20 passing predecessors disables the base approximation for chains; §4.0 says a failed G2 audit makes the gate unresolved and nothing downstream runs. State one action. A valid conservative choice is a hard stop pending a new amendment. Alternatively, pre-register exact-predecessor replacement, coverage and cost before exposure, and define audit failure as disqualifying only the approximation rather than the whole gate. A5 cannot inherit both directives unchanged.

### E4 — Limit the G8 mechanism conclusion

§4.4's powered upper-bound rule can reject a specified incremental predictive benefit for the registered structural representation and contrasts on that population. It cannot “kill the structural mechanism” generally from one pipeline's failure. Replace that phrase with the bounded claim (and keep deployment kill separate). A5 may fill the exact powered contrast, but the breadth of the scientific conclusion should already be correct in the proposal.
