# Features council: representation diagnosis

## Evidence and diagnosis

The measured P4 summary says StatNN reaches trajectory-split Spearman 0.57 / 0.65 on MMLU-Pro / HellaSwag; M1-matched is 0.45 / 0.46, M2 0.35 / 0.32, M3 0.15 / 0.11, while held-out-family correlations are all weak (StatNN −0.02 / 0.11). These figures and the preregistered failure are in `docs/council/2026-11-05/BRIEF.md` and the D3 board review. StatNN is a LightGBM regressor on the 1,098 pooled φ_stats coordinates, despite its historical name; it is 300 trees, 8 leaves, min child 5 (`src/wsp/predictor/baselines.py`). The saved P4 artifacts are metrics/predictions, not fitted LightGBM objects, and do not expose gain importances. I did not claim specific top coordinates: reconstructing them requires refitting on frozen folds and labels, which this review did not do. Thus claims that it uses specific norms/sparsity remain unverified. The available feature definition shows likely candidates: Frobenius and relative Frobenius norms, sparsity, mean/std/max, and role/depth membership; each coordinate averages tensors, so those signals are scale and edit support by role.

The neural failures are consistent with a small supervised set (300 evaluated edits) and an input bottleneck: the neural models consume pooled vectors (M1/M2), or cumulative pooled vectors (M3); they cannot recover directions or tensor identities already averaged away. Family holdout collapse and the family-centered audit mean apparent trajectory fit is not evidence of cross-family behavior. P4 review further flags label qualification/provenance and incomplete delta-norm baseline; do not interpret reported correlations as a clean capability ceiling.

The admissible targets are repeatable fast-tier likelihood/teacher-forced endpoints: MMLU-Pro, HellaSwag, KL and perplexity; stochastic generation metrics are excluded by Board A. They may be predictable within familiar edit mechanisms, but evidence does not show pooled stats predict beyond “edit magnitude and support location”: no coordinate importance reconstruction, family-only variance decomposition, or controlled norm/support ablation is persisted in P4. Family-only variance is a mandatory next diagnostic, not a number to invent. KL/PPL may primarily respond to global perturbation size; benchmark likelihood may require alignment with task-relevant residual directions. The low held-out-family scores suggest no robust mechanism transfer.

## Feature v4 information-loss audit

`stats.py` extracts 18 values per tensor: Frobenius, relative norm, cosine(base,new), mean/std/max, positive/nonzero fractions, top eight singular values, spectral entropy and stable rank. `pool.py` averages per tensor into 17 roles × four depth buckets plus global stats; pooled spectra are only mean top-eight singular values by role. The stored per-tensor `features.safetensors` includes truncated SVD directional tokens, but predictor data loads only pooled stats and spectra (`src/wsp/predictor/data.py`). The feature manifest confirms 1,235 entries, 24 layers, four buckets, 1,098 stats + 136 spectrum dimensions (the brief’s 1,378 refers to the stored predictor concatenation as configured; coordinate accounting must be reconciled before importance labels).

| Edit family | Facts lost by v4 pooling | Likely target relevance |
|---|---|---|
| random_dir | exact residual direction, per-layer/tensor identity, sign pattern; singular vectors survive only in unconsumed row tokens | MMLU-Pro/HellaSwag could depend on alignment with task directions; KL/PPL may depend more on norm and affected layers |
| abliteration | which refusal direction was removed and its orientation; writer identity, layer-local projection, sign; pooling hides whether attention or MLP writer carries it | KL/PPL and benchmark shifts may depend on functional alignment; refusal labels are not admissible here |
| heretic_ara | same directional information plus optimization trajectory and per-trial localized edits | KL and PPL plausibly reflect broad distribution damage; benchmark likelihood can be selective |
| lora_sft | canonical per-position direction/rank tokens are saved, but pooled predictor discards them, rank ordering, module/layer identity and cross-position relations | benchmark likelihood likely needs alignment and composition; KL/PPL may correlate with magnitude |
| task_vector | source-task direction and layer-specific signs/coordinates; mean pooling erases cancellation and ancestry structure | task benchmark likelihood should be direction-sensitive; KL/PPL less so |
| chain | stepwise direction/order and local cumulative changes; cumulative pooling collapses history | all targets can depend on path; current evidence gives no H2 benefit and only one qualifying long labeled trajectory |

Across all families: residual-coordinate identity, relation to embedding/unembedding, row/column alignment and signed cancellations are absent from pooled inputs. Global dimensions do not preserve whether a direction raises or lowers a particular logit. Four coarse positional bins collapse within-bin layer location. Norms/sparsity are retained only as averages, losing tensor counts and heterogeneity. Rank is reduced to top singular values (and then averaged); exact rank beyond eight and singular-vector subspaces are discarded. SVD sign is intrinsically paired-sign ambiguous, and current sign convention on stored tokens does not repair loss from pooling.

## Ranked feature proposals

Costs below are incremental extraction estimates per entry, CPU-oriented; baseline extraction is 66 s/entry. Measure before scaling. Use frozen base-only bases/probes and never fit them on labels/test edits.

1. **Unpooled per-layer role tokens with positional encoding.** Keep each changed tensor row, its role, exact layer index, shape, norm/spectrum and existing projected singular vectors; add layer index/depth and mask. Output ~Nchanged × (1+18+8×129) floats if retaining current tokens, variable length; practical storage ~same as current per-tensor files. Cost ~66 s/entry (already computed; pooling is negligible). Leakage: architecture/base identity and edit-family fingerprints; split by ancestry and report family-only controls. Prediction: improves within-family MMLU-Pro/HellaSwag over pooled input, especially random/task-vector, but not held-family without alignment. **First add.**
2. **Fixed-base directional projections.** For each matrix delta, emit signed coefficients and energy on unembedding/embedding directions, top base singular vectors, and refusal/loop directions computed once from base-only calibration data; retain role/layer. Dimension e.g. 32+32+8+8 = 80 per tensor plus norms, with explicit bases. Cost: one-time basis construction; per entry O(sum d_out*d_in*k) matmul, likely seconds to tens of seconds and potentially exceeds 66 s if many dense tensors; benchmark. Leakage: calibration prompts or benchmark-derived directions can directly leak targets; use disjoint generic text and publish provenance. Prediction: direction coefficients outperform norm-only for benchmark likelihood and distinguish ablation variants at matched norm.
3. **Delta activations on fixed probes.** Cache base hidden states h at selected layers for a public, disjoint probe set, then compute ΔW·h (and where needed output contractions); per layer/module summarize mean, variance, signed coordinates and logit effect. Dimension: e.g. 32 probes × selected 24 layers × 4 roles × 2 moments = 6,144. Cost estimate at 32 probes: one dense delta-hidden multiply per changed tensor, 32× forward-sized activation projections; likely ~0.5–2× base forward cost, estimate 30–120 s/entry on current GPU, measure. Leakage: probe overlap with eval prompts/answers; strict prompt hash exclusion and base-only cache. Prediction: improves KL/PPL and benchmark likelihood over weight-only scalar stats at matched norm.
4. **W2T-style canonical tokens for dense deltas.** Truncated SVD per tensor (rank 8 or adaptive energy threshold), separate left/right projectors, singular-value modulation, rank and position tokens. Current code already computes truncated SVD tokens, but predictor discards them; W2T’s full LoRA method uses QR then SVD and rank-wise direction/singular-value tokens, followed by rank-level and position-level Transformers. Cost current 66 s/entry includes SVD; do not recompute. Leakage: fixed projection seed/base shape can identify base; keep split controls. Prediction: canonical tokens beat averaged spectra for LoRA first; dense deltas may lose useful low-energy tail at rank 8.
5. **Graph metanetwork.** Nodes are changed tensors with role/layer/shape and directional features; edges encode adjacent layers, residual stream, attention/MLP paths and shared input/output spaces. ~N nodes, O(N) edges; negligible extraction beyond node features, higher training burden. Leakage: graph topology largely encodes architecture/family; compare with topology-only baseline. Prediction: gains only with substantially more labeled edits and multiple base architectures.

W2T verification: the arXiv HTML for [W2T, arXiv:2603.15990](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.15990) says equivalent LoRA factors share a canonical form via “QR decomposition followed by SVD” (abstract, lines 83–85); it defines each component by “an output direction, an input direction, and a singular value” (lines 97–99), then rank-level and position-level Transformers (157–183). Its LoRA QR construction avoids materializing ΔW, cost O((d_out+d_in)r²+r³) (152). This directly supports canonical LoRA tokens, not the claim that rank-8 SVD tokens are adequate for arbitrary dense deltas. Our `canonical_lora_tokens` implements QR/core-SVD, but its fixed random projections and simple concatenation are not W2T’s learned projectors/modulation/Transformer.

## Minimum data regime

At n=300 total, per-family counts are much smaller than the total and multiple families have few independent ancestry clusters. A neural encoder should not be expected to beat a low-capacity tree on supervised training alone. Planning estimate: require at least 300–500 independent labeled edits per family for a modest role/layer encoder to have a plausible comparison, and 1,000+ per family for a directional token encoder; these are design thresholds, not measured power results. Compare learning curves at 50/100/200/300/500 per family with ancestry-grouped nested splits and paired cluster bootstrap. Self-supervised pretraining on 1,235 unlabeled edits may help learn base-specific geometry but does not create target supervision or independent families; expect modest gains only if pretraining is masked reconstruction/contrastive over symmetry-aware tokens and downstream frozen/low-rank adaptation beats supervised-only at equal label count. It cannot fix leakage, target reliability, or family support.
