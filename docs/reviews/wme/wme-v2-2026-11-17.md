**WM-CP** (in `wm.wmcp`) has these parts:
- **Shared representation R(E).** Per objective it holds a_k (degree 1) and q_k (degree 2), the chart and coordinate
  columns (degree 1) and a norm column, so that R(tE) = R(E)·t^degree.
- **9B R(E).** On 9B, R(E) comes from field contractions of the realised cumulative delta (`r_from_contractions`).
  Unsupported q enters as 0 and is flagged.
- **Canonical-path model.** The residual step operator is integrated over K equal sub-steps of R(E_T), with
  exact-zero masking.
- **Frozen K rule.** K = 0 if R = 0; otherwise K = min(16, max(1, ⌈‖E_T‖/s0⌉)), where s0 is the median training
  h = 1 norm, computed within each fit.
- **Shrinkage λ** is chosen on a component split from {0, .25, .5, .75, 1}; ties go to the smaller value.
- **The R-space static family:**
  - S1, F3u(a, q);
  - S2, StaticR;
  - S3, StaticR-aux, sized to WM-CP's parameter count ±10%.
- **WM-E v0 as a descriptive arm on R** (`v0_field`, `v0_trajectory`, `v0_trajectory_r`).

**The A1 required tests all pass:**
- an exact-zero edit gives exactly 0;
- alternative decompositions give identical predictions;
- 2- and 4-step round trips give exactly 0;
- WM-CP equals the static map where the dynamics are quadratic.

## 4. A6: full-refit validation and ruling §4(b)

**Pipeline per replicate** (`wm.powersim_refit_v2.replicate`; this is v1's world, bank, allocation and labels):
- **Alternative: γ = 3, not re-tuned.** It is the decomposition-dependent path effect of v1. Under the null, the
  residuals are shuffled beyond the field.
- **Model fits:** 5 outer folds. WM-CP is fitted on training h ≤ 2. The static member (S1/S2/S3) is chosen on 4
  inner component folds at h = 2 (0.5% relative tie → lower capacity); S3 is sized to WM-CP.
- **Test:** the held-out h = 3–4 errors enter the component-level sign-flip test (10,000 flips, one-sided 0.025).
- **C2-I gate,** per fold:
  - I1 round trips (realised E_T = 0, which must predict exactly 0);
  - the I2 analogue: the embedding-chart column, which is seen non-zero in training through row_scale roots, as on
    9B. It is set with a = q = 0, so E_T ≠ 0 for the representation and the field is zero.
- **C2-S and C2-D** are asserted by construction: a held-out ladder re-expressed as one step must give the same
  WM-CP prediction.

**Runs:**
- **Selected cell** (16, 6, 12): on the Lambda A10 (28 workers).
- **Next larger cell** (20, 6, 12): locally (6 workers, ≤ 1 GB RSS each).

**Futility rule.** Both runs were stopped under a deterministic rule, which is disclosed here. Passing at the
planned replicates needs k_req rejections under the alternative: 497 of 600, or 54 of 60. A run stops once its
alternative non-rejections exceed planned − k_req (103, or 6). From that point, passing is arithmetically
impossible even if every remaining replicate rejects. The null-arm figures below are therefore descriptive, at the
replicates completed.

| Cell (r_f, n_blk, n_cal) | Replicates (null + alt) | Alternative rejections | **Achieved Ĉ_T (alternative)**: mean, [10%, 50%, 90%] | Mean statistic (alternative) | Null rate | Null coverage at 0 | C2-I pass | Decomposition gap | Static chosen (alternative) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **(16, 6, 12) selected** | 110 + 110 | **0 / 110** | **−0.260** [−0.435, −0.248, −0.069] | −2.03 (< 0 in 99%) | 4 / 110 = 0.036 | 0.964 | 1.00 | 0 (exact) | S2 489, S3 61 (fold choices) |
| (20, 6, 12) next | 30 + 30 | 0 / 30 | −0.289 [−0.527, −0.248, −0.149] | −2.32 (< 0 in 100%) | 1 / 30 | 0.967 | 1.00 | 0 (exact) | S2 144, S3 6 |

18.2 CPU-h on Lambda (max worker RSS 0.44 GB) and 3.8 CPU-h locally.

**Reading:**
- **A1 fixed what it was meant to fix.**
  - C2-I passes in every replicate (v1: 0/401 under the alternative).
  - C2-S and C2-D hold exactly: the decomposition gap is 0.
  - Exact zeros hold.
- **But WM-CP is worse than the matched static map in essentially every alternative replicate.** The achieved
  C_T is −0.26 against the nominal +0.15.
  - The v1 alternative's signal is a path effect: two decompositions of the same E_T differ in outcome.
  - A predictor that is a function of R(E_T) cannot represent such an effect, and that includes WM-CP and every
    static member. The alternative therefore gives WM-CP nothing to win on.
  - What is left is extrapolation from h ≤ 2 to h = 3–4. There, integrating a step operator learned on short
    paths extrapolates worse than the direct static map (S2 is chosen in about 90% of folds).
- **Under the null,** WM-CP and the static member coincide exactly in 63% of replicates. Shrinkage picks λ = 0 for
  both, so both reduce to the field and every d_c = 0. The test is then conservative (p = 1). Null size
  (0.036) and coverage (0.964) are within one replicate of the tolerances at these replicate counts; they are
  descriptive, because power decides the cell.

**Ruling §4(b).** The selected cell fails, and so does the next larger cell. **H-T registers "unresolved — not
powered".** The achieved C_T is reported as instructed (−0.260 at the selected cell, −0.289 at the next). The
alternative was not re-tuned. A powered H-T would need a registered alternative under which a path-integrated
local field carries information that a direct map on R(E_T) does not. That is a design question for the chair
(§7), not something to tune here.

## 7. Flags for the chair (registration questions; nothing was changed)

1. **H-T's registered alternative cannot favour WM-CP.**
   - Under A1, WM-CP is a function of R(E_T), so v1's decomposition-dependent alternative (γ = 3) is invisible to
     it by construction.
   - The refit therefore measures only extrapolation, where integration loses (Ĉ_T −0.26).
   - Powering H-T would need a dated amendment: an alternative in which the response field's local structure (the
     a and q growth along the path) predicts h = 3–4 better than a direct map fitted at h ≤ 2. Possibly it would
     also need a WM-CP config or K rule chosen on construction data.
   - Nothing was re-tuned here.
2. **§8.5 tie-break between equal-size claim sets.** ht+e1 (50.8) and ht+e2 (58.9) are both feasible two-claim
   sets. The script took the cheaper one, and §8.5 does not say which should win. After H-T fails, neither E1+E2
   nor any set containing both is feasible within the caps, so E2 stays unresolved either way.
3. **The design after §4(b).** Should the pre-refit ht+e1 design be kept (H-T descriptive on a 16-root-per-family
   bank, 50.8 GPU-h-eq), or should §8.5 be re-applied to {e1} (r_f 8, 40.1 GPU-h-eq)?
4. **Generation cost drives the confirmatory budget.** The sampled-decoding audit at batch 8 costs 8.3 s per
   answer (P-H100). At n_gen,syc = 500, generation is 23.2 of the 28.0 confirmatory GPU-h-eq (4.9 without it). A larger generation batch would
   lower the cost of E1 at higher N, but it needs a P-H100 re-measurement.
5. **TriviaQA wrong answers** come from a frozen within-type rule (§2), so they are less plausible than
   sycophancy-eval's curated ones. The E1 alternative's effect size came from the pilot template on curated items.
