# Board A: protocol v2 and council synthesis

This is an audit of saved outputs and implementation, not an independent rerun or a full reproduction of the council analyses.

## A. Protocol-v2 suite

The 25 run directories contain three seed-level metrics files each. Top-level `seed_summary` is the mean and min/max of finite per-seed point estimates, **not a confidence interval**; `n_seeds` can be below three. In particular, do not interpret NaN for random-direction HellaSwag as zero. Seed-0 baselines in the summary are not three-seed means. In all 75 seed files the `per_family`, `family_centered`, `family_centered_pooled`, and `paired_differences` objects are nonempty; this presence check does not establish every individual cell is estimable or correct. Paired cluster intervals condition on fitted models and do not cover item sampling, training variation, or data provenance. Within-fold centred and pooled-centred correlations are distinct diagnostics; centring with test labels is not a deployable prediction.

The five `m2-holdout-*` rows are separate, explicitly named one-fold family-purge runs, not five additional seeds or M3 transfer runs; for example LoRA records `split=family`, `holdout=lora_sft`, `n_splits=1`. The labels are meaningful descriptive holdouts, subject to ancestry and support limitations. M3 saves pretraining effective ranks but has no M6-style acceptance gate (`train.py:253,277,470`); do not report it as passing collapse qualification. M6's all-seed collapse gate is false for all three standard splits: the implemented gate requires effective rank ≥2 and ≥2 times the frozen-predictor rank (`audit.py:18–30`). This is failure of the acceptance gate, not necessarily proof every fold has rank <2. No M6 LoRA acceptance run is included. The suite has eight rate targets, not KL or WikiText heads; dashes in the table are missing outputs, not negative results. Historical inferred runtime remains a provenance limitation. The audit found no reason to discard the whole suite, but these limitations prevent qualification or a transfer success claim.

- **H1:** not demonstrated; qualification remains incomplete. On trajectory splits M1-matched mean ρ is .45/.46, M2 .35/.32, versus seed-0 StatNN .57/.65 for MMLU-Pro/HellaSwag. Norm and descriptor controls now exist. This agrees directionally with the earlier verdict, not a controlled improvement comparison: protocol and data changed. HellaSwag accuracy has weak effect resolution; neither target is certified by repeatability alone.
- **H2:** undetermined; M5 trajectory .41/.26 versus M1-matched .45/.46 does not establish the registered ≥15% MAE gain on adequately powered length≥4 independent trajectories. Current cross-architecture comparisons do not isolate history.
- **H3:** undetermined; M3 now has completed seed/trajectory results, but no qualifying held-out LoRA JEPA result or demonstrated collapse gate. M2 LoRA holdout is not that test. M6 fails its collapse gate.
- **H4–H6:** untested here: no matched LLM-reader comparison, critic efficiency experiment, or held-out base-model experiment. These results cannot falsify those hypotheses.

Paper-safe wording: “In an exploratory three-seed protocol-v2 analysis of matched-reference fast-tier accuracy, trajectory-split M1-matched had mean Spearman .45 and .46 on MMLU-Pro and HellaSwag; seed-0 StatNN had .57 and .65, respectively. Seed ranges describe training/split variation, not confidence intervals. Labels have inferred historical runtime and target qualification is incomplete.” “The original H1 criterion was not demonstrated; H2 and H3 remain unresolved, and the suite does not test H4–H6.” Report row and independent-component counts, exclusions, seed ranges, paired conditional intervals and all relevant negative controls alongside these sentences.

## B. Council synthesis

### Evidence and scope

Five numerical checks against recorded files: MMLU descriptor ρ .523855 and StatNN .565906 match `evidence-statnn-importances.md:13–14`; residual θ .1462 over 345 rows/278 clusters matches `round2_residual_estimand_output.txt:2–8`; HellaSwag θ −.0342 matches that file:42–43; 94 non-null entries/86 roots matches `round2_metrology_output.txt:21`; H0/H1/H2 timing means 8.63/9.92/23.66 minutes match that output:37–44. The synthesis appropriately labels the residual result exploratory, projected reliability unmeasured, descriptor importance noncausal, and KL a sensitivity outcome. Caveat: its opening table calls StatNN .57/.65 three-seed values whereas the v2 summary explicitly labels baselines seed 0; identify the precise source and aggregation before claiming a three-seed baseline.

### H1a estimand and inference

The proposed ratio is a sensible *conditional predictive loss contrast*, not an identified causal contribution of weights or a universal within-family law. It is preregistrable only after fixing nuisance design, support, prediction aggregation, denominator and inference. Inner OOF residuals for training and an outer-training nuisance fit for held-out residuals avoid direct target leakage if all model selection and preprocessing stay inside training components; ensure inner OOF splits also close ancestry and shared sources. Cross-fitted residual training and full-refit test residuals have different nuisance-fit distributions: audit and report this mismatch. Unseen family/device levels and zero norms need a frozen fail-closed policy, not outcome-driven imputation. Device is a nuisance in scoring and stratification, not an inference-time weight feature; family/device confounding and held-out support can defeat identification.

Proposed registration: “On the sealed, source-closed fresh LoRA/task-vector/chain components, predict predecessor-relative 1,000-item MMLU-Pro normalized gold-letter log-prob using a fixed D3-trained pipeline; subtract the training-only additive family, device and log effective-delta-norm nuisance prediction from each target, and estimate θ = 1 − sum of absolute StatNN residual errors divided by sum of absolute train-fitted isotonic-norm residual errors, row weighted, with shared references preserved.” Fix zero-norm transform, categorical coding, isotonic direction, hyperparameters, training data, item IDs, three-seed prediction aggregation and missing-data rules before unsealing; no model or seed selection on the sealed panel. Reject a zero or near-zero denominator as undefined by a frozen numerical tolerance. Supply flexible family×norm and equal-component-weight sensitivity analyses, without changing the primary.

Use 4,000 paired source/ancestry-component percentile bootstrap replicates of the complete row-summed loss ratio, resampling all rows and paired predictions together; report valid replicate count, denominator failures, and cluster-size distribution. This conditional CI does not cover model fitting or item population: separately bootstrap independent items/blocks jointly across edits and references, and assess seed variation; prespecify a two-way component/item sensitivity or joint resampling if the confirmatory claim generalizes to items. One primary endpoint and one primary contrast at two-sided 5% require no multiplicity adjustment for that single claim; label all margins, HellaSwag, subgroup and alternative-model analyses secondary and use Holm correction within any family promoted to confirmatory claims. Do not choose the stratum or model by its sealed outcome. Pass only θ≥.15, lower 95% bound >0, precision half-width ≤θ/2, ≥100 genuinely fresh components, qualified measurement and prospectively demonstrated ≥80% power at .15. Kill practical readability for this specified endpoint only if the upper 95% bound <.05 on the qualified adequately powered panel; otherwise a wide or intermediate interval is inconclusive. The accuracy-derived 79 is not TF power.

### Deterministic qualification amendment

**Conditional approval as a prospective replacement for rank/ICC thresholds, not a retroactive pass.** Freeze endpoint, effect scale, intended smallest meaningful effect δ, panel and confidence procedure before new outcomes. Require matched measured identity and independent repeat executions; maximum same-device repeat discrepancy ≤1e-4 nats for MMLU/log-PPL or ≤1e-5 for KL, with failures investigated; cross-device SD of paired centred differences ≤0.2 times between-entry SD for MMLU and ≤0.1 for KL/WikiText, otherwise restrict claims to the qualified device. Require a lower 95% component-bootstrap bound on signal reliability ≥.90 and an upper 95% bound on relevant paired item/reference uncertainty and repeat-error 95th percentile each ≤δ/2, on a prespecified variable, representative effect panel. Report bias, tails, ties, host×edit interaction and sampling uncertainty; constant/undefined panels fail qualification. Precision must be on predecessor-relative effects, with item pairing and shared references retained. If the small panel cannot support these bounds, expand it or leave unqualified. Keep the existing stochastic endpoint rule. Projected TF reliability is not a measurement; thresholds are proposed criteria, not results.

### Sequence, budget and omissions

Pilot → bridge → qualified fresh components → sealed residual test → directional screen → conditional history feasibility is a defensible prioritization, not a demonstrated powered programme. Establish scorer and panel qualification, source closure and a TF-specific paired-loss simulation before spending on confirmation; ≥100 is a floor, not proof of 80% power. Freeze directional features before any sealed test; reuse of sealed labels for development invalidates later confirmation. H2 needs complete measured ancestry, independent lineages, a matched mask/shuffle and length-one placebo, plus a new variance-based power calculation; 40 closures are feasibility only. Placebo non-significance does not prove equivalence: predeclare an equivalence margin and require its CI inside that margin.

The 552×1–2 minutes = 9.2–18.4 h estimate is arithmetic based on an **unmeasured** TF assumption; 552×9.92/60 = 91.26 h illustrates the measured H1 fast-tier anchor, not TF timing. 230×66 s = 4.22 h uses measured extraction; 230×33 s = 2.11 h is an unmeasured directional cap. The 19 h scoring allocation leaves at most 0.6 h at two minutes per run for all overhead, so pilot cold starts, references, retries and closure counts must fit the cap or scope must stop. Reconcile the open rental before any new commitment; local caps are not a billed-cost forecast.

Explicitly quarantine inferred-runtime backfills until measured provenance is rebuilt, and account for device×edit effects. Enumerate and disposition the 12 flagged ARA entries with IDs, failure reasons and exclusions; do not assume a corrected rebuild repairs historical labels. Freeze the prospective cohort and source hashes before a corrected ARA rebuild lands; treat that rebuild as a separately versioned future cohort, with independent qualification and no midstream inclusion or outcome-driven substitutions. Chain closure must include all actual predecessors and source-connected components, including unlabeled ancestors, and record missing references and deduplication; nominal roots are not necessarily independent. These are requirements to verify, not assertions that the current artefacts satisfy them.

### Blocking changes for programme of record

1. Resolve baseline aggregation and publish complete per-cell audit and collapse limitations; distinguish qualified endpoints from descriptive accuracy.
2. Freeze the full H1a pipeline, sealed component/item partition, seed aggregation, denominator policy, multiplicity and joint uncertainty; demonstrate TF-specific power.
3. Amend the deterministic qualification prospectively with the explicit effect-resolution and uncertainty gates above, and validate them on measured matched-reference data.
4. Certify provenance, device support, ARA quarantine and full source/chain closure; reconcile budget and show pilot-derived timing and overhead fit before scaling. Specify placebo equivalence and avoid reusing sealed outcomes for feature development.
