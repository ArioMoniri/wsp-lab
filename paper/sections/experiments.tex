\section{Experiments}
The subject is Qwen3.5-0.8B with thinking enabled. Fixed subsets cover GSM8K, MATH-500, IFEval, MMLU-Pro and HellaSwag. We also measure WikiText perplexity, refusal on harmful prompts, over-refusal on harmless prompts, and KL to the base. Loop rate is the fraction of responses pooled across GSM8K, MATH-500 and IFEval that reach the token limit without EOS. Refusal keyword matching is a surface measure. It does not establish harmfulness.

The fast tier uses 50 GSM8K, 25 MATH-500, 25 IFEval, 100 MMLU-Pro and 100 HellaSwag items, plus 50 prompts each for refusal, over-refusal and KL. Generation is capped at 1024 tokens. At these sizes a single binomial score has a standard error of 0.03 to 0.10 (Section~\ref{sec:reliability}), so GSM8K, MATH-500 and IFEval are reported as descriptive at this tier. MMLU-Pro, HellaSwag, WikiText perplexity and KL become predictor targets only after their measurement floors are established. Cheap scores will calibrate a subset of the fast-tier entries. The cheap tier runs the larger fixed battery with a 4096-token limit, except for MMLU-Pro, which has a 1024-token limit. A faithful 32768-token tier is reserved for the base and at most 10 selected entries. We plan to start with a clean timing measurement and a stratified production subset, and to extend coverage after that. D3 samples production entries without replacement using seed 0 and sorted IDs, with quotas of 60 random direction, 100 abliteration, 60 LoRA SFT, 30 task vector and 50 chain steps; it takes all available entries if a family falls short. On the manifest snapshot in Table~\ref{tab:zoo-counts}, these quotas resolve to 300 entries. Sampling settings and fixed item IDs are recorded in the evaluation configuration.

Table~\ref{tab:base-repro} gives the completed base run at the cheap tier, with item counts and 95\% Wilson intervals, beside the base values that the blog reports \citep{takehi2026wsp}. The blog's budgets, sample counts and loop definition differ, so these figures are not matched estimates. The 32k rerun was interrupted when the GPU ran out of memory, and the IFEval difference still needs checker reconciliation.

Measured runs used four hosts. The Heretic ARA family runs on Lambda GPU compute: one NVIDIA A100-SXM4 40 GB instance at \$1.99/h. The local RTX PRO 4500 has 32 GB. Cross-host controls and D3 shards also ran on a Lambda A10 and a Lambda H100 PCIe, and some D3 entries moved between hosts during the run.
