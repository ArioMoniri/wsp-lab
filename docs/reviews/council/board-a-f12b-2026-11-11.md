## Numbered conditions and rulings

1. **Provenance, head and cheaper fits — ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS.** Zero-initialising the output and scaling the residual target by that fit's training-only SD are legitimate prospective training amendments. Code inspection confirms separate inner-training nuisance fits/scales and an outer-training refit; the output is unscaled before adding F3. Zero output gives nesting for every input; no-token zero edits remain zero after training. The architecture and nominal count are retained. A degenerate residual scale fails closed. This is a change to the training algorithm, not merely numerical formatting, and must govern the analysis as well as simulation.

   ACCEPT the one-thread StatNN fit and ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS seed-0 inner tree-count selection: the latter changes selection and potentially comparator strength, even though the outer predictor still averages three seeds. Freeze it for both simulation and analysis, disclose it, and retain the conjunction. Do not describe unchanged final seed averaging as unchanged model selection. These amendments do not spend held outcomes on the stated evidence. The implementer's attestation supports its own session only; obtain all required decision-maker attestations through the final freeze, including the disclosed prescore-audit qualification in original condition 1. Synthetic diagnostics do not retrospectively satisfy the C1 requirement for governed execution. No held outcome may choose the next amendment.

2. **Studentiser — ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS as a new procedure; REJECT a claim of established conservatism.** Apply exactly `max(V_comp + V_item - V_both, V_comp)` at the point and every joint draw for the two F12 contrasts only. Keep all conditional rules, loss/component guards and planned draws; no retry, replacement or deletion. This explicitly supersedes original condition 3's prohibition on repairs for these contrasts, not elsewhere.

   The rule defines a reproducible bootstrap-t procedure, but positive donor-level common-item correlation does not prove nonnegative interaction corrections for the linearised absolute-loss ratio. Flooring raises some estimated variances, while changing both bootstrap t quantiles and the point multiplier; interval width and rejection probability need not move monotonically. Frequent binding is a substantive procedure change. In the supplied reduced run it binds in 99–1,713/4,000 draws; strict validity is zero of forty replicates. Twenty of twenty valid under the amended rule is not twenty of twenty valid under A5.4.2 as originally written.

   Report separately, by contrast/k/scenario: amended validity rate with planned denominator; strict validity rate; raw nonpositive/nonfinite counts; draw binding fraction and distribution; point binding rate; and strict/one-way-max sensitivities on identical draws. Report failed guards by cause. No invented rarity threshold or post-run choice of studentiser. Size/coverage qualification under condition 3 is indispensable. Enforce the text's finite positive V_comp requirement explicitly for joint draws and point: the present joint validity mask checks the final variance, not V_comp separately. `joint_variance` can also replace a nonfinite raw value by finite V_comp through `np.where`; reject nonfinite terms rather than treating them as a repairable negative subtraction. Add adversarial tests for these cases and aggregate validity/binding summaries.

3. **Null and size interpretation — REJECT gamma=0 as an exact no-correction null; ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS a minimal reinterpretation.** Under `y = a + epsilon`, ridge-1 is an estimated, shrunken predictor, not the oracle linear response. A nonlinear residual head can recover its deterministic shrinkage. The reported OLS advantage of about 0.036 is a relevant warning, not evidence of type-I error. Substituting a population ridge fit into the generator does not repair this: refitting ridge shrinks again. Nor does an out-of-fold ridge operator in the generator automatically supply a self-consistent null. Changing F3 to OLS would change the scientific comparator and requires its own amendment; it is not authorised here.

   **Minimal registrable fix:** retain the actual F3 algorithm and gamma=0 DGP, rename this the *linear-signal/ridge-bias stress scenario*, and retain its five <=0.07 upper-bound requirements as conservative operational safety requirements. Explicitly withdraw their interpretation as type-I size certification at C=0. This changes no observed count, threshold or purchase escape route: a failed stress gate still blocks purchase. Add interval coverage/error qualification at each contrast's actual expected-row-sum risk ratio under the frozen procedure; do not centre at zero merely because gamma=0. Register the target computation using independent noise/training streams, its Monte Carlo uncertainty, treatment of qualification failures, and fixed replication/cost before stage 1. Target estimation from the same accepted replicates would be circular; valid-only conditioning must be labelled rather than silently changing the estimand.

   If the intended release claim includes controlled false detection at a no-correction boundary, **also register and qualify genuine boundary-null cases** for the relevant contrast(s), including the composite-null case where only one comparator has no gain. State/prove the relevant expected-risk inequality or equality for the complete learning procedure, not just absence of an h* term. A zero-signal generator alone does not prove equal risks of two finite-sample learners. Each marginal lower-bound size requirement must be applied only where that marginal null is true. Keep exact CP bounds, invalid-as-rejection and a prospective simultaneous allocation for any added gating cases. No broad type-I guarantee follows from the present single DGP, even if it passes. This interpretation and the missing coverage qualification must be explicitly resolved before stage 1; a favourable stress count cannot waive them.

4. **Alternative and decision language — ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS the existing alternative and conjunction; REJECT a general K+F-specific power interpretation.** Retain the two-comparator ordered claim. A head-shaped token function with oracle C=0.15 against F3 need not be a signal uniquely available to WM-R. StatNN receives the same tokens and may learn it. The reduced run has alternative mean C_WM 0.224 and mean C_WM,S 0.057, but the governing ratios of sums are 0.166 and 0.025; four detections in twenty are descriptive, not a power verdict. Do not substitute mean replicate ratios for the registered estimand.

   Adopt the implementer's statement that this is a token-function alternative calibrated against F3, but qualify the proposed failure wording: **“The registered two-comparator decision did not meet its power qualification under this alternative; it does not establish absence of a K+F correction.”** Attribute failure specifically to the same-input tree only when the separate comparator results support that attribution; fit failures, noise and F3 uncertainty can also cause failure. Publish both contrasts and their marginal detection rates descriptively without creating separate purchase routes. Retain the original field-only wording for a qualified upper-bound result; no failure-to-reject becomes absence.

   A second alternative is not required to estimate the detection probability for this explicitly limited scenario. It **is required before claiming adequate power against a specified WM-over-StatNN advantage**: prospectively define a joint alternative with nontrivial margins against both comparators, calibrate/independently validate the StatNN margin as well as the F3 margin under the frozen learning algorithms, and cost it. Calibrating an oracle's margin is not sufficient to assert a learned WM-R margin. No restriction of h* chosen after power results, no removal of StatNN, and no “either alternative passes” purchase path without a new multiplicity/selection registration. The existing conjunction can be a meaningful operational gate for its scenario, not a universal sensitivity certificate for the thesis.

5. **Calibration, two-stage selection and Monte Carlo precision — ACCEPT WITH CONDITIONS.** ACCEPT 4,000 calibration plus 4,000 independent validation panels, ratio-of-expected-row-sums calibration, +/-0.02 validation qualification and 50-halving limit. For positive fixed numerator, nonzero residual signal direction and C(0)<target, the absolute-affine denominator is convex and a higher level has a unique positive crossing when bracketed. Thus replacing global monotonicity by the registered first bracket is legitimate. Keep degeneracy/bracket/nonfinite failures, no new h* seeds, and report the old monotonicity checks and validation MC SE. The gamma artifacts support improved precision on synthetic inputs, not real-token qualification.

   ACCEPT the two-stage selection in principle. Stage 1 deliberately selects on pilot *detection and stress-size rates*: smallest k with >=36/40 alternative detections and <=2/40 events for each of five stress rates; otherwise k=145. It is not effect-blind, but independent stage 2 prevents a choose-the-passing-stage-2-k path. Stage 1 uses r=1000–1039 and its own panel domain; stage 2 uses r=0–999. Shared fixed features/noise distribution do not defeat independence of these draw streams. Freeze the stage-1 report/hash and computed k* before any stage-2 replicate. Stage 2 is exactly one k, 1,000 null/stress + 1,000 alternative replicates, no fallback k, extension, partial-run decision or repeated pilot. Retaining alpha=0.05/36 is conservative for the original six rates; power >=838 and stress counts <=46 at n=1000 are correctly implemented boundary examples. Invalid replicates remain power failures and stress rejections with planned denominators. Added qualification gates require a prospective allocation, not reuse of the same nominal coverage claim without accounting.
