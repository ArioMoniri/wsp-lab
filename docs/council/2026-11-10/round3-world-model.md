## 1. What the six news items change

**1 — WeightLoop: amend H5 implementation, not its evidential threshold.** The fixed editor rebuilds patches from the original checkpoint, selects using support validation and preserves a permanent test set. The primary paper distinguishes seven-of-eight improvements over unchanged models from eight-of-eight over random edits ([paper, §§2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.07319)). This is feedback-driven adaptation, not a prospective LLM-delta forecaster. Register immutable anchor/current-state identities, deterministic rebuild/reload checks, validation-panel exposure and rollback/no-op accounting. For adaptive search, validation feedback belongs to episode history; the critic remains frozen during G8. Retain A5.7's ≥30% realised savings lower bound, final-score noninferiority lower bound ≥−1 percentage point, missed-winner upper bound ≤5%, and net compute savings. Rebuilding from the base cannot silently replace G8's actual-incumbent proposal definition.

**2 — Neural Thickets: add a separate scale diagnostic.** It measures Gaussian-neighborhood task-improvement density across Qwen2.5 scales and selects/ensembles sampled models; it does not learn edit-response forecasts ([§§2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12228)). Inference: few winners at 0.8B hurt search utility and label diversity, but harmful edits can still have predictable signed responses. Neither sparse winners nor a positive KL threshold estimates the density defined for task-score improvements. Task, perturbation radius, architecture and pretraining all limit transfer to our zoo. Keep Part A; avoid purchasing hundreds more random directions.

**3 — LoL: add a registered secondary, not a replacement primary.** Its 2,000 Qwen2-1.5B LoRAs support held-out-network ARC-C prediction; Table 5 reports R² .987 for GL-net and .983 for singular-value MLP. Thus published accuracy prediction is real, but does not isolate directional information beyond spectra ([§§3,5](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.04207)). For Δ=sBA, the gauge action is B→BQ, A→Q⁻¹A. GL-equivariant layers with an invariant head remove this factorization ambiguity without erasing functional orientation relative to fixed base coordinates. Our field/chart are already invariant to factor refactorization; the new arm adds a learned representation of directions discarded by their contractions, not privileged factor information. Canonical tokens and GL-net overlap in purpose, not implementation. No theorem here aligns different bases or supplies predecessor dynamics.

**4 — Blog debate: no programme change.** The [authors' own experiments 7–8 and Gemma addendum](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/) separate tool-assisted loop repair from adding reasoning ability. The [MaceTenth replication](https://github.com/MaceTenth/weight-space-programming-replication) reports small-network successes; these do not qualify our TF endpoint. The fetched [csinva repository](https://github.com/csinva/weight-space-programming-autoresearch) now describes interpretable model construction, so I do not repeat the digest's purported negative conclusion as its current finding. Commentary is not an independent predictor experiment.

**5 — Neural Field Adaptation: nothing to add.** Multiplicative LoRA supports representation/reconstruction/generation for neural fields ([abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01759)); that is not an LM behavioral response field. Shared base plus low rank is useful population structure, not evidence for our response decoder.

**6 — Adjacent forecasting: no new primary.** [Proxy-metric forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18607) reads output distributions; [KNOWN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05059) predicts weights through forgetting trajectories. Neither answers our held-out delta-to-benchmark question. A limited source check cannot establish that no relevant 2026 paper exists. Keep activation-assisted comparators explicitly labelled.
