# Round 8 — metrology

## BLUF
1. Count independent source-closure components, not edit steps, items, or dose variants, as trajectory replication units.
2. Put every trajectory sharing a root, direction/seed, adapter, ancestry, or identical realised delta in one split and one resampling unit.
3. Hold out entire trajectories for rollout claims; hold out edit families separately if claiming family transfer.
4. Make continuous paired log-likelihood change the primary forecast target; never turn an unresolved interval into a “same” label.
5. Generation outcomes are a separate, noisier endpoint: use fresh prompts and repeated decoding runs, not NLL as a proxy.
6. Round-7 MSRM's 123 rows collapsed to 29 closure groups; this is a direct warning against row-count power claims.
7. Freeze disjoint development and final-test item groups before enhancement search; choose the winner on development items only.
8. Name one primary capability and one primary enhancement contrast; treat other endpoints/arms as secondary with multiplicity control.
9. The 1.04 min/edit TF-v1 reference is measured on 0.8B, not a 9B/4B price; pilot the chosen target and full scoring path.
10. No large-bank or enhancement spend until a timed cost pilot and outcome-blind component/item power simulation pass.

## Units, outcomes, and splits

**Independent units.** The unit is a connected source component: a root edit trajectory with all its dependent steps and aliases. Join trajectories that share a generating direction or seed, adapter/task vector, parent or ancestry, or byte-identical realised delta. Steps on one path are repeated measures, not independent observations; multiple prompts/windows scored on the same step improve endpoint precision but do not increase the number of independent trajectories. Keep all connected material in one train/validation/test fold and resample whole components. The same principle matters in the prior study: 123 MSRM rows were 29 closure groups, with 105 chain rows concentrated in 16 groups ([MSRM-1 dev, “Rows, closure and provenance”](../../reviews/msrm/msrm1-dev-v0-2026-11-16.md)). Do not register an N in rows. Set the required count of independent trajectory components by an outcome-blind simulation on pilot variance and actual cluster sizes.

For rollout prediction, split by complete trajectories before fitting any feature transforms or selecting hyperparameters. Report error by horizon against a frozen per-step/static predictor, and obtain uncertainty by component bootstrap. If the paper claims generalisation to unseen edit families, also hold out whole families; that is a distinct, harder estimand and should not be inferred from random trajectory holdout. Use one primary continuous endpoint and horizon (or a prespecified aggregate); correct secondary horizon/family comparisons rather than selecting the best-looking horizon.

**Label noise.** WSR's paper labels changes with per-behaviour Welch tests and minimum effects, based on sampled generation outcomes; its cells ran once, the changed-label sample was small, and the authors report no multiplicity correction ([Weight-Space Reading](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.weight-space-reading-language-models)). Its thresholded up/down/same labels are therefore a poor primary trajectory target here. Prefer paired teacher-forced log-likelihood/NLL deltas on fixed examples: retain the continuous values and item-level pairing, report intervals and the established TF-v1 resolution floor (0.0049397 nats/token; [SYNTHESIS-r7](SYNTHESIS-r7.md)). “Same” means unresolved, never evidence of equivalence. NLL is not a substitute for generated task success, refusal, or style: if a generation endpoint is needed, freeze decoding, use fresh prompt groups, replicate stochastic decoding, and analyze it separately with prompt/run clustering.

**Enhancement split and multiplicity.** Hash-normalize and deduplicate prompts into groups before freezing disjoint world-model training, search/development, and final-test panels. The planner, candidate ranking, stopping rule, objective, and guardrail margins may use development outcomes only. Freeze the world-model winner and the named comparator's winner from development, then score both and the untouched base on the final test panel with paired item-group intervals. Use a single primary capability and one primary contrast (world-model-guided winner versus the strongest prespecified equal-budget comparator); compare all search arms on the same development budget. Prespecify any multiple confirmatory arms/capabilities and adjust them (e.g. Holm); guardrails need their own declared non-inferiority margins and simultaneous or adjusted bounds. A test-set result cannot select the objective, candidate, arm, or threshold. If several edits are retained as co-winners, that is another multiplicity problem, not a reason to report the best test score.

## Q3 — target-specific GPU pricing and pilot

`PREREG_H1_TF.md` F8a.8 prices the full-config 0.8B TF-v1 path at 1.04 min per entry, based on a 1.003-min warm median (0.994–1.040); the 47.1-s cache load and 1.751-min cold-base entry are separate costs. These are reference rates, not a scalable 9B/4B estimate. The earlier 4B Lambda scale diagnostic recorded 0.16 GPU-h and $0.54 ([PROGRESS.md, 10 Nov 22:30](../../PROGRESS.md)), an observed effective rate of about $3.38/GPU-h for that particular diagnostic only. It used a limited WikiText diagnostic and is not the price of full TF-v1 or a current provider quote.

For the proposed Qwen3.5-9B target, first run a bounded, pre-registered timing pilot on the exact frozen eval config and intended device: one base, three distinct edited checkpoints, and one fresh-process repeat of an edit. Record GPU-seconds separately for model load, cache build/load, each endpoint, edit restore/application, and retries; record peak memory and whether any endpoint changes numeric path. Use the complete planned item counts for the primary endpoint on this pilot (a reduced panel would not price the final path). Reuse no 0.8B/4B rate as a cost estimate. Before launch, set a dollar/GPU-hour source and a hard GPU-hour cap; then compute per-entry cost and all-in trajectory/enhancement budgets from the measured warm and cold rates. If 9B fails the device/memory or cost cap, use Qwen3.5-4B only as the explicitly chosen single target and repeat the same pilot there. Do not compare model claims across targets as if they were one study.

WSR's public repo describes measurement as GPU work, with five base runs and edited-model measurement, but gives no aggregate GPU-hour cost for its paper; vLLM is optional for faster measurement. It releases the harness, presets, tests, and downloadable probe pools (`wsr data`), not the paper's result data; the eight agent-proposed self-edit pilot edits are explicitly not distributed ([WSR repository](https://github.com/suayptalha/wsr)). Thus a matched WSR head-to-head needs its own timed pilot and data-availability check, not an assumed reproduction cost.

## Q6 — staged go/no-go

1. **Measurement pilot:** continue only if the target loads within the device budget, repeated deterministic likelihood scores meet a preregistered reproducibility tolerance, and the measured full-config cost fits a fixed cap. Otherwise stop or make the predeclared 4B switch before outcome collection.
2. **Noise and power gate:** use pilot paired item residuals and actual source-component sizes in an outcome-blind simulation. Test null size, interval coverage, denominator/failure rates, and power for the exact primary trajectory contrast, enhancement contrast, and multiplicity rule. Require size and coverage error no greater than 0.07 and a lower binomial bound on power of at least 0.80, matching the prior programme's simulation gates ([SYNTHESIS-r7](SYNTHESIS-r7.md)); find the smallest passing number of independent components. If no feasible count fits the declared budget, do not build the large bank.
3. **Trajectory development gate:** on whole-trajectory held-outs, require the frozen world model to beat the strongest frozen static/per-step baseline on the primary continuous target, with an interval excluding zero and performance distinguishable from the paired measurement floor. No claim follows from better fit on steps seen during training.
4. **Enhancement confirmation:** proceed only with a fresh item test panel, a frozen winner-selection policy, equal real-evaluation budgets, and powered paired inference. Call the model enhanced only if the primary held-out gain's lower confidence bound exceeds zero and every registered guardrail's non-inferiority bound passes. Otherwise report unresolved or no-go; do not enlarge the panel, swap endpoints, or retune after test exposure.
