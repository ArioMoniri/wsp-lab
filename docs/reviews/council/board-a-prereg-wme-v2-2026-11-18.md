## Scientific assessment and arithmetic

The registered E1 claim is a selected edit's fresh-panel sycophancy-gap benefit with surrogate preservation and transfer to suggested-answer correctness. It does not establish broad accuracy improvement or generated safety. E2 tests the frozen WM-E-guided search against the registered static-guided search at equal evaluation opportunity. It does not establish intrinsic memory dependence, trajectory understanding or value at total compute parity. W's pooled-curve results defeat H-T2 registration under the declared rule; H-T3 remains a slot, not a claim. S-TE must retain the frozen-budget wording and the longer-budget/untruncated analyses regardless of the result. No “improves accuracy” upgrade is available under P3.

The H-E1 intersection–union construction is appropriate: selective designation on development data does not require an extra test-set winner correction when the single designated winner and all conjunctive endpoints are fixed before reading the fresh test. Holm over ten E2 secondaries includes S-BoN, which remains reported even if the primary fails. Separate alpha families require the existing prohibition on an unadjusted study-level success assertion.

### R6 decision

**Accept retaining the frozen failed R6 analysis only as a non-evidentiary descriptive diagnostic; do not require another redesign to seek a favorable result.** The latest addendum explicitly permits this. It is scientifically preferable to show the failure and stop rather than tune against the same null/alternative repeatedly. Under the quadratic truth, WM-CP stays at the exact field more often while static selection leaves it; the resulting small same-signed errors can generate apparent WM advantages. The reported 22/284 and 1/107 are stopped-run rejection fractions, not evidence of calibrated nominal inference. Ordinary fixed-N Clopper–Pearson intervals at these outcome-dependent stopping times are not guaranteed sequential coverage. The stopping rule nevertheless establishes failure of the planned 600-replicate size criterion: 22 rejections already exceed its allowance of 21.

“Descriptive” alone is insufficient protection: no positive C_T, p-value or interval from this pipeline may support WM-CP superiority. The 0.077 fraction, stopping caveat, selection mechanism and negative alternative result must accompany its presentation. H-T3 must use its own pooled-curve comparator and independently specified null calibration. The failed static-field diagnostic does not certify or automatically invalidate that different comparison. E2 still needs its own test assumptions; R6's failure cannot be dismissed as proof that every other inference is valid.
