## Reading (implementer; exploratory, development decisions only)

- **k1 does not fire.** On these exposed rows MSRM-1 (F3u + tree head over x(D)) reduced pooled out-of-fold absolute error relative to the unpenalised field readout: C = +0.378 [+0.206, +0.501] with 29 closure groups as units. The sign holds in the 79-row subset (+0.244 [+0.071, +0.389]) and with the 89 registered components as units (+0.405 [+0.286, +0.542]). All three shuffled-outcome controls show no gain.
- **k2b fires.** The scripted reader (the same statistics, no field anywhere) is not resolved below MSRM-1: C(MSRM-1 : scripted) = +0.258 with interval [−0.094, +0.440]. The point estimate favours MSRM-1, but under the pinned margin rule "the field adds nothing beyond statistics" is not excluded at 29 units. With registered components as units the lower bound is +0.002. Within the nested model the ten field columns of the head add C(MSRM-1 : field-ablated) = +0.102 [+0.025, +0.163].
- **k2 does not fire.** C(MSRM-1 : description-only) = +0.354 [+0.032, +0.510]. A description-only model alone is roughly as good as F3u (+0.037 vs F3u), and adding a description head to F3u already gives +0.245. A sizeable part of MSRM-1's gain over F3u is therefore reachable from family/dose/role/layer priors on top of the field. MSRM-1 still improves on F3u + description: +0.176 [+0.079, +0.275].
- **StatNN.** The F12 same-input StatNN comparator (LightGBM over x, the v4 statistics and the 186 × 43 token block) is at least as good as MSRM-1: C(MSRM-1 : StatNN) = −0.091 [−0.372, +0.075]. This is not a kill criterion here, but it repeats F12's lesson: the learned correction to the field is available to a tree over the existing token matrix, and the 54-column x(D) adds nothing visible beyond it.
- **Independence is the binding limit.** The closure audit joined registered components that share random-direction generator seeds or identical abliteration directions, applied at some chain step. The 123 rows therefore form 29 closure groups, 21 in the 79-row subset; one outer fold holds 59 rows. Chain contributes 105 rows in 16 groups. Per-family and leave-one-family-out results rest on 2–10 groups and are descriptive only. Bootstrap intervals over 21–29 units are approximate.
- **Development consequence.** Under SYNTHESIS-r7, k2b firing is a development stop signal for MSRM-1 v0 as specified. Whether to stop, to narrow the comparison, or to buy the optional development roots is the chair's and the user's decision, not this report's. Nothing here is evidence for or against the thesis, and nothing here bears on Part A.

## Implementer choices (fixed before outcomes were read)

- x(D) = the 48-vector plus six scripted columns (54). a_t, q_t and σ₁ come from the pinned F12 token build; the stats.py statistics from the pinned v4 features; ⟨D, W⟩, ‖W‖, the intended delta and the leading output vectors from a CPU pass over the stored bytes and the pinned base weights, authenticated against `increment_sha256`.
- Unembedding mean ū = mean over vocabulary rows of the unembedding × (1 + final-norm weight); the |cos| column covers residual writers (o, down) only, energy-weighted.
- Fraction changed = changed tensors / 186 supported matrix slots; rounding loss = intended nonzeros realised as 0 / intended nonzeros.
- Input transform: signed asinh by the training median |x| of nonzero entries, then standardisation; all statistics from training rows only.
- MLP head follows F5.1 / F12b.1 (zero-initialised output, residual target scaled by its training SD); the tree head uses LightGBM with the F12 settings and an L1 objective; ridge λ from {1e-2, …, 1e3}.
- Scripted reader and description-only arms have an intercept-only base (no field anywhere); field-ablated MSRM-1 keeps F3u and drops the ten field columns from the head; StatNN is the F12.3 augmented comparator with F3u in place of ridge F3.
- Name-blind: depth buckets reordered by |a_b| and role shares sorted (identity-free order statistics); delta-energy-weighted depth dropped.
- Every residual head is fitted as the function it is evaluated as, h(x) − h(x(0)): the MLP is trained on that difference, ridge on the anchored design x − x(0) without intercept, and the tree head with an anchor row (x(0), 0) weighted by the training-row count (found in the synthetic dry run, before any outcome was read).
- Leave-one-family-out uses the 89 registered components (F12a.1(i)); its rows are not closure-independent of the training rows, so it is descriptive only.
- Head class chosen once on all 123 rows (literal reading of 'chosen once'); a nested per-fold re-selection is reported as an honesty check. The 79-row sensitivity reuses the chosen class.
- Description-only uses edit.json parameters and the names of stored tensors; 'F3u + description' is reported descriptively to separate family priors from the field.
- Closure audit: ancestry, generator, per-direction random seeds (seed + 1000·layer + 100·di, as wsp.zoo.build draws them), exact abliteration directions (refusal fingerprint + direction_index) and duplicate delta bytes. The registered A5 closure does not join directions; joining them merges the 89 registered components into the closure groups used as fold and bootstrap units (registered components reported as a sensitivity). provenance.reuse_key is not a join (an environment hash without parameters or seed). Part A edit records are never opened, so a direction shared with a Part A entry is not detectable.
