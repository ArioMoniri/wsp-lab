## Declared harness (M1), as implemented

**Prompt.**
- The question, then "Options:", then "(A) …" to "(D) …".
- Then: *Think step by step, then end your response with the final answer in the form "Final Answer: (X)", where X is
  the letter of the correct option.*
- It goes through the model's own chat template, which has no thinking switch.

**Extractor.** Rules are tried in this order. Nothing else is accepted, and a letter outside the item's options is
unparsed.
1. **Final Answer:** the last "Final Answer:" immediately followed by "(X)" or a bare capital X.
2. **Trailing `\boxed{X}`:** the response ends with `\boxed{X}`. Only whitespace, `$`, `.` or `*` may follow it.
3. **Letter-only final line:** the last non-empty line holds only the letter.

A truncated response is incorrect and is not parsed.
- Tests: `tests/test_wmm_gate0b.py` has 34 tests, covering accepted forms, rejected forms (including the old
  "The answer is (X)"), precedence, designation and the pass rule.

**Budget:** 4,096 new tokens.

**Items.**
- 400 MedQA 4-option dev items (`openlifescienceai/medqa` @ `153e61cd`), out of 1,272.
- They are ranked by `sha256("wmm-g0b:medqa:dev:" + text hash)`.
- Every item in the Gate 0 exposure list (784 items) was removed first, by text hash and by (source, id).
  Disjointness is asserted in code; the overlap is 0.
- The 400 items are recorded as EXPOSED in `data/wmm/gate0b-2026-11-20/medqa_g0b_exposure.json`.

**Observation (descriptive, not a change).**
- With the card-format instruction, MedGemma 1.5 almost always ends with its trained form, "Final Answer: The final
  answer is $\boxed{X}$". Rule 1 does not match that form, because a word follows the colon. Rule 2 does.
- The declared precedence therefore resolves 96 % of answers through the trailing-boxed rule. That is as declared;
  the extractor was not changed.
