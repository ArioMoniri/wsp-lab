The input hashes therefore also match the live committed metadata.

Continuing A5.3.1's key is the only order that can honestly be described as a continuation of that registered “next-in-cell” selection. It is not the only conceivable legitimate prospective amendment: a different independently frozen order could be proposed and reviewed as a new choice. Neither ledger storage order, norm sorting nor a new convenient seed is interchangeable with this registered continuation. No such alternative is authorized here. Likewise, root/exact-gradient/not-alias-only filters and the allocation are new disclosed choices, not rules that the original quota already required.

The alias-only exclusion is acceptable as a uniformly applied, pre-staging own-bytes requirement. **3. Sizing.** k=12 with four per depleted cell is reasonable as a fixed risk buffer, not a guarantee or a mathematically optimal design. The registered-scale anchors 31/34/37/39 available rotations give power 0.7514/0.7722/0.8251/0.8453. They restore particular old slots; they do not certify an arbitrary supplementary allocation. Cell probabilities explain why: approximately 1.00 for LoRA, 0.573 chain-low, 0.659 chain-middle and 0.906 chain-high. Discrete CP thresholds also prevent a simple monotone slot-count argument. The emulator's 36-slot crossing is allocation-dependent; its all-allocation 38-slot result is planning evidence only.

Four per cell gives one extra candidate beyond the three losses in each cell. It avoids cross-cell optimization and buffers uncertain audit yield, while roots avoid the identified non-base replay mechanism. The 2/2 root pass observation does not establish a near-certain root pass probability or remove other F10 failure mechanisms. The metrology grid gives 0.86–1.00 gate-pass probability under assumed pass rates, not a calibrated confidence guarantee. Its all-pass 500-replicate power 0.872 [0.840, 0.900] is unregistered planning evidence. The metrology draft included the alias-only root, so its numbers are not direct measurements of this final twelve-ID list; the simulator's cell-based model makes the within-cell substitution irrelevant to that proxy, not necessarily to real audit yield or response.
