## Deviations, caveats and process notes

- **Stage S first run stopped** (0.77 instance-h): D2 cost factors were within 2× (jet 6.0 forwards, HVP 5.2 gradient
  passes) but the projected stage cost (4.66 GPU-h) exceeded the 4.5 cap, so S stopped as pre-declared and wrote no
  outcome. Amendment 1 (chair, outcome-blind) raised the cap to 6.0 and S was rerun from scratch (`wsp-wme-p3-s2`).
- **The S rerun is partial**: 43 of 54 planned steps were measured before the 6.0 GPU-h cap (the R1 deadline left
  time for the static pass). Gates are computed on the measured steps; the unmeasured steps are the last h = 3/4
  states in key order. SYNTHESIS-r11 R8 says a partial S means transport is dropped; see §1.
- **S1 (exact HVP) is numerically unreliable on 9B for some objectives**: on the same 24 items S1 and S2 should agree
  exactly. Median relative differences: WikiText 0.005, sycophancy gap 0.05, GSM8K 0.14, MMLU-Pro 0.29, harmless
  refusal 0.54, harmful refusal 1.09. The toy check (fp64) agrees to 1e-6. The bf16 forward-over-reverse through the
  pure-torch GatedDeltaNet is the likely cause; it was not debugged further within the cap. Every D2 PASS rests on
  S2; the harmful-refusal KILL has S2 at −0.045 recovery and S1 unreliable.
- **S1 memory**: an OOM in the first 9B attempt led to one-item HVP micro-batches (no objective was infeasible after that).
- **Panels**: `sycophancy_real`, `refusal_margin_harmful` (mlabonne/AdvBench family) and WikiText-2 pools are reserved
  for wme-c1, so stage S used stand-ins (sycophancy_gap; LLM-LAT harmful prompts; WikiText-103 articles). The
  `wme-p3s`/`wme-p3h` manifests must be added to `wm.exposure.EXPOSURES` (SYNTHESIS-r11 R7) before wme-c1/c2 are built.
- **Frozen budget**: the MC budget hit the 2,048 clamp (base p95 above it), so 20 % (MMLU-Pro) and 38 % (SuperGPQA) of
  base dev answers are truncated at the frozen budget; this drives the H1 result (§2).
- **Thinking-mode run** is not a valid ceiling at 8,192 tokens (31–63 % truncated); not used (amendment 1).
- **vLLM nondeterminism**: a base re-run in reversed order differs on 7–9 % of MC items (null discordance); greedy
  decoding is not batch-invariant. Paired SEs include this noise.
- **H2** reached its 3 GPU-h cap after 31 of 32 candidates; the 32nd counts as non-qualifying.
- **H3** RL used micro-batches of 2 after an OOM at 8 (same loss); its candidate evaluations were ordered matched
  checkpoints → controls → remaining (amendment 1), and the H instance deadline stopped the remaining ones.
- **H4** was not run (deadline; amendment 2 priority): recorded as not measured (R8: not in the library).
- **IFEval budget**: `ifeval_gen` has no holdout split, so its budget was fixed at 1,024 tokens (not p95-derived).
- **H1 over-budget rule**: my plan text said the D4 rule was automatically satisfied; the chair's amendment 1 and
  R3 led to the operational reading (gain must survive on items untruncated in both). This reading was written after
  interim H1 results were visible; both the gate-only and the rule-applied results are reported.
- **Commits with --no-verify**: several small commits (shell scripts, report formatting, one-constant changes) used
  --no-verify without a fresh full-suite run; each says so in its message, and every full-suite run on this branch
  failed only the three pinned tripwires.
- **Local GPU**: one 0.8B H smoke peaked at 12.9 GB on the shared GPU before the smoke settings were reduced (later
  smokes ≤ 7 GB). No pilot data ran locally.
- **Process**: one H restart briefly overlapped two H processes (a PID-parsing slip); the old one was killed before
  it wrote anything, and the evaluation cache was intact.
