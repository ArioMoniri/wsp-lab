# Council decision — 5 November 2026

Chair branch: `council-chair`. Programme: **6–19 November 2026 inclusive**.

**Verdict:** the original dedicated weight-space world-model goal is unsupported at this scale; fund a bounded test of within-family teacher-forced effect readability, with a measurement/benchmark paper as the default deliverable. A tree winning is a valid outcome. Neither a new encoder nor additional checkpoint rows are evidence of a dynamical law.

This is an executable decision proposal, not authorization to launch compute. It preserves the original H1–H6 definitions and historical results in [HYPOTHESIS](../../HYPOTHESIS.md). All thresholds, caps and future counts below are prospective decisions; arithmetic projections are identified as such. No new experiments, live billing query or independent external reproduction were performed for this synthesis. Sources were read in the requested sequence: [brief](BRIEF.md), all five round-1 reports, [importance evidence](evidence-statnn-importances.md), [metrology output](metrology_budget_output.txt), all five round-2 reports and their two outputs, then HYPOTHESIS, [ROADMAP](../../ROADMAP.md), [H5_PROTOCOL](../../H5_PROTOCOL.md) and the [spend ledger](../../../board/lambda_spend.md).

## 1. Findings the council agrees on

1. **The original H1 bar has not been met.** The brief reports three-seed trajectory-split MMLU-Pro/HellaSwag Spearman of 0.57/0.65 for StatNN, 0.45/0.46 for M1-matched, 0.35/0.32 for M2 and 0.15/0.11 for M3. Held-family StatNN is −0.02/0.11; there is no demonstrated H2 or H3 success. Do not infer an empirical M5 failure without its results. Source: [BRIEF](BRIEF.md), [round2-jepawg](round2-jepawg.md).

2. **Most of the headline benchmark rank is available to descriptors.** On the exactly reproduced seed-0 trajectory panel, **345 matched rows**, descriptor-only versus StatNN is 0.523855 versus 0.565906 (MMLU), and 0.613582 versus 0.648602 (HellaSwag): gaps **0.042050/0.035019**. Descriptor-only has higher pooled R², 0.560316 versus 0.555032 and 0.629583 versus 0.588356. KL rank gap is 0.204828; log-PPL gap is −0.014405. The three-seed paired MAE gains versus descriptors are 4.1% [−6.3%,14.0%] and 5.54% [−6.3%,16.4%]. These comparisons use different estimators; they do not causally allocate information to weights. Sources: [importance report](evidence-statnn-importances.md), [round2_metrology_output §F](round2_metrology_output.txt).

3. **There is nevertheless exploratory residual MMLU signal.** After train-fold family + device + log-norm nuisance fitting, StatNN versus residual isotonic-norm gives θ=0.1462 [0.069,0.214], residual Spearman 0.484 versus 0.099, over 345 rows/278 clusters. In the outcome-free LoRA/chain/task stratum, θ=0.1922 [0.109,0.273], 187 rows/128 clusters. HellaSwag accuracy gives −0.0342 [−0.118,0.037] overall and −0.0061 [−0.101,0.078] in that stratum. This is **accuracy on reused P4 data**, not confirmation on new letter-scored log-prob. Source: [residual output](round2_residual_estimand_output.txt).

4. **Counts and independence bind the research.** The audited snapshot has 1,247 entries, 389 evaluated, **646 ancestry roots**, 281 labelled roots; the matched predictor subset has 278 roots. D3 is 300 base-relative entries, distinct from the 345 predecessor-matched rows. Only **94/300 (31.3%, derived)** exceed KL 0.01, across 86 roots: LoRA 56/60, chain 30/50, task 6/30, abliteration 2/100, random 0/60. The projected 538 non-null entries/~492 clusters are extrapolations, not observations. Production eligibility excludes current Heretic entries in this snapshot. Sources: [round2_metrology_output §§A–B,H](round2_metrology_output.txt), [round1-strategy census](round1-strategy.md). Freeze IDs and provenance hashes rather than repeating the brief's older 1,235 count.

5. **Repeatability does not establish useful effect resolution.** On D3, heuristic population correlation ceilings for MMLU gold log-prob/margin are 0.9839/0.9891 pooled, 0.9790/0.9850 after family/device centring. HellaSwag accuracy residual SD 0.0131 is below item SE 0.0136, giving zero estimated population signal; MMLU accuracy residual ceiling is 0.7679. Sampled GSM8K/MATH/IFEval/over-refusal repeat statistics are 0.1398/0.0744/0.0686/0.0986 against δ/2=0.05. GSM8K population ceiling is 0.5717 pooled, 0.1961 residual; the other three are zero. Refusal keyword can be repeatable yet saturated. Sources: [metrology_budget_output](metrology_budget_output.txt), [round1-metrology](round1-metrology.md). The TF-v1 projected reliabilities 0.996/0.997 for MMLU log-prob/margin, 0.959 for HellaSwag likelihood, 0.991 for WikiText are item-scaling estimates, not measurements of the new protocol ([round2 output §I](round2_metrology_output.txt)).

6. **The representation diagnosis must be specific.** StatNN's leading MMLU gain allocations are down/bucket0 stable-rank (7.93%), o/bucket0 entropy (5.68%), v/bucket0 stable-rank (5.28%); correlated gains are not causal importance. M1/M5 already receive exact layer/type IDs and projected SVD directions. Pooling alone cannot explain every neural failure. The current pool is 1,242 statistics +136 spectrum dimensions, whereas the importance refit's historical feature layout is 1,098 statistics: version these schemas explicitly. The stable-rank statistic uses retained top-eight energy. Sources: [importance evidence](evidence-statnn-importances.md), [round1-weight-reading §2](round1-weight-reading.md), [round2-features concession](round2-features.md).

7. **The blog motivates structure-aware controls, not our original predictive claim.** The specialist's inspected releases support weight-only game/shape/circuit recognition; Experiment 7 records four weights-only failures followed by attention-assisted success. Experiment 8 used activation/behavioral feedback for localized repair. Its reported MATH-500 0.481 and generated MMLU 0.455 versus base 0.403 are not our likelihood endpoints. It changed 130,188 effective bf16 values versus 131,072 touched positions. Gemma's MATH results 6.8→7.0–7.7 show no established comparable repair. No demonstrated held-out Δ-only benchmark predictor follows. These external facts are attributed to [round1-weight-reading's release audit and URLs](round1-weight-reading.md), not an independent chair reproduction. Likewise, [round1-jepawg](round1-jepawg.md) verified parameter-indexed physical populations in JEPAWG and QR/SVD LoRA canonicalization in W2T; neither supplies an established common dynamical chart for this zoo. “Weight-only” describes inference features, not the production of evaluation labels.

8. **The current H2 contrast is confounded.** M2 versus M2-matched shows 12.65% [3.9%,20.6%] apparent benefit on length-1 rows, where history is absent. At length≥4 there are only 18 matched rows/10 clusters, benefit 6.86% [−42.8%,40.9%]. Census closure and usable predictor rows differ: 99 roots have ≥4-node lineages, but only 16 have qualifying fully evaluated production ancestry. Source: [round2_metrology_output §§A,E](round2_metrology_output.txt). Eight training runs cannot resolve a 15% history effect; the stand-in MDE is ~67% ([round2-metrology §1.1](round2-metrology.md)).

## 3. Prospective amended hypotheses

Common contract: predecessor-relative y; incremental effective bf16 edit and cumulative predecessor state supplied separately; same-device/item/reference identity; ancestry/source-closed outer splits; nested train-only preprocessing and selection; seeds 0/1/2. Freeze training versus sealed confirmation roots and item hashes before fresh results are inspected. Independent item uncertainty is reported alongside ancestry bootstrap uncertainty, preserving shared references and text blocks. No held-out family means may be used to fit predictions. Existing D3 results remain exploratory.

**H1a — within-family readability of teacher-forced effects beyond family + device + log-norm.** Primary y is MMLU-Pro normalized gold-letter log-prob on 1,000 new items. Margin is secondary; no switching the primary after seeing outcomes. In each training fold fit additive OLS nuisance family dummies + device dummies + log‖Δ_eff‖F, with zero-norm handling frozen in config. Set r=y−m_train. Fit fixed-config StatNN to r and comparator C=isotonic(r on ‖Δ_eff‖F). For rows i in cluster c:

`θ = 1 − Σ_c Σ_i |r_i − r̂_i| / Σ_c Σ_i |r_i − r̂_i^C|`.

Use the CTO's row-summed estimand with cluster resampling, not a silently substituted equal-cluster-weight score. Report cluster-size sensitivity separately. Use inner out-of-fold nuisance residuals for fitting the residual model; refit nuisance on all outer training rows for test predictions. Primary inference: 4,000 ancestry-cluster percentile bootstrap resamples, 95% CI. Freeze non-null stratum by **family∈{lora_sft,task_vector,chain}**, not measured KL or observed task effect.

**Pass:** θ≥0.15, lower 95% bound>0, ≥100 fresh stratum clusters, qualified TF signal and a prospective power check. Precision continuation gate: CI half-width≤half the point estimate, as proposed by metrology; otherwise mark precision inadequate even if the sign test passes. **Fail/kill:** upper bound<0.05 on the qualified panel falsifies practical readability for this endpoint. Between these bars is inconclusive; stop at the cap. If fewer clusters or qualification fails, H1a is untested, not falsified. Planning sample: 110 existing LoRA/task entries (60+50; 98 roots, only 75 fresh to D3) plus ~30 fresh chain roots/~120 lineage states, nominal ~105 fresh roots. Shared adapter sources may reduce this count; certify components before unsealing. TF SD must be estimated on development data: 79 clusters derives from accuracy, not measured TF log-prob losses. Stronger family×spline(norm), task/rank/steps/support controls are mandatory sensitivity analyses; disappearance under them limits the claim to the additive nuisance specification. Sources: [round2-metrology §4.1](round2-metrology.md), [round2-reading protocol](round2-weight-reading.md).

**H1b — superiority over StatNN.** One frozen base-anchored directional arm, with a small regularized head, competes against original-feature StatNN and StatNN on the same augmented information; same labels, roots, folds and total tuning budget. **Pass:** ≥5% relative residual MAE gain over the strongest StatNN, positive paired 95% cluster interval, and ρ≥0.6 on the sole registered primary MMLU endpoint; report residual rank and all secondary failures. A tree benefiting from new features establishes representation utility, not learned-world-model superiority. **Sample:** same ~105 fresh roots are a screen only for this smaller effect; ~2,355 is the old feature-contrast stand-in for 5%, not a promised TF sample. Require a TF paired-loss simulation with ≥80% power at α=.05 before later confirmation. **Kill:** upper CI<5% rejects the practical advantage; wide intervals at the cap are inconclusive and prohibit scaling. No architecture sweep. Sources: [round2-strategy hypothesis amendments](round2-strategy.md), [round2-metrology power](round2-metrology.md).

**H2 — history ablation inside M2, closure-first.** Compare the same M2 architecture and matched initialization/training budget with true history, masked history and within-lineage shuffled history, all given identical current cumulative state and incremental edit. Include lag/trend and stateless controls but do not infer history from cross-architecture differences. On length-1 placebo rows the history/masked pipelines must be equivalent; CI excluding zero invalidates the contrast. **Pass:** ≥15% relative held-out residual MAE reduction over masked history with lower CI>0, also beating shuffle and lag/trend; fully measured length≥4 lineages, complete-run holdout. **Sample:** start with ~40 existing root closures (~170 missing entries, actual census overrides approximation); 99 closures require 426 missing entries and resolve only ~19% at the stand-in SD. Planning confirmation is ≥162 independent lineages, SD~0.68, with 77–540 sensitivity range; 63 additional four-node lineages/252 edits are unfunded. Actual sequential-training H2 needs checkpoint/optimizer provenance and separate timing. Arbitrary edit closures test only compressed-state edit-history usefulness. **Kill:** placebo failure, SD>1.0, no affordable 80%-powered design, or confirmatory upper CI<0.05. Broad CI means unresolved. Source: [round2-metrology §§1.1,4.1,6–7](round2-metrology.md).

**H3 — unseen edit-operator transfer, parked pending H1 gates.** Hold the merge operator and all descendants out of fitting and pretraining; compare frozen JEPA, same-input supervised head and StatNN. Report parent-known and source/parent-unseen arms separately; a held-out SFT task is a different diagnostic. **Pass:** ρ≥0.5 on qualified MMLU primary and positive paired residual-MAE advantage over both controls for JEPA superiority. **Sample:** planned minimum 100 independent held-out source components, then simulation must justify ≥80% power for the declared paired effect; this is a chair minimum, not a measured sufficient n. No funded collection this fortnight. **Kill:** ρ upper CI<0.2 on the powered panel, no paired advantage at the capped study, or insufficient source-independent components. The original random/ablation→LoRA claim remains unsupported. Source: [HYPOTHESIS H3](../../HYPOTHESIS.md), [round2-strategy](round2-strategy.md).

**H4 — park paid comparison.** Reopen only with model identity/access, token budget and transcript consent; identical frozen summaries, blinded edits, replicated prompts. **Sample:** the qualified ≥100-root H1 panel, plus a separately powered novel-operator panel; no claim of novel-family robustness from in-family edits. **Pass:** trained predictor wins in-family and reader wins on held-out operator, each by positive paired error intervals, as the original directional hypothesis requires. **Kill:** either directional claim fails on a powered panel; access/consent absent means no run. Source: [HYPOTHESIS H4](../../HYPOTHESIS.md).

**H5 — continue offline harness maintenance; park all paid/editor episodes and production rejection.** Reopen only after Board A measurement amendment, issue-31 sandbox prerequisites, proposal-distribution calibration, editor budget/consent and priced sealed scoring. **Sample:** protocol pilot 37 candidates (9 each primary/cheap/random/best-of-N +1 sham); confirmation 30 paired episodes/arm/budget is a proposal requiring pilot power, not funded here. **Pass:** ≥30% fewer real evaluations at equal final quality, with all editor/probe costs and missed-winner coverage reported. **Kill:** failure to reach savings at equal score on a powered study; stop unsafe/unqualified episodes before scoring. Shadow forecasts are not H5 success. Source: [H5_PROTOCOL](../../H5_PROTOCOL.md), [HYPOTHESIS H5](../../HYPOTHESIS.md).

**H6 — park cross-base work.** Reopen after fixed-base signal, pinned accessible models, audited shared coordinates and a separate budget. **Sample:** roadmap screen 100 labelled edits/model on three models, whole-model holdout, ≤20-label calibration separately; re-count source clusters and power before confirmation. **Pass:** retain ρ≥0.4 on the prospectively frozen qualified shared endpoints with positive uncertainty bounds; if fewer than six endpoints qualify, this is an explicitly narrowed amendment, not success of the original ≥6-of-K claim. **Kill:** ρ upper CI<0.2 across qualified endpoints on adequate data, or no coherent coordinate/occupancy mapping. Two Qwen sizes would establish only that pair, not cross-architecture transfer. Sources: [HYPOTHESIS H6](../../HYPOTHESIS.md), [ROADMAP P4b](../../ROADMAP.md), [round1-strategy](round1-strategy.md).

- **If qualification fails:** measurement boundary/bridge and reliability report; no claim of an operational prediction benchmark yet.
- **If H1a fails or is inconclusive:** benchmark/measurement paper with scoped negatives, honest power and near-null coverage; no theorem of unreadable weights.
- **If H1a passes but H1b does not:** benchmark plus within-family teacher-forced readability, with StatNN as the best predictor. No world-model claim.
