## 1. News, endpoints, and the best test

**WeightLoop** changes the H5 *critic contract* only as a guardrail reminder: retain every attempt, reconstruct each accepted patch from the same base, and validate on support disjoint from evaluation. It is an outcome-feedback update loop, not a held-out predictor of benchmark change from a delta; the paper reports offline replay and ranking/discovery outcomes, not LLM benchmarks ([arXiv:2611.07319](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07319)). It does not justify adding transcripts or proposal-selection history to the primary predictor; A5 already excludes rationale and requires complete attempt accounting (`PREREG_H1_TF.md` A5.7; `SYNTHESIS.md` §4.4).

**Neural Thickets** should make scale an explicit effect modifier, not make 0.8B “the wrong place” by extrapolation. Its Qwen2.5 study samples 1,000 Gaussian perturbations/model at σ=0.005 across 0.5B–32B; the measured fraction clearing improvement margins increases with scale. The project’s 0.8B random-direction finding (0/60 beyond KL 0.01) is about a different edit distribution and validity proxy, not the paper’s task-accuracy density (`docs/council/2026-11-10/BRIEF.md`; [arXiv:2603.12228](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12228), §§2.1–2.2). It motivates the small 4B screen below, not a switch of primary model or outcome.

**LoL** is the closest precedent and merits a registered exploratory arm before final hash freeze. It trained on 2,000 Qwen2-1.5B LoRAs and evaluated held-out LoRA networks on 19-source membership, finetuning validation loss, and ARC-C accuracy; the report includes strong held-out ARC-C regression (up to R²=.987) ([arXiv:2410.04207](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04207), §§5.1, 5.3). This differs from our 122 raw-adapter rows (`docs/reviews/P5/d8-regeneration-2026-11-06.md` §2) and from a predecessor-relative change target. Add one GL-equivariant encoder over raw factors, respecting per-layer GL(r) symmetry, as a named secondary beside the field; compare with augmented StatNN, descriptor/norm baselines, and the existing canonical-token/M1 arm. Train only on development folds and hold out complete source components; fit no architecture choice on sealed outcomes. A5’s field remains the sole primary, and any later confirmatory LoL family needs fresh outcomes and multiplicity control (`PREREG_H1_TF.md` A5.4, §§4–5).

No endpoint upgrades follow from the other news: the reported critiques/replications do not supply a powered, held-out Δ-to-our-endpoint test; Neural Field Adaptation studies weight representations for reconstruction/generation rather than benchmark-change prediction ([arXiv:2512.01759](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01759)); and the brief identifies no other 2026 held-out Δ→benchmark predictor (`docs/council/2026-11-10/BRIEF.md`). Keep WikiText as the qualified **mechanism/sensitivity** endpoint (64 × 1,024-token windows); do not relabel it task readability. MMLU gold-letter log-prob remains blocked by qualification, so its H1a status is untested, not a negative (`docs/council/2026-11-07/SYNTHESIS.md` §§1, 3; `docs/reviews/P5/tf-v1-requalification-2026-11-06.md`).

**Best predictor test now:** A5’s base-anchored sensitivity field (gradient/K-FAC contractions) against augmented StatNN, with field-absent, random-field and permuted-field controls, stays primary; the chart stays secondary. The LoL encoder is a useful symmetry-aware secondary because raw factors preserve structure that canonical pooled tokens may lose. Existing P4 evidence is not a general structural-model falsifier: the 342-row/278-cluster rerun reports StatNN 0.56/0.66 and 0.55/0.64, descriptor-only 0.51/0.61 and 0.48/0.61, with neural instantiation drift (median 0.09) (`docs/reviews/P4/d3v2f-summary-2026-11-08.md`). A5’s 40-edit Part A is a local-response check, not a substitute for fresh predictive evaluation.

## 2. LoL power on 122 rows

The closest available H1w simulation used 120 development components at θ=0.15; its bootstrap-t lower 95% power bounds were **0.728 conditional / 0.600 joint**, below the registered 0.80 gate (`docs/reviews/P5/h1w-gate-2026-11-06.md`; `docs/council/2026-11-07/SYNTHESIS.md` §1). Even treating all 122 adapter rows as independent gives only 122/120 = **1.017×** the design size. Under the same variance/effect assumptions, a square-root-size approximation changes θ-equivalent scaling only to 0.15√(122/120) ≈ **0.151**—not evidence of 80% power. This is an illustration, not a LoL power calculation: source clustering can make effective N smaller, and H1w’s target/contrast differs. The correct decision is no powered evaluation claim on these rows; use grouped cross-validation only as exploratory development, then size a fresh test with the registered paired component/item simulation.

The A5 null-size report’s 0.018 ordered-go rate (95% interval 0.0155–0.0208) and two-sided size 0.0372 pass its stated ≤0.07 null-size gate, with a variance×2 scenario at 0.0162 / 0.0347. This is conditional on the registered edit-minus-predecessor proxy and sign-flip DGP, not a measured rotation null or a power pass. The “unavailable audit fails” scenario resolves no pairs; that sensitivity must not be hidden (`nullsize/report.json`, branch `prereg/a5-freeze-evidence`; A5 F2).

## 3. Cheapest scale check and order

Use **20 outcome-blind Gaussian weight perturbations** on Qwen3.5-4B at one fixed registered scale, plus one shared base. Score predecessor-relative mean NLL/log-PPL on the same 64 WikiText windows used for H1w; report each edit’s paired ΔNLL and the improvement fraction, without fitting a predictor. This directly probes density, not LoRA-trained edit effects or a learned predictor. The 4B endpoint needs its own matched identity/repeat and precision check; 0.8B qualification does not transfer. The 20-edit screen is not powered to establish a small density change and cannot kill the world-model line.

Arithmetic (rate extrapolation only): 4B/0.8B = 5; therefore 1.04 min × 5 = **5.2 min/TF-v1 entry**, and 7.5 min × 5 = **37.5 min/fast-tier entry**. Twenty edits plus one common base are 21 entries: 21 × 5.2 / 60 = **1.82 GPU-h TF-v1**; fast-tier would be 21 × 37.5 / 60 = **13.13 GPU-h**. This assumes one shared root reference and linear scaling; it excludes cold load, repeats, 4B qualification and edit construction, and actual throughput may be nonlinear (`docs/council/2026-11-07/SYNTHESIS.md` §3; `docs/reviews/P5/d8-regeneration-2026-11-06.md` §§2, 5).

Run the scale screen **before Part A**, after a dated pre-outcome A5 amendment and the registered freeze/null-size/G2 prerequisites, but before any G3 variant is scored. It can inform whether to spend later budget on larger-model studies; it must not change the 40 selected 0.8B edits, outcomes, or Part A thresholds. Part A then runs exactly as registered: 40 components × three variants = 120 scores; A5.11 lists 24 missing original and 6 unique predecessor references, so (120+30) × 1.04/60 = **2.60 GPU-h** (`docs/prereg/a5_manifests/README.md`; `PREREG_H1_TF.md` A5.3).

## 4. Ranked 30-GPU-h plan and stopping rule

This totals 30.00 GPU-h; the reserve is conditional, not an instruction to spend. The G7 minimum arithmetic is 720 entries × 1.04/60 = 12.48 h plus 2 h overhead; 50 more blocks cost 300 × 1.04/60 = 5.20 h, leaving 2.90 h of reserve for qualification/repeats. A5 caps G7 at 300 blocks and requires a development-only ≥0.80 joint and conditional power lower bound; the H1w 120-component result alone does not establish that gate (`docs/council/2026-11-07/SYNTHESIS.md` §§3–4.3; `PREREG_H1_TF.md` A5.6). G6’s chain census is already recorded as 95/99 long lineages fully scored (512 nodes), so do not repurchase it (`docs/PROGRESS.md`, 7 Nov 22:10 entry).

**Kill versus unresolved.** Part A failure, Part B’s retrospective result, a failed endpoint qualification, or insufficient power is not a scientific falsifier. Stop buying compute if G7 cannot meet its frozen power/precision gates within 300 blocks. Publish the measurement paper as the endpoint-specific final word only after a fresh, qualified, source-closed test has ≥0.80 lower-bound power for conditional and joint procedures and its 95% upper bounds exclude even the registered **5%** useful gain for the field (against augmented StatNN and field-absent controls) and, in a separately powered LoL registration, for LoL; report the intact-versus-rotated response as well. This kills useful incremental predictive benefit only for the tested models/endpoints/population. It does not convert the unqualified MMLU endpoint into a negative or establish universal unreadability (`PREREG_H1_TF.md` §§4–7, A5.4–A5.6).
