## Required edits

1. **Repair Part A's inferential contract — `docs/council/2026-11-07/SYNTHESIS.md:98` (also 116–117).** Specify an exact binomial interval (e.g. two-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson), analysis unit, zero-prediction/tie/missing-pair rules, resolved-pair denominator and one fixed analysis time. For that interval, 15 resolved independent pairs require at least **12/15** correct for lower bound > .5; with 40, at least **27/40**. These are CPU exact-tail calculations, not empirical results. A binomial bound is not justified merely by counting edits: chain/shared trained-source pairs can share a component, all outcomes share windows, and a common field induces additional dependence. Select one pair per certified source component or use an explicitly calibrated clustered/randomization procedure; for an item-population conclusion also account for the common item draw. Do not claim an independent fair-coin null just because orientations are randomized: D is a trained edit, R is random, and a constant-sign degradation predictor may win. Report that comparator and define the null being rejected.

   Define d_ij as the same-window score of W_i+D_i minus W_i+R_i and compute h_pair from its **paired** block bootstrap (32 two-window blocks, common draws across edits/references). Do not use h_D, h_R, their maximum, or the qualification panel's maximum as h_pair; their covariance matters and the shared predecessor cancels. Prefer a frozen zero-excluding paired interval rule over an unspecified half-width for asymmetric intervals. Conditioning on measured resolution changes the estimand to agreement among resolved pairs and can select noise; preserve all 40 in reporting, disclose this estimand, and validate null size with the resolution selection included. An independent resolution panel is an alternative. Existing D3 outcomes were already read: replace “before outcomes” with “before new variant outcomes,” retaining exploratory status.

   **Can 40 yield 15 resolved pairs? Possible, not established.** The h1w report gives max h_D=.01282, panel-mean half-width upper bound .00487 and no h_D>.0244; it gives neither R effects nor their covariance with D, nor the selected 40's contrast distribution. As an illustrative equal-SE normal calculation only, h_D=h_R=.01282 gives h_pair≈.01813 at zero correlation and at most .02564 at correlation −1; these are not validated bounds for the new rotations. Thus those numbers cannot forecast 15 resolvable contrasts, and KL non-null rates cannot supply that forecast. Even under independent equal-resolution Bernoulli trials with probability .375, P(N_resolved≥15)≈.5594, not .80; it takes probability ≈.4286 to reach .80 for the count alone. Simulate both resolution yield and agreement power with development-only contrast/noise assumptions before calling this purchase decisive. Fewer than 15 must be explicitly unresolved; no post hoc replacement/resampling until success. Ladder resolution at s=.25 needs its own denominator/uncertainty and sign convention, and cannot kill a mechanism on unresolved signs.
