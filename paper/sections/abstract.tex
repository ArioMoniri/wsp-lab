Can weight changes predict how a model's measured capabilities change, without running the edited model? Recent work by Takehi et al. reports that a small, targeted edit to Qwen3.5-0.8B can improve a benchmark score without gradients \citep{takehi2026wsp}. That result motivates this question but does not show that effects can be predicted across edits or models. We propose to test whether weight-only features and edit histories predict benchmark changes. We will also test transfer to unseen edit families and compare an LLM reader with a trained predictor. A planned alignment sub-study will compare instruction-tuned, directionally ablated, and Heretic-edited models on capability, refusal, and KL-to-base measures. These outcomes remain unknown; we will test the hypotheses on a controlled edit zoo. The study will focus on prediction and auditing, not gradient-free optimization or a new safety-editing method.
