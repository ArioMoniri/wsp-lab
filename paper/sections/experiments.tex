\section{Experiments}
The subject is Qwen3.5-0.8B with thinking enabled. Fixed subsets cover GSM8K, MATH-500, IFEval, MMLU-Pro and HellaSwag. We also measure WikiText perplexity, refusal on harmful prompts, over-refusal on harmless prompts, and KL to the base. The cap-hit share is the fraction of responses pooled across GSM8K, MATH-500 and IFEval that reach the token limit without EOS (1,024 tokens at the fast tier, 4,096 at the cheap tier). It is a truncation proxy, not a validated detector of semantic repetition. Refusal keyword matching is a surface measure. It does not establish harmfulness.

The fast tier uses 50 GSM8K, 25 MATH-500, 25 IFEval, 100 MMLU-Pro and 100 HellaSwag items, plus 50 prompts each for refusal, over-refusal and KL. Generation is capped at 1024 tokens. At these sizes, a single binomial score has a standard error of 0.03 to 0.10 (Section~\ref{sec:reliability}). GSM8K, MATH-500 and IFEval are therefore reported as descriptive at this tier. MMLU-Pro, HellaSwag, WikiText perplexity and KL are candidate predictor targets, but none is qualified until the criteria in Section~\ref{sec:reliability} are met. Measuring their floors is the first of those steps.

The cheap tier will run on a subset of the fast-tier entries. It uses the larger fixed battery with a 4096-token limit, except for MMLU-Pro, which has a 1024-token limit. A faithful 32768-token tier is reserved for the base and at most 10 selected entries. We plan to start with a clean timing measurement and a stratified production subset, and to extend coverage after that. D3 samples production entries without replacement using seed 0 and sorted IDs, with quotas of 60 random direction, 100 abliteration, 60 LoRA SFT, 30 task vector and 50 chain steps; it takes all available entries if a family falls short. On the manifest snapshot in Table~\ref{tab:zoo-counts}, these quotas resolve to 300 entries. Sampling settings and fixed item IDs are recorded in the evaluation configuration.

Table~\ref{tab:base-repro} places the completed cheap-tier base run beside the base values that the blog reports \citep{takehi2026wsp}. The blog's budgets, sample counts and loop definition differ, so these figures are not matched estimates. The 32k rerun was interrupted when the GPU ran out of memory, and the IFEval difference still needs checker reconciliation.

\subsection{Predictor suite under protocol v2}
\label{sec:v2}
We ran the predictor suite under protocol v2 with three seeds per run. Protocol v2 adds nested model selection, train-only preprocessing, and paired bootstrap intervals over ancestry clusters. The matched set has 345 rows from 389 evaluated entries. The other 44 entries have no matched predecessor target and are excluded. The run files record 260 targets with inferred runtime metadata, backfilled from job logs. The 345 rows are not the 300-entry D3 subset, and they are not the 260 matched D3 edits counted in Section~\ref{sec:reliability}. Twenty-five run directories are complete. The directory \texttt{d3v2-m3-family} holds a configuration and a split plan but no metrics, so it is not reported.

M1 is the stateless tensor-token predictor and M2 is the state-space sequence predictor. M1-matched and M2-matched are matched-input controls. M3 and M6 use JEPA-style objectives, and M5 is a causal trajectory transformer.

Table~\ref{tab:v2-suite} lists the 25 complete rows. Each entry is the mean Spearman over three seeds, with the minimum and maximum over seeds in brackets. The brackets describe training and split variation. They are not confidence intervals. Both targets are fast-tier accuracies scored by option likelihood. Neither is qualified under Section~\ref{sec:reliability}.

In an exploratory three-seed protocol-v2 analysis of matched-reference fast-tier accuracy, trajectory-split M1-matched had mean Spearman .45 and .46 on MMLU-Pro and HellaSwag; seed-0 StatNN had .57 and .65, respectively. Seed ranges describe training/split variation, not confidence intervals. Labels have inferred historical runtime and target qualification is incomplete.

The original H1 criterion was not demonstrated; H2 and H3 remain unresolved, and the suite does not test H4--H6.

Table~\ref{tab:v2-baselines} gives the three baselines. These values come from seed 0 only. They are not three-seed means. Within a split, the baseline values are identical across models, so each split cites one source run. Section~\ref{sec:exploits} examines why the descriptor baseline comes close to StatNN. On seed 0 of the trajectory split, every learned row has a larger MAE than StatNN on both targets, and each paired interval excludes zero. For M1-matched the MAE difference is +0.016 [0.012, 0.021] on MMLU-Pro and +0.011 [0.009, 0.014] on HellaSwag. Across the seven learned rows, all 14 intervals lie above zero. A positive difference means the learned row has the larger error. These paired cluster intervals condition on the fitted models. They do not cover item sampling, training variation, or data provenance.

The five \texttt{m2-holdout} rows are one-fold family-purge runs. Each holds out one edit family and its descendants from training. They are not extra seeds, and they are not M3 transfer runs. Their test sets contain 98, 98, 59, 60 and 30 rows from 98, 45, 59, 60 and 27 clusters. Ancestry and support limits apply to every holdout.

The suite scores eight targets, and it has no KL or WikiText heads. The dashes in the source summary are missing outputs, not negative results. M6 fails its all-seed collapse gate on all three standard splits. That gate requires an effective rank of at least 2 and at least twice the rank of the frozen predictor. The failure does not prove that every fold has rank below 2. M3 has no acceptance gate, so it is not reported as passing, and no M6 LoRA acceptance run exists. H2 is undetermined. On the trajectory split, M5 reaches 0.41 and 0.26, against 0.45 and 0.46 for M1-matched, and the suite does not establish the registered 15\% MAE gain on adequately powered trajectories of length four or more. H3 is undetermined. No qualifying held-out LoRA result exists, and the M2 LoRA holdout is not that test.
