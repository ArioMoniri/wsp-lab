## 0. Corrections the Board asked for

1. **157, not 160, null-family controls.** The fit uses 229 stratum rows plus **157** null-family controls (386 development rows; `null_family_complete: false`): the 160 registered controls minus the three legacy rows excluded in commit 975efc4. The sentence "160 null-family controls" in [the re-qualification report](tf-v1-requalification-2026-11-06.md) (limitation paragraph) and in PREREG A2.3 should be read as 157.
2. **Leave-out fit convention.** The A2.1 (iv) leave-out (θ = 0.0067 on 187 rows, 96 components) drops the rows from the *scored* sums only; the nuisance, StatNN and isotonic were still fit on all 386 development rows, including the dropped ones. The last table below adds the other convention (drop the rows from training and scoring and refit), which gives a different number.
3. **Flexible nuisance as implemented.** PREREG §3 asks for a "training-only cubic spline with knots at development norm quartiles, family interactions". The first reading I tried (truncated-power cubic, fully interacted with family) is numerically unstable (cubic tails: 3 held-out development rows lie outside the training norm range; mean |residual| 0.61 against 0.16), so it is reported but meaningless. The primary flexible nuisance is the **natural (linear-tail) cubic spline** on the training-row quartile knots of log1p(incremental norm), fully interacted with family; a less flexible shared-spline variant with family intercepts and slopes is also shown. This choice was made on stability grounds after the unstable variant failed, and before looking at the stable variant's θ (the choice is disclosed as post hoc).

## Findings in one paragraph

Does the signal survive? **As a point estimate, yes, under every outcome-free reading** (flexible nuisance 0.135, equal-component weights 0.176, high-incremental-norm tertile 0.196, LoRA 0.230, item halves 0.11–0.13 with 20/20 random splits positive), **but the intervals still sit on zero** (flexible nuisance: the six lower bounds are 0.0018, −0.0046, −0.0010, 0.0019, 0.0078, 0.0068, so by the all-six rule it is "not distinguishable from zero"), the signal is absent in task vectors (−0.048, 30/27) and clearly **negative in the lowest-norm chain stratum** (−0.776 [−1.96, −0.09], 42/27). It **does not survive 1/h_i²-weighting** (−0.257) or scoring only the h ≤ δ/2 rows with the all-row fit (0.007): both remove the large-effect rows where the advantage lives (h tracks |y|). It **does survive a full refit without those rows** (0.119 [0.0003, 0.231], 187 rows), so the 0.007 is partly a fit-convention effect, not evidence that the signal is item-variance noise; that variant's lower bound is also at zero. Nothing here lifts the endpoint's status, and the percentile interval remains non-decisive by registration.
