## 2. Gaps found (to be resolved outcome-blind by one dated amendment before any data; flagged to the chair)

- **G1 Bank graph.** Part II §II.4 states r_f 8 / n_blk 6 (48 roots). The frozen manifest's hash-bound
  `bank.blocks` (12 blocks), `bank.component_graph_hash` `c8d50fe2…`, `bank.calibration_components` (12, incl.
  `block:04`, `block:07`) and `bank.closure_allocation` are the v1 graph over the 72 sampled roots (r_f 12,
  n_blk 12): `scripts/wme_manifest_v2b.py` updated `sim.r_f/n_blk` but never regenerated the graph. No rule selects
  8 of 12 roots or 6 of 12 blocks.
- **G2 Reader model.** The two LLM reader arms are "Claude only" with cost lines for Haiku, Sonnet and Opus 5.5; no
  model is registered.
- **G3 R(E) on 9B.** (a) The single registered chart column (`chart:schema:CHART_SCHEMA_VERSION=2`) has no value
  definition on 9B (in the synthetic spec its slot held the dictionary coordinates). (b) `r_of` on 9B needs dense
  kept K-FAC factors for all 64 writers x 6 objectives (about 206 GB) and fp64 eigendecompositions; planner R0
  compute was priced at 0 (`planner_gpu_s` absent). Instance time is authoritative for the cap, so this compute is
  charged.
- **G4 Canary binding.** The `LaunchAttestation` holds one `canary_reference`; the registration binds three engine
  canaries (teacher-forced, HF generate, vLLM).
- **G5 Dead-man price units.** `CombinedLedger.remaining_usd()` uses 4.89 x 1.10 per GPU-h; the dead-man charges the
  provider price (4.89).
- **G6 Freeze gate file.** `wm.bankrun.require_freeze` reads `docs/prereg/wme_freeze.json`, which does not exist.
- **G7 B_par inputs.** The planning B_par values (180.2, 86.72) add the field seconds to a bank price that already
  includes them; the registered text names two measured quantities (bank build; R1 field).
- **G8 Generated safety engine.** §II.12.3 (E1 winner on the c2-gen guard panels, descriptive) names the rule, not
  the engine.

## 3. Engineering architecture (no scientific content)

One controller (this host) owns the single study log (`ProductionGuard`, one serialized call stream). One worker
process on one Lambda `gpu_1x_h100_sxm5` instance executes unit requests from an inbox and writes results to an
outbox; the controller admits a result only for the request id it issued under its current owner epoch and only
through `harness.run_units` -> `guard.succeeded`. Canaries bracket chunks of units. Details in §4 when built.
