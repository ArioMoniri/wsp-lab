# Round 2 — Metrology

## Concessions

The three proposals identify plausible information missing from pooled statistics: predecessor-aligned directions (world-model and representation), and actual module inputs for localized edits (editor). I agree these are worthwhile mechanisms to test, not conclusions licensed by P4. I also accept the editor's distinction between forecasting an effective edit and proving H5 evaluation savings: cached activations are an added information and compute budget, and a shadow archive does not demonstrate savings. Rationale should stay out of the primary predictor; retain it for a separately blinded ablation.

I agree with the world-model proposal that history is not intrinsic when current weights fully determine the evaluated function; H2 can only establish useful information omitted by the compressed current-state representation. I agree with representation that direction-permutation controls are essential, and with editor that every proposal—including rejected and failed attempts—must remain in the denominator.

## Rebuttals and common measurement plan

None of the existing negatives establishes that these proposed structures fail. But the current power and label gates also do not support the proposed positive claims. H1w uses an already-qualified WikiText endpoint, but its estimate is 0.0071 and all six intervals include zero; at a true effect of 0.15, the bootstrap-t power lower 95% bounds are 0.728 conditional and 0.600 joint, below the registered 0.80 gate (`docs/reviews/P5/h1w-gate-2026-11-06.md`). This is inconclusive, not a pass or structural kill. Conversely, MMLU-Pro gold-letter log-prob on 1,000 items is **not qualified**: max item half-width 0.1137 exceeds 0.10 and the tripwire upper bound 0.2264 exceeds 0.20 (`docs/reviews/P5/tf-v1-requalification-2026-11-06.md`). Do not call that endpoint a shared confirmatory target until it passes.

**One shared, gated plan:** use predecessor-relative WikiText log-PPL on 64 disjoint 1,024-token windows as the common mechanism endpoint. It qualified (max half-width 0.01282 vs δ=0.04879; repeat discrepancy 0; reliability lower 95% 0.9923), but requires a fresh, sealed component panel and a new power simulation. Keep MMLU-Pro on 1,000 disjoint items as the task endpoint only after its full item, tripwire, repeat and reliability gates pass. Treat it as blocked today, not interchangeable with WikiText. For all three structures use the same frozen item/window IDs, actual effective delta and predecessor, StatNN + descriptors + delta-norm baselines, and paired per-item losses. Probe/cached-activation-only is an additional baseline for the editor structure; the field must be formed on windows disjoint from scored WikiText outcomes. Fix all feature choices, nuisance fits and seeds on D3; test only on new components. Split by complete source/ancestry component (and by whole editor episode, shared direction and prompt-template group for `llm_edit`); no descendant, alias, or shared proposal source may cross the split. Report H1 results as untested unless the endpoint qualifies and conditional **and** joint TF-specific power lower bounds reach 0.80.

The H1w gate's measured power is for its 120-component development design, not a sample-size formula for these new contrasts. The old H2 calculation is a separate, useful scale check: 162 independent length-≥4 lineages (at least 648 nodes) were estimated for 80% power at a 15% H2 effect, two-sided α=.05; the current contrast has only 18 rows / 10 clusters, and its length-one placebo is 0.1265 [0.039, 0.206] (`docs/council/2026-11-05/round2_metrology_output.txt`). For H1 superiority and the editor's episode contrast, estimate paired source/episode variance on development and size a fresh panel by simulation; do not borrow 162 as their power. At measured brief rates, N scored entries cost approximately N/60 local GPU-hours for TF-v1 (≈1 min/entry) or N/8 for fast tier (7.5 min/entry). The editor's proposed 120-candidate shadow panel therefore costs ≈2.0 GPU-h for TF labels or 15 GPU-h for fast-tier labels, before base references/repeats. Feature extraction adds ≈54 sec/entry (about 1.8 CPU core-hours for 120 at the measured rate), not GPU time; predictor fits are 1–10 CPU min. Cache/probe and sensitivity-field construction costs are unmeasured and must be timed, not hidden in the label budget (brief; `docs/reviews/P5/d8-regeneration-2026-11-06.md`).

## Decisive-test verdicts

| Proposal | Powered and leakage-safe as written? | Smallest repair |
|---|---|---|
| World-model local chart vs orientation controls | **Not powered as proposed; conditionally leakage-safe.** It explicitly says 100 sealed source blocks have unknown power; the historical feature-contrast stand-in needs about 262 clusters for a 15% effect at SD≈0.87 (`docs/council/2026-11-05/SYNTHESIS.md`; the proposal also acknowledges this uncertainty). Whole-source holdout is the right rule, but freeze direction generation and tuning before the sealed set. | Pilot the paired loss variance, then increase *independent* sealed source blocks until the joint simulation clears 80%; 100 is not a confirmatory default. |
| Representation base-anchored sensitivity field vs random/permuted fields | **Not powered or leakage-safe as a decisive test on D3.** D3 outcomes and H1w have already been examined; the 120-component H1w design has only 0.600 joint power lower bound at θ=.15. The proposed field must not use the same WikiText windows as its scored label. | Freeze one field and both destruction controls on development, compute it using outcome-disjoint windows, and compare on fresh source-closed components sized by the same joint/conditional gate. |
| Editor delta × cached-activation critic, 120 candidates | **Not powered; conditionally leakage-safe as a shadow prediction test, not as H5.** Ten held-out episodes cannot be declared powered (the proposal correctly says so). ## H2: what regeneration buys

D8 reports 358 lost deltas, all chain; 62 already had evaluations, leaving **296 never-evaluated entries** to score (`docs/reviews/P5/d8-regeneration-2026-11-06.md`, manifest/sidecar paths `data/zoo/qwen3.5-0.8b/manifest.jsonl` and `artefact_status.jsonl`). The lineage census reports 242 chain rows in 99 ≥4-node roots, but only 36 rows in 16 roots currently have complete production-and-evaluated ancestry (`docs/council/2026-11-05/round2_metrology_output.txt`, section A). If all needed entries are evaluated and every ancestry/feature gate passes, the ceiling becomes **242 rows / 99 lineages**—an increase of 206 rows and 83 lineages, not 358 independent examples. That remains 63 lineages short of the 162-lineage H2 power estimate. At the brief's ≈1 min/entry, evaluating the 296 unevaluated entries costs ≈4.9 local GPU-h, plus ≈4.4 CPU core-hours for feature extraction; ancestor closure and retries can add cost. The regenerated snapshots restore eligibility, not labels, independence, or adequate H2 power.
