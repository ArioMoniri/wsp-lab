# Board C — synthesis review, 2026-11-07

**Verdict: REVISE.** Both proposed encoders are implementable, but the intervention and its cost and interpretation are not specified tightly enough to approve the gates. The work is an architectural proposal, not an established shared mathematical object. Line references below are to the reviewed SYNTHESIS.md.

## Required edits

1. **Lines 40–48, 123–124:** Define the actual chart projectors, shapes, seeds, normalization, masks, and handling of non-matrix tensors. The chart contraction with weights is not the loss Jacobian; a neural response map over it is not automatically a Taylor expansion, nor is a K-FAC Fisher the Hessian. Treat field and chart as competing representations; test predecessor gradients against the base field, not assume equivalence. Specify objective and score sign, averaging, and a strict zero-edit head. Keep full-attention and GatedDeltaNet roles distinct.
2. **Lines 42–45, 58–62, 97–99:** Define the exact state and rounding convention: extraction currently computes effective *base-relative cumulative* chain deltas. Reconstruct actual bf16 predecessor and successor from pinned cumulative snapshots, then subtract their realized weights for the incremental D; do not apply a cumulative delta to a predecessor again. Base displacement and D must use the same state identity. Specify whether field gradients and factors are at base or predecessor; audit actual predecessors. Version/cache all identities and disjoint panel hashes. Define loss versus score orientation and frozen common TF-v1 windows.
3. **Lines 97–98, 125–126:** Specify rotation per tensor and what support means. For D=UΣVᵀ, orthogonal Q_out/Q_in give R=Q_out UΣVᵀQ_inᵀ with identical singular values and rank in exact arithmetic, and preserve changed *tensor names and shapes*, not arbitrary elementwise sparsity or the original row/column subspaces. Constrain rotations to an explicitly selected rectangular coordinate block if required; arbitrary masks cannot generally preserve both spectrum and exact support. Fix seed, matrix distribution, rank tolerance, zero/rank-degenerate cases, non-2D policy, actual predecessor and how rotations are applied before bf16 rounding; audit actual realized support, full spectrum, norm and residuals after rounding. LoRA raw factors permit cheap thin SVD but effective bf16 delta may be full rank; truncate only with a reported residual and matched comparator, or rotate the full realized matrix. Chain cumulative snapshots can be full rank and include heterogeneous/nonmatrix edits: select valid matrix increments, define exclusion/coverage before outcomes and price full SVD, rather than treating them as LoRA. Never claim exact matched effective spectra after rounding without checking.
4. **Lines 97–100, 105:** Replace ≤2 GPU-h as a feasibility assertion with an unmeasured stop-and-reprice allocation, including objective count, sequence lengths, factor coverage, preprocessing, full rotations and predecessor audits. State peak VRAM, CPU/disk bytes, throughput and scoring references as measurements required before G3. K-FAC is an approximation to a specified Fisher, not automatically available for fused/recurrent operators; forbid silent zero factors. Specify what happens if a supported module lacks hooks.
5. **Lines 63–73, 100:** `EditorAPI.preview()` returns aggregate metadata, not the effective tensors or exact coordinate mask, cached writer inputs, predecessor identity, calibrated uncertainty, or an intervention hook. `run_episode` registers valid proposals immediately and records failures; it has no pre-registration critic decision point or scoring loop. Define a trusted adapter between validation and registration, immutable effective-delta snapshot, hashes, all-attempt ledger, cost meter and accept/abstain contract. Ensure rejected/failed attempts are logged even if no valid delta exists; live providers still require production validation. Do not expose rationale to critic. Probe inputs need separate hooks/cache with panel provenance.
6. **Lines 84–87, 98–99:** Register a cheap probe-response comparator now, rather than only after a ladder failure; include probe-only and probe+descriptor and cost. Existing M1 and canonical features merit an identical-fold LoRA-only baseline, not a new generic transformer presented as a decisive alternative. Define paired cluster bootstrap and an inconclusive region, address repeated rotations within source and selection on noisy resolved outcomes, and include original D and predecessor scores in the priced evaluation count.

`stats.py` includes mean, positive fraction, sparsity and cosine as well as norms and top singular values; `pool.py` averages per role/depth and `svd.py` includes projected directions. Consequently v4 is not exactly sign- or rotation-invariant. Exact orthogonal rotation preserves each tensor's singular values, Frobenius norm, rank, relative Frobenius norm against the same predecessor, spectral entropy and top-eight-based stable-rank statistic, as well as tensor-name/type/depth occupancy; it does **not** preserve mean, standard deviation, max, positive/nonzero fractions, cosine to predecessor/new weight, projected directional tokens, or rounding statistics. Pooling preserves invariant components only when membership is unchanged. `svd` uses effective delta, whereas `lora_svd` uses unrounded factors; spectra and feature controls must be audited separately.

## Ranked alternatives and deciding experiment

1. **Probe-response encoder**: highest near-term chance of gain when local Taylor assumptions fail, moderate compute and leakage risk. On the existing G1 rows/folds, cache a small fixed disjoint writer-input panel; compare ΔW x with probe-only, probe+descriptor, augmented StatNN and field/chart. Same paired cluster bootstrap, MAE and rank metric; screen at ≥5% residual MAE gain with lower 95% bound >0 and report full probe cost. Reuse all available rows, group by source; if interval crosses zero or cost is excessive, inconclusive. A small linear ΔW x plus regularized head is cheaper than full encoder and can be the decisive screen for incremental probe value, not a weight-only claim.
2. **Canonical LoRA tokens / LoL**: cheapest existing-feature control for LoRA-only rows, low engineering, modest expected gain because M1 already uses projected directional tokens and labels are scarce. Compare canonical tokens with pooled stats and field on identical source/family folds; paired MAE improvement ≥5% with positive lower 95% bound is a screen, otherwise inconclusive. Rank degeneracy and bf16 effective-vs-raw mismatch matter. [LoL, arXiv:2410.04207](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04207) and [W2T, arXiv:2603.15990](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15990) motivate factorization-aware comparisons, not proof for these chains.
3. **Local typed GMN**: potentially better coordinated channel interactions, higher engineering and overfit risk; hybrid recurrent graph and symmetries are not supplied by this repo. Pilot same rows and splits with a strict 2 GPU-h cap only after a graph-coverage CPU test; require ≥5% paired MAE improvement with positive lower bound over augmented StatNN, plus relabeling equivariance. [GMN, arXiv:2312.04501](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04501) does not establish a Qwen hybrid graph implementation.

Activation patching is a useful causal localization comparator on a small disjoint probe set, not a cheap replacement for all endpoint labels: patch matched predecessor/edited activations at selected writer layers, include sham and probe-only controls, time all forward passes, and compare localized effects with full edited-model responses. It cannot alone establish generalization or weight-only prediction. JEPA/world-model, SSM and larger transformer families add training complexity before the input representation and sample size are resolved; retain as follow-up if the matched baselines show residual learnable signal. Diffusion/flow and TTT have even less direct value for this small supervised prediction test.
