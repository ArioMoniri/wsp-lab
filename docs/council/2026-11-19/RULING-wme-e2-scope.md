# Chair ruling, 19 Nov 2026: H-E2 claim scope after the Board A change-4 sensitivity check

The outcome-blind sensitivity check (`prereg/wme-v2d`, 5944229) found one asymmetric scenario with a true mean
difference of 0 in which the episode sign-flip test has size 0.0715, and the robustness test 0.078. That triggered
the consequence frozen before the run.

**Ruling: accepted.** H-E2 keeps its registered test, α and power (0.828, model-based). If the test rejects, the
paper may report only this:

> "the WM-guided minus StatNN-guided paired differences are not sign-symmetric about zero, in the direction
> favouring WM, conditional on these test items and this edit library"

It may not report a mean-advantage claim.
- The episode mean difference and its interval are reported descriptively.
- No other test is substituted. This ruling is made before any confirmatory outcome exists.
