## 2. A7: WM-CP config and K rule (synthetic data only, frozen)

**Procedure** (`scripts/wme_v2b_sim.py cpsel`, recorded in the manifest as `model.wmcp.selection`):

- **Criterion:** the pooled median of WM-CP's *own* held-out SE-scaled |error| at h = 3–4. A candidate within 2%

| Candidate | Change from the v2 default | Pooled median |
|---|---|---|
| **C0 (picked)** | none (hidden 64, latent 32, k_max 16, s0 = median h = 1 norm) | 3.063 |

| C2 | hidden 128, latent 64 | 3.094 |
| C3 | observation weight 0.1 | 3.027 (within the 2% tie, so C0 is kept) |

**Frozen:** `CPConfig()`, the v2 default. The K rule is K = min(16, max(1, ⌈‖E_T‖/s0⌉)), with s0 = the median
training h = 1 norm. 0.88 CPU-h.

## 3. A7: magnitude calibration (static map only)

**Target:** 2.17 paired SE, the exposed exploratory pilot-2 median refitted-static error at h ≥ 3. The quantity is
the selected static member's held-out SE-scaled |error| at h = 3–4, pooled over all objectives
(`static_errors`). WM-CP enters only through its parameter count, which sizes S3. Nothing was set from WM-CP's
errors.

**Registered rule:** common random numbers. The same 16 calibration replicates (seeds 70,300–70,315) are run at
every κ, and κ* is linearly interpolated between the two grid points nearest the target.

| κ | 0.80 | 0.85 | 0.90 | 0.95 | 1.00 | 1.10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pooled median static error (SE) | **2.172** | 2.271 | 2.349 | 2.439 | 2.517 | 2.727 |

**κ* = 0.799; registered κ = 0.80.**

**Disclosure.** Two earlier exploratory grids used different replicates at each κ (4–12 per κ). Variation between
replicates made their pooled curve non-monotone: κ = 0.93 gave 2.58 on 12 replicates, while κ = 1.00 gave 2.31 on 4.
Their first-crossing value was 0.908. The common-random-numbers grid replaced them before any H-T power quantity
was computed. All grids are in `wme-v2b-calibration-2026-11-17.json`. 14.0 CPU-h in total.

## 4. A7: the alternative's achieved C_T, and the cheap H-T grid

**Calibration-seed refit replicates.** I ran the full refit pipeline at κ = 0.80, cell (16, 6, 12), seeds
70,000–70,017, with 18 replicates per scenario. Its role is to map the registered alternative into the cheap
error model's C_T parameter. It is a measurement of the registered alternative, not a tuning step.

| Scenario | Rejections | Mean Ĉ_T [10%, 50%, 90%] | Median error: static / WM-CP (SE) | Coverage at 0 | Static chosen (90 folds) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **alt** | 0 / 18 | **−0.349** [−0.546, −0.378, −0.154] | 1.99 / 2.70 | — | S1 71, S2 10, S3 9 |
| null_shuffle | 1 / 18 | −0.049 | 3.04 / 3.15 | 0.944 | S2 75 |
| **null_quadratic** | **6 / 18** | −0.097 [−0.350, 0, 0.051] | 0.684 / 0.682 | **0.667** | S2 71, S1 15, S3 4 |

**Reading:**
- **The alternative favours the static map.** The departure saturates the field along each dose direction. The
  least-squares refit of (a, q), S1, absorbs much of that by shrinking the field coefficients, and the inner-fold
  choice picks it in 79% of folds. WM-CP integrates a step operator learned at h ≤ 2 and extrapolates worse
  (median error 2.70 against 1.99). The static median, 1.99, is near its calibrated 2.17: these are different
  replicates and a different static choice per fold.
- **The quadratic-world null fails the size gate:** 6/18 rejections, coverage 0.67. In an exactly quadratic world,
  WM-CP's shrinkage picks λ = 0, so WM-CP *is* the true field. The inner-fold static choice often picks S2, whose
  MLP adds a small fitted residual (noise). Then WM-CP beats static, and the sign-flip test rejects.
  - This is the pipeline's static-selection behaviour, not a property of the alternative. H-T therefore cannot
    pass A7's paired-null gate as registered, whatever the alternative.

**Cheap H-T grid** (156 cells × {null, null_heavy, null_quadratic, alt at C_T = −0.348}; 1,000 replicates, 2,000
flips): **0 of 156 cells pass.** The alternative's power is 0 everywhere. At the selected bank cell (8, 6, 12, 48;
m = 18): alt 0.000; nulls 0.001 / 0.002 / 0.001.

**Limitation of the cheap model.** It represents `null_quadratic` as exchangeable errors at a quarter of the
static scale, so it does not reproduce the full pipeline's quadratic-null size failure. The full refit (§6) is the
gate.

## 10. Flags for the chair (registration questions; nothing was changed)

1. **H-T's paired quadratic null fails in the full pipeline, independently of the alternative.**
   - In an exact quadratic world, WM-CP (λ = 0) is the true field, while the inner-fold static choice often picks
     S2, whose MLP fits a little noise. WM-CP then "wins": 6/18 rejections, coverage 0.67.
   - Two options for a dated amendment: put S1 (the exact field refit) first in the static order whenever it ties
     the error, or require the static member's shrinkage to select λ = 0 the way WM-CP's does.
2. **Under the registered A7 alternative, the static map wins** (C_T −0.35). The least-squares refit of (a, q) (S1)
   absorbs the saturation; integrating a short-path step operator does not. The magnitude is the only calibrated
   quantity and was not tuned for power.
3. **The generation batch is part of the registered decoding.** Greedy text changes with the batch size (§5).
   Batch 128 is frozen in the manifest; the PREREG author should state it.
4. **The bank design is E-driven now.** The bank r_f 8 is the cheapest that powers E1 and E2. H-T runs on it as a
   labelled descriptive analysis, with m = 18 held-out components.
