**Safety boundary, binding:** fictitious persons and synthetic medical records only; no real personal data; no attempt to
extract real people's data from any model. Unlearning is evaluated defensively.

## BLUF

1. **Neither idea is a world-model problem by default.** First-order saliency (magnitude, Wanda ≡ our q_A up to a
   factor, OBS/SparseGPT, Fisher a + q) already predicts deletion damage with nothing fitted. All three seats agree.
2. **But the test itself is open.** No fetched LLM paper reports how well saliency *ranks* masks by realised damage
   (literature). A within-sparsity ranking study is new even if the answer is "saliency suffices".
3. **Mandatory baselines.** Pruning: a fitted first-order stack (Wanda/q_A, a + q, ‖W⊙M‖², sparsity). Unlearning:
   random, forget-minus-retain saliency and layer-only sets at equal size and update norm, plus the pooled layer profile.
4. **Localisation is a weak prior.** Causal tracing does not pick the best layer to edit (Hase et al.); the only reported
   benefit of mechanism-level localisation is resistance to relearning (Guo et al.).
5. **Unlearning mostly suppresses.** Relearning, 4-bit quantisation (about 21 % → 83 % retained), probes and
   pre/post logit differences recover "forgotten" content. Claims are limited to "suppressed under the listed attacks".
6. **Model: Qwen3.5-0.8B for both studies,** local, $0. MedGemma is excluded (post-norm cancellation, 13.5 % batch-flip
   floor, HAI-DEF); 9B belongs to C1.
7. **Two exploratory studies, P1 (pruning) then U1 (unlearning),** each with its own pre-registration, pilot and kill
   rules. P1 ≈ 7 GPU-h (cap 8); U1 ≈ 30 GPU-h (cap 36, an estimate a pilot re-prices).
8. **Nothing runs without the user's approval of each pre-registration.** No Lambda spend.

## Rulings

**R1. Corrections to the challenger.**
- "r13 proves / dooms learned pruning prediction" is too strong. r13 found the *9B W2 bank* varied along one dose axis
  (r = 0.996). A mask bank can be built to vary within sparsity by construction (per-layer allocation, criterion,
  module class), which is the condition r13 R4(ii) asked for. Whether a learner then adds anything is the open question.
- The challenger's 24-mask gate with Δτ ≥ 0.15 at p < 0.01 is underpowered. The methods seat's Monte Carlo needs about
  120 held-out masks for power 0.81 at Δτ ≈ 0.10. The 24-mask panel is kept only as the P1 pilot (16–24 masks).
- The challenger's E4 figure (rule–judge agreement 0.66–0.74) is correct for the original audit; the paired re-label
  gave 0.75–0.78. Both show rule-based scoring is unfit, which is why U1 uses relearning, quantisation and probes.

**R2. P1, prunability ranking (exploratory, Qwen3.5-0.8B).**
- **Units:** 256 unstructured masks at 30 % and 50 % sparsity (128 fit, 128 held out), varying per-layer allocation,
  criterion (Wanda, one-shot SparseGPT, Fisher, random) and module class. Count-matched random masks are the null.
- **Ground truth:** realised WikiText ΔNLL **and** a knowledge endpoint declared before scoring (literature: knowledge
  drops at 25–30 % while perplexity holds). Separate τ per endpoint.
- **Primary:** held-out within-sparsity Kendall τ, learned ranker minus the first-order stack, cluster bootstrap and a
  within-sparsity permutation null.
- **Kills:** stack already at τ ≥ 0.90 (report saliency only); lower bound of Δτ ≤ 0 (close WM pruning); pilot overrun
  of 1.5× (stop and re-price).
- **New code:** Wanda/OBS/SparseGPT ports for the Qwen3.5 hybrid (no fetched repository lists it), mask generator.
  Reused: Fisher fields, R0 guard, provenance, canaries.

**R3. U1, predicted targeted unlearning (exploratory, Qwen3.5-0.8B).**
- **Data:** 200 fictitious persons × 20 attributes (10 biographical, 10 synthetic medical), generated by us, inserted
  by full fine-tuning on a local copy (LoRA insertion would reveal the location). TOFU's MIT-licensed fictitious authors
  are the second set and the fallback.
- **Gate U-0:** insertion verified on forget QA, and one TOFU NPO run reproduced, before any unlearning.
- **Episodes:** 40 held-out persons, forgotten one per episode by masked NPO at equal weight count and equal update norm.
- **Arms:** predicted set; uniform random; forget-minus-retain saliency; layer-only; unmasked NPO and RMU references;
  task-vector negation as an *oracle* arm only (it reads the insertion delta).
- **Forget battery on every arm:** forget QA and paraphrases, benign relearning, 4/8-bit quantisation, linear probe,
  pre/post logit difference. Retain: neighbour persons and general benchmarks at matched retain loss.
- **Primary:** relearning-resistant forget score, predicted vs saliency-matched, paired and Holm-corrected.
- **Kills:** insertion fails (TOFU only); predicted < saliency-matched + 5 pp on dev (stop, report the negative);
  relearning restores ≥ 80 % in every arm (report suppression only, no localisation claim).

**R4. Wording and release.** Reports say "suppressed under the listed attacks", never "forgotten" or a privacy
guarantee. Pre- and post-unlearning weights are never released together. Synthetic data generation is audited before
insertion; any non-synthetic record aborts the study.

**R5. Order.** C1 keeps priority. P1 and U1 use only the local GPU (≤ 16 GB, niced) and touch nothing on Lambda, so P1
may run alongside C1 once its pre-registration is frozen and approved. U1 starts after P1's pilot.
