## 1. What changes before freeze

**WeightLoop: amend H5’s acceptance contract.** The [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.07319) edits 128 output weights of a roughly 3.6M-parameter predictor with a fixed program. Support-training diagnoses edits; support-validation chooses settings; the accepted rule is reconstructed on all support from theta_0. Patches never compound. Permutation controls preserve change count/norm and receive equal validation opportunities. Crucially, the abstract reports seven of eight ranking settings improved against the base, **all eight against random edits**, not seven against random. These are assay-ranking results, not LLM forecasting evidence.

My proposed transfer is an engineering contract: immutable base and candidate hashes; separate proposal-training support, support-validation, calibration and sealed test; fallback to base on no qualifying validation gain; reload verification; complete attempt ledger. After rebuilding on all support, score a further untouched acceptance slice before deployment, since the rebuilt tensor differs from the fold-tested tensor. Reused validation is adaptive development, never fresh evidence. Permute updates within architecture-compatible coordinates with the same tuning budget; distinguish this control from permuting critic features. The learned critic ranks candidates and can abstain; it cannot certify improvement in place of measurement. Existing base-relative delta semantics already fit this contract (`docs/H5_DESIGN.md`).

**Neural Thickets: amend random-search controls, not the primary hypothesis.** [Gan and Isola](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12228) sample perturbations, select top-K on training data and ensemble predictions. Improvement density rises with scale; their displayed experiments include 5,000 guesses, so “hundreds” is not a universal budget. This does not establish density for Qwen3.5-0.8B, our perturbation distribution or WikiText. Low density makes our setting difficult, not scientifically invalid.

Random best-of-N is mandatory for claims of editor/search advantage, not for every held-out prediction contrast. Propose **32 independent Gaussian draws per each of ten G5 blocks**, matched to effective support and norm, fixed seeds, and nested N=1/8/32 development-selection curves. Preserve original six G5 arms. Match editor opportunities at each N and report equal-GPU comparisons too; an unmatched editor gets no superiority claim. This is a feasibility budget, not a powered demonstration of absent random winners: zero successes in 32 iid draws has a one-sided 95% binomial upper bound about 8.9%, conditional on that block. Freeze scale selection before testing. Single-model K=1 is primary; an optional K=4 ensemble needs matched four-pass baselines and separate scoring costs, so defer it. Do not label K=1 a full RandOpt replication.

**LoL: new registered secondary.** [Learning on LoRAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04207) is direct precedent for downstream-property prediction from low-rank factors. Add a compact GL-equivariant factor encoder with invariant pooling on the authentic-factor subset, sharing folds and endpoints with the field. Test invariance under A→AR, B→R⁻¹B and compare with effective-product features. Missing original factors cannot silently become synthetic “raw LoRAs.” Its larger training collections do not guarantee a gain here. Keep the field primary.

**Critiques: amend treatment manifests and comparisons.** [Honnibal](https://honnibal.dev/blog/astra-read-write-misleading) separates small raw-number experiments from Python-assisted LM work; [Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/3/qwen-can-interpret-model-weights/) explicitly separates those questions and replicates game identification, not Qwen editing. The [original blog](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/) reports no established Gemma improvement and attributes Qwen gains to loop repair. Its approximately sixfold aggregate ratio does not provide a paired causal estimate against a competitive non-thinking baseline. I did not locate a directly verifiable González/Polaris primary thread; that attribution remains unverified, rather than importing the brief’s wording as evidence. The fetched [MindStudio discussion](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/weight-space-programming-overhyped-debate) is commentary, not primary experimental evidence.

G5 must record `python_tool_access`, `forward_access`, `hidden_state_access`, `feedback_access`, actual calls, code hash, decode configuration and costs. Python algebra without inference differs from running the model. Hold access fixed across critic/no-critic arms; any no-tool treatment is separately registered. G5 scripted edits are not live-model rediscovery. For eventual generation claims pair items/seeds and compare edited/base under thinking-on and thinking-off settings; report loop hits and accuracy separately. Gemma is a negative-control experiment, not an assumed null.

The [csinva repository](https://github.com/csinva/weight-space-programming-autoresearch) currently presents executable search and systematic weight-reading tests; its evolving README should not be quoted as a timeless universal impossibility result. The [MaceTenth repository](https://github.com/MaceTenth/weight-space-programming-replication) reports small-network replication and architecture-dependent weight-block audits, which do not replicate Qwen math editing. Pin repository revisions before using their artifacts. Neither supplies our held-out delta-to-benchmark validation.

**Items 5–6: no primary-programme change.** [Neural field adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01759) supports base/low-rank structure for reconstruction and generation, not our endpoint. [Proxy forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18607) motivates a behavioral comparator; [KNOW](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05059) predicts weights rather than edit effects. These examples do not establish an exhaustive absence of relevant 2026 work.

## 2. Best critic now

Use a **regularized support-conditioned residual predictor**. Inputs: effective bf16 delta, exact tensor/column support, rounding loss, pinned base/predecessor, operation descriptors; per-objective signed gradient contractions; K-FAC magnitude; chart contractions; cached post-gating activation responses for supported MLP writers. Exclude rationale. Treat Fisher as trust-region context, not signed Hessian curvature. Unknown modules or changed predecessors without a valid field trigger abstention.

Architecture: objective-specific ridge residual heads over fixed module summaries, with zero-delta centering; a shallow tree residual head is a development-only comparator. Train with whole source/ancestry/episode groups held out, fold-local scaling and regularization, and identical folds for augmented StatNN, field-only, chart-only, activation-only and correspondence-destroyed arms. Do not train a large token network on the 342 matched rows; 1,252 feature sets and 694 TF records are not that many independent supervised examples (`BRIEF.md`, linked P4/P5 reports). Select hyperparameters only within development folds.

Predict signed item-score changes, aggregate WikiText NLL change and, only after qualification, task gain/collateral loss and probability of satisfying a frozen goal. Calibrate uncertainty on separate whole episodes, with shared item resampling. Reject only if the utility upper bound cannot qualify and the one-sided missed-winner bound is certified; otherwise evaluate. G5 rejects nothing: its two calibration/two test blocks cannot certify a ≤5% rejection-error bound (`../2026-11-07/SYNTHESIS.md`, G5).

Expected gain is uncertain; direction/support sensitivity offers a mechanism beyond norms, not a numeric forecast. Use existing field/rotation/chart/intervention code (`BRIEF.md`, “Code on main”). Build the external broker, calibration head, validation/rebuild ledger and LoL adapter. `driver.py::run_episode` still calls `validate_production` except for exact `ScriptedEditor`; `api.py` supplies bounded probes/delta capture, not a learned critic. Transcript hash/shape summaries are not activation caches (`docs/H5_DESIGN.md`). Keep production disabled.

Registered tests remain A5: Part A rotation/ladder ordered decision, Part B exploratory incremental contrast C against augmented StatNN and both destruction controls, then separately powered G7. No chart/LoL promotion after exposure. H5 ultimately needs G8’s joint ≥30% evaluation-savings lower bound, final-score lower bound ≥−1 point, rejection upper bound ≤5%, guardrails and positive net GPU savings—not merely predictive rank (`../2026-11-07/SYNTHESIS.md`, §4.4).
