# Round 2: JEPA and weight-space dynamics

## Answers to the council

> Features CTO: “does a JEPA objective on cumulative deltas learn anything beyond ancestry/family and edit magnitude at this sample size; which held-out perturbation test would falsify that?”

We do not know that it does. M3's trajectory-split ρ is 0.15/0.11 versus StatNN 0.57/0.65 for MMLU-Pro/HellaSwag ([brief](BRIEF.md)); neither result isolates representation information. Freeze ancestry-disjoint folds and a held-out family-and-norm-matched set of edit directions, with actual perturbations and measured effects. Compare a frozen JEPA encoder plus small head to an identical supervised encoder, descriptor plus norm, and StatNN, with equal inputs and tuning. Test incremental and cumulative features separately and together. If JEPA has no positive paired cluster-bootstrap MAE advantage on qualified within-family residuals, or its advantage survives scrambling direction while preserving family, norm and support, the proposed direction-sensitive interpretation fails. This needs a real matched intervention panel; current observational data cannot supply that counterfactual. Failure to beat supervised controls also ends the practical pretraining claim; it does not prove the representation encodes nothing.

> Metrology CTO: “Will you train and score on family-residual targets (Δ minus family × device mean) and make that the headline? Pooled ρ on these targets is mostly family offsets.”

Yes, with a leakage qualification: estimate family × device means on each training fold only, apply them to train and test, and report paired residual MAE and centered rank along with uncentered scores and sample counts. For an unseen family the family mean is unavailable; use a predeclared training-only global fallback and report that separately, never a test-family mean in a deployable prediction. For explanatory within-family evaluation, a test-family-centered descriptive score may be shown explicitly as such. Family explains η² 0.21–0.65 in the continuous fields, and 94/300 are non-null ([metrology](round1-metrology.md), [budget output](metrology_budget_output.txt)). That is not proof all pooled rank comes from offsets: quantify the paired incremental result. Use TF-v1 continuous measures and independent item panels; repeatability 0.98–1.00 is conditional on the panel and does not establish resolution of small effects. Generated GSM8K, MATH500 and IFEval at this tier are not primary endpoints.

> Weight-reading researcher: “What observed low-dimensional, smoothly controlled latent parameter indexes this zoo, analogous to the physical control parameters in JEPAWG, and what falsifier distinguishes learning that structure from classifying edit families?”

None has been established. Within an edit family, strength or training step is an observed candidate control, not a demonstrated common latent coordinate; categorical task and edit identity are not a continuous physical manifold. The physical parameter-to-trained-weight setup in [Bifurcations from Weight Space](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30847) does not identify a comparable variable here ([round 1](round1-jepawg.md)). Predeclare a strength grid with replicated independent directions and matched support, hold out intermediate strengths and entire source runs, and compare interpolation of measured predecessor-relative TF effects and weight features against family × norm splines. A JEPA must improve held-out residual prediction and preserve strength ordering within family; failure, or success reproduced by family/norm-only controls, falsifies the claimed additional structure on that panel. It cannot establish a universal manifold.

> Strategy CTO: “what is our analogue of a known physical parameter manifold, and which observable would falsify that analogue? Would task/dataset/step-indexed genuine training runs support a prospective JEPA-vs-supervised comparison, or are we importing the architecture without its source of identifiability?”

A controlled step or hyperparameter grid within a fixed training protocol is a candidate, with measured checkpoint weights and predecessor-relative TF effects as observables; task/dataset are discrete conditions, not a single smooth axis. Held-out run interpolation failing against the spline and supervised controls would falsify the practical analogue for that grid. Genuine runs make a prospective comparison possible, but do not themselves confer identifiability: independent runs, deliberate parameter variation and held-out tasks are required. Do not advertise it as JEPAWG replication. Round 1 verified the papers' methods and limitations ([round1-jepawg.md](round1-jepawg.md)); the weight-reading audit found no blog demonstration of Δ-only benchmark prediction ([round1-weight-reading.md](round1-weight-reading.md)).

## Challenges and concessions

1. Strategy proposes “48-run/240-checkpoint SFT core” and a JEPA transfer target of “ρ≥.5” ([strategy](round1-strategy.md)). Forty-eight runs are the independent units, not 240; holding out tasks reduces them further. No measured power establishes that this panel can resolve either transfer or 15% MAE improvement. Gate collection on the eight-run pilot's per-run variance and a cluster-level simulation with an explicit minimum detectable effect; report a feasibility bound if underpowered. I concede strategy's central correction: actual sequential training and complete run holdout are necessary for its H2 question, and equal full state and edit leave no fundamental path dependence.
2. Features proposes “1,000+ per family for a directional token encoder” and suggests masked/contrastive pretraining ([features](round1-features.md)). That is an unvalidated planning threshold, and more same-family null or correlated edits need not improve conditional signal. First demonstrate a matched-direction intervention with resolvable effects and a learning curve at independent-run level. I concede pooling discards directions and exact layers; M3's failure cannot distinguish a bad objective from missing inputs.
3. My round-1 “geometry and conditional history audit” and pretraining as a top-ranked experiment overvalued latent structure. Descriptor-only ρ 0.524/0.614 versus StatNN 0.566/0.649 on the same seed-0 trajectory folds, but 0.493 versus 0.698 for KL, makes the case target-dependent ([importance evidence](evidence-statnn-importances.md), [CSV](evidence-statnn-importances.csv)). These are predictive comparisons, not a causal decomposition. HellaSwag random_dir is identically zero and abliteration is mostly tiny ([same evidence](evidence-statnn-importances.md)); geometry can be learned without learning useful behavior. Weight-reading is right to put cached exact-layer ablation first.

## Revised model decision

Retire M2, M5 and M3 as current-scale contenders and stop architecture tuning. Report their tested negative results as conditional on the zoo, compressed representations and labels; M5 must not be described as empirically failed unless its actual results support that statement. H2 remains unresolved, not disproved. A future history experiment is conditional on qualified variation and power, not a priority purchase: 48 independent genuine training runs × five measured states (including initial state), four predecessor-relative TF-v1 transitions per run; reserve complete runs and tasks prospectively. Compare identical incremental edit plus cumulative predecessor features in a stateless descriptor/StatNN and capacity-matched neural model against M2 or M5 with true history, dropped history, and within-run shuffled history; include lag/trend and train-fold family/device controls. Report paired cluster-bootstrap MAE and the preregistered 15% reduction, plus item-panel uncertainty. A pilot-based power simulation must justify 48 or stop before full collection; 48 is a proposed cap, not a power calculation. Assuming 240 new evaluated states, full fast-tier upper proxy is 30 local GPU-h or 12.4 effective H100 GPU-h, plus 4.4 GPU-h extraction, training and reference/repeat costs; actual TF-only throughput and training cost are unmeasured ([brief](BRIEF.md)). The strategy pilot budgets 56 batteries at 7 local GPU-h plus 0.587 h extraction and capped training/measurement/model work ([strategy](round1-strategy.md)); do not add this pilot twice if included in the core.

Pretraining on 1,235 entries might learn base-specific geometry, but a norm/family reconstruction shortcut could dominate; unlabeled checkpoints are neither independent families nor behavioral labels. Only reconsider after the matched-direction panel shows residual signal, using fold-scoped ancestry-excluded pretraining, supervised same-input and PCA controls, and equal tuning. A full-zoo transductive arm is separately labeled. No current evidence warrants expecting an improvement; extra extraction if uncached is approximately 22.6 GPU-h at 66 seconds per entry ([brief](BRIEF.md)).

## Ranked actions for the chair

1. **Metrology — qualify TF-v1 and residual signal.** Use existing panel and train-fold family × device baselines; estimate within-family effects and independent-item uncertainty. Cost: CPU analysis; new panel throughput must be measured. Prediction: at least one endpoint has reproducible non-null within-family variation. Kill: intervals and resolution do not distinguish useful effects from zero; do not scale predictors.
2. **Weight-reading / features — cached exact-layer StatNN ablation.** Compare with pooled stats, descriptor and norm on identical ancestry folds and family residuals. Cost: cached extraction, predictor cap 0.5 GPU-h ([weight-reading](round1-weight-reading.md)). Prediction: ≥5% paired MAE gain on a qualified endpoint with positive cluster interval. Kill: no gain with adequate feature variation; do not claim pooling alone caused failure.
3. **Features / metrology — matched orientation panel.** Hold support, norm and spectrum fixed while changing orientation relative to base; measure predecessor-relative TF effects and compare directional features with descriptors. Cost: first measure creation and TF throughput; reserve no more than strategy's 6 GPU-h measurement expansion cap until priced. Prediction: replicated effect differences exceed item uncertainty and directional features improve paired MAE ≥5%. Kill: no resolvable effect or no held-out benefit; no directional encoder scaling.
4. **Strategy / metrology — genuine-run feasibility pilot.** Eight independent runs, four checkpoints each, as specified in strategy's 19.59 local GPU-h two-week allocation, including 56 evaluation batteries and capped training and measurement. Prediction: pilot variance permits a cluster-level simulation to resolve 15% MAE at an affordable run count. Kill: cost cap exceeded, insufficient target range, or simulated power inadequate; publish feasibility instead of expanding.
5. **JEPA / modeling — conditional history test only after action 4.** Use the 48-run design above, prospective power gate and matched ablations. Prediction: true history reduces held-out MAE ≥15% with positive paired cluster interval and beats shuffle/drop. Kill: gate fails or effect misses the bar; retain the negative conditional result and stop M2/M5/M3 development.
