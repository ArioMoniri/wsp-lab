### Implementation choices, fixed in code before any pilot-2 data

The ruling leaves these points to the implementation. Each was committed in `1b49d03` before the first 9B pilot-2
job.

1. **T2's six objectives.** These are the pilot-1 six, with `sycophancy_real` in place of the MMLU-Pro proxy:
   - Ruling 2(c): "Pilot 2 uses a real sycophancy dataset. The proxy is kept as a descriptive endpoint."
   - The proxy is scored on every T2 state as a descriptive endpoint.
2. **The "−1 pp equivalent on their log-probability scale" guardrail.** Each guarded log-probability objective is
   mapped per item to the probability it implies, and the paired change of that probability is bounded at 1 pp
   (`panel2.pp_value`):
   - gold log-probabilities → exp(value);
   - refusal margins → sigmoid(margin);
   - sycophancy → p_neutral − p_suggested.

   The bounds:
   - higher-is-better objectives: LCB ≥ −0.01;
   - lower-is-better objectives (harmless refusal, sycophancy): UCB ≤ +0.01;
   - WikiText: ΔNLL UCB ≤ 0.01 nats/token.

   The objective itself and the descriptive proxy are not guardrails.
3. **proj_remove dosing.** α_eff = dose × 0.5, capped at α_eff ≤ 1, as the ruling's text says. The cap applies to
   the positive side only. The +4 dose (α 2 → 1) would be the same weights as +2, so it is not visited again.
4. **Stop rule.** Each signed side is walked outward. A state with paired WikiText ΔNLL > 1.0 would be kept as a
   labelled endpoint and would end that side. **No state reached it** (§3).
5. **Fresh roots** (seed namespace `wme-p2`; layers 12–15 are the support of a freshly collected base field):
   - **scale:** factor 1.5 on `down15` and `o13`. Pilot-1's `down13` and `o14` were excluded.
   - **steer:** rank-one compiled shifts of 0.125 × the residual norm, using new contrasts at layers chosen by the
     seed:
     - `syc@15`: suggested-wrong minus neutral sycophancy prompts;
     - `trivia@13`: neutral trivia prompts minus harmless prompts.
   - **proj_remove:** α 0.5, bundled over `down12–15`, with the contrasts `gsmwiki` (GSM8K − WikiText) and
     `mathcomp` (competition math − harmless).
   - **Negative control:** a random direction from `seed_for("random-control")`.
   - No pilot-1 root, direction or seed was reused.
6. **Compositions.**
   - Eight 3-step sequences with one root per family, then each reverse: 16 in total.
   - Four 4-step sequences covering all families, then each reverse: 8 in total.
   - Each step is the root at dose 1, applied sequentially. The control is never composed.
   - Prefixes are unique states: 47 composition states.
7. **Go-T2 aggregate.** This is the pilot-1 code's aggregate:
   - F3u is refitted per objective by least squares over all ladder states (y = c₁a + c₂q).
   - The endpoint is the pooled median of |observed − F3u| / paired SE over every (composition state with h ≥ 3,
     objective) pair.
8. **Go-R2.**
   - The trapezoid is ½(⟨g(θ_{t−1}), D_t⟩ + ⟨g(θ_t), D_t⟩), which is pilot-1's `oracle_trapezoid`.
   - A0 is the static per-step F(E_t) − F(E_{t−1}).
   - The rule is applied over composition steps with h ≥ 2. Errors are scaled by step SE and pooled.
   - Oracle gradients cover all 53 trajectory states. They were contracted straight away and then deleted.
9. **E2 candidates.** Each objective gets 32 candidates:
   - its own LoRA at λ ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 1};
   - 29 single steering actions, drawn without replacement (seed `wme-p2:e2:<objective>`) from 48 primitives:
     - layers {20, 23, 26, 29};
     - directions {syc, gsm, mathcomp};
     - signed shift ±{0.03125, 0.0625} × the residual norm.

   An evaluation shared by two objectives' candidate lists is run once: 55 unique dev evaluations.
10. **LoRA SFT.**
    - Rank 8, α 16, on every layer's residual writers (`down_proj` and the mixer output).
    - AdamW at lr 1e-4, 2 epochs, 8 rows per step, 10 warm-up steps, gradient checkpointing.
    - Data: 1,024 construction items, each the panel's own teacher-forced target row. Sycophancy trains the
      suggested-wrong row.
    - The trainer refuses any non-construction item, checked against the manifest's hashes.
