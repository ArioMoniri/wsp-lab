## Q4: evidence ledger—what improved, on which models, under which evaluation?

**Loop repair and direct WSP.** [Sparks, experiment 8](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/) reports Qwen3.5-0.8B with thinking enabled: removing an MLP output direction improved held-out MATH-500 7.9→48.1%, GSM8K 13.8→67.1%, and IFEval 21.0→62.3%. Discovery used training problems; evaluation sets were unavailable during editing. Token-limit hits fell from 93.0% to 33.1%. This is an author research report, not an independent replication. Its non-looping Gemma experiment did not establish improvement. Our non-thinking 9B configuration therefore needs a generation diagnosis before copying this mechanism.

[Exploring WSP, Table 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.09382v1) is more directly relevant: Qwen3.5-9B retained edits show held-out IFEval +1.85 pp, SciBench +3.03 pp, and SciCode +1.54 pp. The 9B math candidate’s +29.69 pp development gain became −0.35 pp held out, and the original checkpoint was retained. These are reported benchmark gains, not our paired-confidence/guardrail-qualified E1. The study supports instruction following/scientific problem solving as candidates, not assuming arbitrary math headroom or transferring its decoding regime.

**LASER and deletion.** [LASER](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.13558v1) replaces selected matrices by truncated-SVD approximations, predominantly late MLP matrices. GPT-J CounterFact top-1 rises 13.3→24.1%; the study also evaluates Llama-2 and RoBERTa across factual/reasoning datasets including HotpotQA, FEVER, and BIG-Bench tasks. Hyperparameters use a 20% validation partition with remaining test data, sometimes repartitioning original benchmark splits. Thus “held out” means held out from edit selection, not necessarily from pretraining. PILE perplexity worsens. Appendix D additionally demonstrates magnitude-pruning gains on GPT-J CounterFact. Neither establishes improvement on Qwen3.5-9B instruct. Treat spectral edits as a bounded secondary generator, and retain general-text guardrails.

**Task arithmetic/merging.** [Task arithmetic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.04089) supplies signed fine-tuning deltas; it is foundational rather than direct evidence for current instruct models. [DARE, Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.03099v3) merges homologous Llama-2-based instruction/math/code checkpoints, dropping and rescaling **delta** parameters. Task arithmetic plus DARE merges WizardLM/WizardMath to GSM8K 66.26 versus WizardMath 64.22, while MATH falls 14.02→12.86. Three-way TIES+DARE reaches AlpacaEval 72.50 versus WizardLM 67.20. HumanEval and MBPP are also evaluated, with substantial tradeoffs. These benchmark comparisons are not a fresh contamination-controlled confirmation. A shared architecture alone is insufficient: use the identical initialization/tokenizer, construction-only donor training, and compare against the strongest donor, not only the weak starting checkpoint. DARE is not evidence that pruning the original dense weights improves capability.

**Contrastive weight steering (CWS).** [Steering Language Models with Weight Arithmetic](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.05408v2) forms D=θ_positive−θ_negative from opposing fine-tunes. Its default Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct improves resistance to misleading user cues on OOD TruthfulQA/TriviaQA open-generation questions. It measures content correctness, rather than merely agreeable tone. Separate weaker-model experiments preserve learned GCD or GSM8K gains while reducing sycophancy or restoring appropriate refusal. Therefore prioritize content-based factual robustness; do not advertise CWS as demonstrated 9B math enhancement. The paper’s rank-32 all-module LoRA also differs from our proposed rank-8 writer-only budget.

**TFTV.** [Training-Free Task Vectors](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09054v3) maps contrastive activations and mean module inputs into rank-one weight updates; main models are Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct. Persona Vectors trait scores measure behavioral control; MMLU/GSM8K mostly measure preservation. Appendix F’s OOD Qwen tests give TruthfulQA MC1 47.98→52.75 and MC2 64.69→69.04 under hallucination-direction negation, plus Moral Stories 48.74→53.29. NLP/philosophy sycophancy improve, politics does not. This supports truthfulness as a candidate objective. Trait suppression sometimes damages GSM8K, and coefficient selection uses trait/coherence criteria: OOD transfer is stronger evidence than tuned in-domain scores, but neither certifies our guardrails. Map only audited output projections; do not assume attention results transfer to DeltaNet internals.

**Test-time/RL editing.** [TTRL](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.16084v2) updates weights using majority-vote pseudo-rewards on unlabeled target prompts; benchmarks include AIME24, AMC, MATH-500 and GPQA-Diamond. Its headline gains are transductive, not sealed-item holdouts. Cross-benchmark transfer on Qwen2.5-Math-7B is more relevant but is not modern-instruct-only evidence. [TTC-RL](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04786v1) includes Qwen3-8B and Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507, with improvements on math and code benchmarks including AIME, MATH-500, CodeElo and LiveCodeBench v6. It retrieves verifiable training tasks for target tasks, and reports the highest smoothed test performance across checkpoints. That selection protocol is weaker than our single sealed evaluation. Adapt its generator to construction-only RL and freeze checkpoints on development; label this a protocol change, not a reproduction of headline numbers.

**Pruning evidence filter.** [ShortGPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03853) primarily demonstrates compression relative to other pruning methods. [CAP](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19350v1) studies Llama-3-8B-Instruct/Mistral-7B-Instruct on GSM8K, StrategyQA and ARC-Challenge; its highlighted improvements are predominantly versus Wanda. These do not establish broad enhancement over the dense baseline. The defensible positive deletion examples here are targeted loop repair and LASER’s older-model magnitude-pruning experiment. Generic layer removal is low priority for modern-instruct enhancement.

## Q1(c)/Q5: ordered, prospective contingency

All thresholds and caps below are **proposals**, not measured results. Adopt and timestamp them before generating the testing data. Keep construction, predictor-validation, search-development, and final-test groups disjoint by source closure; reserve new panels for each new hypothesis.

1. **Diagnose before editing (≤1 local GPU-h).** On fresh development prompts, freeze decoding and measure correctness, loop/truncation rate, instruction violations, factual errors and susceptibility to incorrect suggestions. Proposed loop-repair eligibility: lower 95% bound on loop/truncation prevalence >5%, with repeated-text diagnostics separating loops from legitimately long solutions. Otherwise skip loop repair. Do not toggle thinking after inspecting outcomes.
2. **Behavioral headroom first (≤4 local GPU-h).** Register two objectives in order: correctness under misleading factual cues, then TruthfulQA-style truthful-answer discrimination with generation confirmation. For each, freeze 32 candidates split equally between TFTV and CWS, signed/shrunk doses, plus base. Train contrasting rank-8 adapters on construction only; bound both runs together to 1 GPU-h/objective. Freeze support, norm caps and seed library before scoring. Pass when ≥2/32 have development gain LCB>0 and all inherited guardrails pass; a different likelihood scale needs an independently calibrated, frozen noninferiority margin. Select the first passing objective, never the largest observed effect. Treat this as screening; use simultaneous candidate intervals or a fresh replication panel to address selection.
3. **Capability alternatives second (≤4 local GPU-h).** If behavioral screening fails, register IFEval as primary and SciBench as secondary, applying a fixed 32-candidate library of construction-trained task deltas and gentle spectral edits. One objective family per stage; Holm-adjust secondary claims. Require the same headroom gate. Loop repair enters only if step 1 qualified. Keep LoRA alone, best-of-N direct editing, and same-input static ranking as comparators.
4. **RL last (≤8 local GPU-h feasibility cap).** Use construction-only verifiable math/code tasks and sparse adapter checkpoints. Stop if training/rollout throughput cannot fit the cap or no guarded headroom emerges. Never train on sealed test prompts. A cap is not a prediction of convergence.

For **trajectory failure**, first retain static cumulative prediction as the scientific result; wider doses/noncommuting recipes must be a new registered bank. Require ≥50% resolvable noncontrol steps and median static error >2 paired SE at h≥3 before investing in dynamics. For **slope failure**, omit slope transport; reintroduce only if a fresh preregistered oracle comparison cuts per-step error ≥20%, followed by a learned R0 transport test. An R2 oracle cannot serve as a free planner. Memory cannot manufacture beneficial edits; compare any SSM against residual and static models on identical folds before assigning it an enhancement explanation.

Final E1 still requires sealed paired gain LCB>0, generation confirmation and simultaneous guardrail noninferiority. Behavioral improvement must be called behavioral improvement. E2 separately requires world-model superiority over StatNN at equal charged budgets. Failure ends that claim, not another unrestricted search.

## Resource decision

The pilot’s 24.8 s/all-in visit implies **32×24.8/3600≈0.22 GPU-h** for its lean panel only; new generation/judging/training is additional. The reported 960-visit bank is 7.84 GPU-h, or 24.07 with per-state oracle gradients. Reprice changed objectives from measured throughput; stage caps above total at most 17 local GPU-h before any bank, and unused stages cost zero. Retain 28 GB device/24 GB host caps, one heavy job, and `agent-tmux` for long jobs.

The fetched [Qwen3.8-27B card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B) specifies a 27B language model, 64 layers and hybrid DeltaNet/attention. **Calculated:** bf16 language weights alone require roughly 54 GB decimal; 32 GB cannot hold them. An 80 GB-class Lambda device is justified for a separately frozen 27B replication after guarded local headroom and a credible predictor comparison, or a separately declared scale hypothesis—not because 9B failed. Reserve ≤2 GPU-h initially for memory/timing calibration, with a proposed ≤72 GB device peak. Stream edits and backups; two full bf16 copies exceed 80 GB. Full Adam training cannot fit from weights alone plus gradients/moments; adapter training also needs measured activation headroom. Freeze revision/template/precision, re-audit tensor roles, then price the bank from actual 27B timings. Do not extrapolate speed by parameter ratio or substitute quantization silently.
